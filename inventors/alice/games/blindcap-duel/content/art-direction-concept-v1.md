This is an AI-generated mood image, not CAD, a product photograph, a component-count reference, or manufacturing evidence. It gets the palette, low angle, cracked-loam massing, cap texture, owner colors and shadow mood approximately right. It gets important product facts wrong: the probes are staged vertically instead of entering beneath the caps, the visible component count is unreliable, the trays are incomplete, and the hidden reader geometry is invented.

Use it only to brief the actual CAD renderer. Do not send it to the Factory page builder and do not label it as a prototype.

- Growth-ring layer texture on caps.
- Low three-quarter view with a lifted mushroom in the foreground.
- Enough shadow under each brim to make the probe action feel secret.
