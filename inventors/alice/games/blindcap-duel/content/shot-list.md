# Blindcap: Duel — product-page shot list

Every image must make the physical oracle legible. The product is not “mushroom chess.” It is the moment one pin rests low and the other stops high.

## 1. Hero — the field knows

- Low three-quarter view across the joined 6 × 3 loam field.
- Twelve pale caps with one shared species-neutral silhouette; player-one and player-two bite marks remain visibly distinct, alongside six owner-marked probe pins and six crowns.
- Foreground pair: one pin at exactly 3.0 mm proud and one at the 27.628906 mm digital reference.
- One harvested mushroom sits cap-down in its original cell, shank and parallel tunnels visible, with its crown resting on the upward shank.
- Warm neutral light, cracked-loam texture, no text baked into the image.
- Crop-safe at 16:9 and 4:5.

## 2. The reveal — one physical bit

- Tight macro under a cap brim.
- Split composition or two adjacent sockets: admitted probe versus blocked probe.
- Dimension callouts can be added in layout: `3.0 mm` and `27.63 mm digital reference`.
- The probe owner mark must be visible.

## 3. What stays secret

- Four mushrooms standing above the loam share one species-neutral silhouette; use the same owner marks so species is the only changing variable.
- The same four laid on their sides below them show neither / one-dot / two-dot / both tunnel patterns.
- Label only the four species names. No decorative copy.

## 4. A complete turn

- Three-frame sequence: plant a hidden shank; probe a rival; crown a rival.
- The planted cap’s owner bites remain visible in every frame.
- Hands should demonstrate real access around the cap and pin head, not float above a render.

## 5. Two private trays

- Opposing seated-player angle.
- Near tray reveals its six shanks to camera; far tray wall hides them.
- Six shallow mushroom cradles and three crown pockets visible; the three marked probes and flat scoring aid sit beside the tray.
- Show the full tray envelope against a 160 × 160 mm bed outline in the technical variant.

## 6. Harvest and score

- End-state top view after all probes have been recorded and fully removed: each mushroom sits cap-down in its original cell, with its shank visible and any crown resting on that upward shank.
- Outline one orthogonal grove of three with a subtle overlay.
- Show one uncontested `3 × 3 = 9` example and one contested `3 + 3` example.

## 7. Printed set / proof-of-make

- Actual print only, never a render presented as a print.
- Include fit coupon, layer lines, support-free undersides and one assembled tile join.
- Photograph calipers on the 3 mm and approximately 28 mm probe states.
- Do not use this slot until a physical set exists.

## Video — 12 seconds, one mechanism

1. `0–2s`: species-neutral caps with visible owner bites; hand chooses one probe.
2. `2–5s`: probe slides in and stops proud.
3. `5–8s`: second probe slides almost flush.
4. `8–10s`: after every probe is removed, a mushroom lifts, inverts cap-down in its cell, and reveals its keyed tunnel pattern.
5. `10–12s`: full field and title, `Blindcap: Duel`.

No voiceover is needed. Use the real insertion sound once a physical prototype exists. Until then, label any animation `CAD preview`.
