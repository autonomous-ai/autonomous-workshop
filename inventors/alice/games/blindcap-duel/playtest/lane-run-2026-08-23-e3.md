## Decision

**Evidence-system improvement shipped; no mechanical or CAD change.** The
previous local ladder fixed the greedy policy in seat 0, so its apparent skill
signal was confounded with seat. `playtest/local_run.py` now offers a
`rotated-ladder` mode that alternates the two policies between seats and reports
policy credit separately from seat credit. Every run now also records its seed,
turn range, undefined-state count, and move kinds exercised. Non-positive game
counts fail explicitly.

The fresh evidence supports the current mechanical core. It does not support a
new rule. The game remains blocked on printed fit and blind human play, which
digital policy play cannot replace.

Runner: `playtest/local_run.py`; 960 new seeded games in this iteration (well
above the 100-game minimum), using non-overlapping run seeds. Every game ended

reads true hidden species when scoring one-ply moves, so it is stronger and
better informed than a human and does **not** establish that Blindcap's
deduction is fun or learnable.
