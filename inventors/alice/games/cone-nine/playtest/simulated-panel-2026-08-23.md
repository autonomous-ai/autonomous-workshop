# CONE NINE — simulated design panel, 2026-08-23

> **SIMULATED CRITIQUE ONLY.** These are Alice role-playing review lenses over
> rules and digital data. No human board-game expert, designer, printer, or
> player participated. This is not expert testimony or a human playtest.

## Abstract-strategy designer lens

The inherited goals were brittle: CORNERS required all four corners, CENTER
and BLOCK competed for almost the same high-connectivity cells, and nominal
point values did not create equal attainable value. Recommendation: reward
progress and equalize positional classes, not just printed maxima.

**Disposition:** accepted. The first per-cell attempt still showed HEART at
7.3125 mean bonus against a 4.3094–5.4208 field. The retained eight-map
symmetry family compresses the 6,720-game spread to 0.05625.

## Rules editor lens

“Shuffle the sixteen printed bands and load them onto the dial” requires
sixteen loose parts that the component list did not contain. The first-turn
instruction also disagreed between setup and turn text. Recommendation: one
fixed public wheel, spun to a random start, with the first turn obeying that
band and later turns advancing one click.

**Disposition:** accepted and bound identically in rules, idea, and engine.

## Publisher / product-search lens

The title **KILN** is already used by a live, pottery-themed nestorgames board
game. Mechanism difference does not rescue a same-category title collision.

**Disposition:** accepted. Renamed to **CONE NINE: the Firing Dial**. Exact-
title search is only a preliminary screen; commercial clearance remains open.

## Manufacturing lens

The component contract omitted the private objective tiles it required and
made an unverified claim that the whole set fit one 160 mm plate. Recommendation:
list glaze maps as physical parts and limit the current claim to each single
part's target envelope until packing and support checks exist.

**Disposition:** accepted in the design contract. No printability pass is
claimed; no CAD or coupon was produced in this iteration.

## Adversarial evaluator lens

A saved green report is worthless if a fresh engine run crashes. Recommendation:
discard the inherited receipt, bind engine to idea content hash, reject illegal
moves, rerun more than 100 games, and add controlled map-deal evidence.

**Disposition:** accepted. Fresh evidence is 1,600 authoritative games, 6,720
fixed-draw games, 600 setup seeds, and 1,600 multi-seed lookahead mirrors.

## Balance designer lens

The current game is close but not cleared. The authoritative strongest mirror
is 55.5/44.5, just outside the 45–55 band. A separate multi-seed point estimate
clears, but its interval still crosses the failure line. Recommendation: hold;
next test a symmetric opening state rather than fit a point credit to one seed.

**Disposition:** accepted. No publication and no “balanced” claim.
