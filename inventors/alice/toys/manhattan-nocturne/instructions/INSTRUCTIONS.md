# Manhattan Nocturne Chess

New York after midnight, translated into a complete standard chess set.

**By Alice.**

## What arrives

- One seamless 244 × 244 mm midnight-and-brass beveled street-grid board
- Sixteen warm Stone pieces
- Sixteen midnight Steel pieces
- This guide

The rules are ordinary chess. The architecture changes how the set looks and
feels, never how a piece moves.

## Read the skyline

| Chess role | Manhattan form |

| Pawn | Rooftop water tank |
| Rook | Heavy parapet tower |
| Knight | Forward bridge cantilever with an arched opening |
| Bishop | Stepped tower with one diagonal avenue cut |

Stone carries horizontal masonry terraces and one groove around every base.
Steel carries vertical structural ribs and two tactile channels between three
broad base rails. Those cues remain when color is hard to see.

## Set up

Turn the board so the raised square is at each player's right hand. Each raised
square is an isolated 25 mm landing with a broad sloped edge, so the checker
pattern remains visible even before an optional contrasting finish. From left
to right, each back rank is:

```text
rook  knight  bishop  queen  king  bishop  knight  rook
```

Place eight pawns on the row in front. The queen begins on her own square
family: Stone on raised, Steel on recessed. Stone moves first.

## Play standard chess

- The king moves one square in any direction.
- The queen moves any distance along a rank, file, or diagonal.
- The rook moves any distance along a rank or file.
- The bishop moves any distance diagonally.
- The knight moves in an L: two squares in one direction and one across. It may
  jump over pieces.
- A pawn moves forward one square, may move two from its starting square, and
  captures one square diagonally forward.

You may not make a move that leaves your king in check. Checkmate wins.
Stalemate is a draw. Castling, en passant, promotion, repetition, the fifty-move
rule, and insufficient-material draws work exactly as in standard chess.

## Handle the city

Center pieces on their square and lift them when moving instead of dragging
them across the relief. Keep the set away from sustained heat. The king cross,
queen fan, and bishop crown are intentionally robust, but they are still the
tallest details in the skyline.

## Midnight finish

The published design requires warm brass or muted gold on the 32 raised light
squares over a midnight base. The material boundary begins at Z 8.20 mm, where
the continuous base ends and only the isolated light pads continue. A maker may
use one height-based filament/material change or apply the top finish after
printing. This digital plan does not prove either process physically; verify the
chosen method in the target slicer and inspect the produced board before use.
