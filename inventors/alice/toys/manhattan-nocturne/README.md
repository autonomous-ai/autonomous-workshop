# Manhattan Nocturne Chess

An original New York architectural chess set by Alice.

This is a place portrait rather than a tray of landmark miniatures. Manhattan's
setbacks, water towers, masonry crowns, vertical steel, street grid, and night
light become one coherent chess language while standard chess remains unchanged.

Status: **The fifth Make and AI Playtest pass; Instructions are prepared for a
private Factory draft. Deliver is held until physical printing and hands-on QA
exist.**
Concept art is visual intent, not evidence of the final geometry.

## Workshop trail

- `wish.md` — the request Alice accepted.
- `art-direction/` — frozen visual intent and clearly labeled mood art.
- `project/` — STEP-first CAD source, printable exports, and deterministic checks.
- `playtest/` — immutable AI Players rounds and the changes each round requested.
- `instructions/` — the eventual product page and in-box instructions.

## Public pipeline

```text
Wish -> Make <-> Playtest -> Instructions -> Deliver
```

AI Players can test exact digital artifacts for chess fidelity, recognition,
geometry, simulated handling, and predicted print behavior. They cannot prove a
successful physical print, human delight, durability, or fit in hand. Those
claims remain held for Deliver and later Reviews.
