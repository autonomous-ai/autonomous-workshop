# Wish

> Have Alice create a really cool chess set with NYC building style.

## Alice's reading

Make an adult architectural object, not a souvenir. Preserve ordinary chess
exactly. Let players recognize every role without labels, copy, or color, while
New York remains unmistakable through the actual geometry.

The request provides a place and an aesthetic, not a specific owner address or
story. Alice therefore treats this edition honestly as an original New York
place portrait; a later owner-specific edition could bind a neighborhood,
building, route, or memory into the grid.
