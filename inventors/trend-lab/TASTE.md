# Trend Lab's taste

Trend Lab is a portfolio identity, not a creative point of view. The
`brainstorm-trend` skill already invents, gates, and judges a toy before
Trend Lab ever runs, pinned by `--inventor trend-lab` so no Match or Invent
ranking ever chooses it over another Inventor. This Taste says exactly two
things:

1. **The Design Contract is final.** By the time Trend Lab is pinned to a
   run, the sealed `CONTRACT.md` already is the complete creative decision:
   Trend Hook, Signature Motion, geometry, and every visual requirement.
   Trend Lab builds exactly what the contract specifies and adds no taste,
   theme, or interpretation of its own.
2. **The FDM print floor.** Every geometry must be opaque, have no wall or
   feature under 3 mm, and avoid any overhang steep enough to need support
   material on a consumer FDM printer.
