# Ferro Line fleet standard v1

Every Ferro Line release follows this standard so models from different runs
couple, line up, and share a shelf. State the family scale and the datum in
Invent; declare the standard in the manual.

## Scale by transport family

| Family | Scale | Rule of thumb |
|---|---|---|
| Rail, tram, road (locomotives, wagons, trams, lorries, buses) | 1:87 | a 20 m locomotive is about 230 mm; split body and tender |
| Aircraft | 1:72 | a 10 m wingspan is about 140 mm |
| Boats and small craft under 40 m (lifeboats, launches, barges, tugs) | 1:100 | a 9 m lifeboat is about 90 mm |
| Ships 40 m and over (steamers, liners, warships) | 1:350 | a 70 m steamer is about 200 mm; split hull at a frame |

A part longer than the host bed envelope is split at a prototype seam (cab
join, boiler ring, hull frame, wing root), never mid-panel.

## Standing plane

Models stand on a flat desk. Rail and road wheel treads contact the surface;
any flange is inboard of the tread and no deeper than 0.6 mm so the model
still rolls. Aircraft and ships stand on a base plate.

## Coupling

- Rail and road: hook-and-bar. Bar diameter 2.0 mm, horizontal, centred 7.0 mm
  above the standing plane and 3.0 mm behind the end face. Hook opening
  2.4 mm, printed integral to the part, at least 1.6 mm thick.
- Aircraft, ships, and boats: base plate footprint on a 40 mm by 20 mm peg
  grid; peg diameter 4.0 mm, height 3.0 mm; socket diameter 4.4 mm. A boat
  on its own carriage or trailer uses the rail and road hook-and-bar instead.

## Running gear

- Axle diameter 3.0 mm printed, captured in a 3.4 mm bore, or a wheel pressed
  on a 3.0 mm stub with 0.2 mm radial clearance.
- Wheel tread width at least 2.0 mm at 1:87.

## Printability floor

- Minimum wall 1.2 mm; minimum freestanding feature 0.8 mm (two nozzle
  widths at 0.4 mm); fillet or chamfer at least 0.4 mm on handled edges.
- No feature relies on supports inside a bore, a boiler, or under a footplate.
- Fittings smaller than 0.8 mm at scale are simplified with intent or omitted
  and listed in the manual.
