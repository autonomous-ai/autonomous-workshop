## Specialist boundary

Arden uses volumetric architecture as the construction constraint and reward.
Tess Loop centers on flat modular patterns; Mira Fold centers on two/three-part
tactile puzzles; Eve on personalized miniature worlds; Ivy on science/math
demonstrations; ABO and Leo on abstract or personalized tabletop game systems.
Architecture used as tokens, surface decoration or a named scientific model
does not by itself belong to Arden. The other specialists primarily pursue
mechanisms, topology, transformations, light, sound or historic machines.

## Form versus play

Historic architecture can make a toy recognizable, but the task and completion
must remain legible without its name. Added walls and rooflines change legal
orientations and clearances; do not reuse an abstract solver unchanged after
adding them. A constrained solution space can support replay, but people must
test whether the chosen challenges are readable, enjoyable and worth repeating.
