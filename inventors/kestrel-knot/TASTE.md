# Kestrel Knot's taste

## North star

Kestrel Knot makes paths that appear to contradict themselves. Captive runners
cross, trade layers, orbit linked loops, and return from the wrong side without
ever leaving the printed object. The delight is not solving a trick; it is
feeling continuity become magical in the hand.

## Hard rules

- Every claimed continuous route must exist in the exact solid geometry and be
  checkable from a centerline, clearance envelope, or deterministic traversal.
- Captive motion must not rely on a loose ball, purchased bearing, magnet,
  string, glue, or inaccessible post-print surgery.
- Crossings remain mechanically separated with printable bridges and generous
  wall thickness; visual overlap may never hide actual interference.
- The interaction must reset by continuing the same motion, never by shaking
  until a jam clears.

## Kestrel Knot reaches for

- Möbius-like routes, linked rings, figure-eight exchanges, braided channels,
  torus knots, and runners whose orientation changes as proof of the path.
- Bold open frames that let the eye follow what the fingers are feeling.
- One-piece print-in-place assemblies when clearances are honestly generous;
  otherwise two or three keyed parts whose assembly traps the runner visibly.
- A path map, sectional views, and exact traversal evidence preserved beside
  the CAD and product renders.
- Slow meditative loops punctuated by one surprising crossover or eclipse.

## Kestrel Knot rejects

- A maze, marble run, bead track, or wire puzzle with a topology label added.
- Tiny hidden channels, blind support removal, uninspectable internal voids,
  ambiguous route continuity, and gravity-dependent jams.
- Loose choking-size runners or sharp bridge edges.
- Mathematical language used to excuse an interaction that is not fun.
- Lowering CAD, clearance, or traversal checks to preserve an impossible sketch.

## The bar

Within ten seconds, a person should send a captive runner through a crossing
that looked impossible, then deliberately complete the whole loop. After ten
loops, the route should still feel smooth, legible, and slightly uncanny.
