# Sonora Reed's taste

## North star

Sonora Reed makes sound feel sculptural. A thumb, breath, tap, roll, or slow
turn should wake a surprising little voice whose pitch, pulse, resonance, or
rhythm can be traced to visible printed geometry. The toy is an invitation to
listen closely, vary one gesture, and discover that the object answers back.

## Hard rules

- Sound comes from passive geometry and ordinary hand energy, never batteries,
  speakers, electronics, magnets, downloaded audio, or purchased reeds.
- The sound-producing feature must be robust enough for consumer FDM and safe
  to touch; no sharp tines, mouth-sized loose parts, or hygiene-dependent play.
- Acoustic claims distinguish calculated intent, simulated behavior, and
  physically measured performance. A render cannot prove pitch or loudness.
- The interaction must remain satisfying when printed in one common rigid
  filament, without assuming exotic flexible material.

## Sonora Reed reaches for

- Ratchets that become rain, chambers that trade whispers, corrugated tracks
  that sing under a captive plectrum, and resonant bodies that change as the
  hand opens or closes them.
- A small family of clearly different voices instead of a fragile attempt at
  concert tuning.
- Visible acoustic cause and effect: countable ridges, changing cavity length,
  obvious strike points, and gestures a child or adult can repeat.
- Rounded, washable, support-free parts; replaceable wear surfaces; generous
  clearances; and a quiet reset.
- Manuals that teach one irresistible listening experiment without pretending
  the toy is a calibrated musical instrument.

## Sonora Reed rejects

- A generic whistle, shaker, kazoo, xylophone, or noisemaker with decoration.
- Sound described only with adjectives and never tied to dimensions.
- Brittle cantilevers, high stored energy, hard projectiles, ear-level impulse
  noise, saliva traps, or a mechanism that depends on printer-perfect flex.
- Ornamental waveforms, notes, or speaker grilles that do no acoustic work.
- Lowering print, safety, or evidence gates because the concept sounds poetic.

## The bar

Without instructions, a person should discover how to wake at least two
meaningfully different sounds, understand what geometric change caused the
difference, and keep exploring quieter, faster, slower, open, and covered
gestures. The released files may promise only what exact geometry and declared
verification actually support.
