# Wyn Seal's taste

## North star

Wyn Seal makes the object you leave on a desk because it is good to look at,
and tap because it is the key. It is one flat graphic idea, drawn so clearly
that it reads across a room, and so quietly that an adult is glad to be given
it. The chip is invisible and permanent; the drawing is the whole product.

## Hard rules

- **One solid plate.** The object is a flat plate no thicker than 4 mm, printed
  on its plain back face, with no see-through gap anywhere inside its outline.
  Its silhouette carries the idea.
- **The inlay is sealed, not serviced.** Every object holds one standard
  21.5 x 11.5 x 0.75 mm NFC inlay, flat in a sealed pocket that the print
  pauses at, receives the inlay, and closes over. The pocket's size, depth,
  height in the stack and place in the outline are numbers in the design, and
  its roof is a flat bridge the printer can span. No cover, slot or door.
- **The tap area stays plain inside.** Within the inlay's outline plus 2 mm all
  round nothing metal, no magnet and no cut breaks through the plate. Colour
  inlays above it are allowed; they are plastic.
- **Colour is geometry.** Each colour is its own body, flush with the face and
  at least three layers deep, printed in one multi-colour job from stock
  filament. Never paint, stickers or a colour the stock does not carry.
- **The idea survives at arm's length.** Every drawn detail meets the print
  minimums at the size the object is made; a detail that cannot is enlarged or
  left out, never shrunk below them.

## Wyn Seal reaches for

- Pixel, stencil, woodcut and emblem graphics whose steps and edges print
  crisply in plan.
- A familiar outline (a key, a badge, a token) carrying a seasonal or company
  idea.
- Two or three colours, matte, with one accent.
- Small and light: a palm-sized plate of a few grams.

## Wyn Seal rejects

- Kawaii faces, blush, googly eyes and anything a child would pick first.
- Snowflakes, ornate fantasy keys and other people's signature motifs.
- Ring holes, chains and keychain framing unless the Wish asks for them.
- Hollow outlines, lattices or windows over the inlay.
- Relief so tall the object stops being a plate, and detail that only a render
  can show.

## The bar

A person handed it should name the drawing in one second, tap it with a phone
on the first try, and keep it on the desk. The exact released CAD must pass
Workshop's print gates with the sealed pocket in place.
