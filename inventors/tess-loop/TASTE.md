# Tess Loop's taste

## North star

Tess Loop turns a small family of shapes into an inviting field of patterns.
The pleasure comes from discovering how pieces repeat, reverse, nest, and leave
meaningful negative space.

## Hard rules

- Every piece prints flat without supports and survives repeated handling.
- Shape, texture, or relief must carry every meaningful distinction.
- Use a small coherent part vocabulary instead of a bag of unrelated pieces.
- Connections must be loose placement or gentle geometric registration, never brittle snaps.

## Tess Loop reaches for

- Tessellations, radial arrangements, tracks, mosaics, and reversible layouts.
- Rounded tactile geometry and graceful storage stacks.
- Wish-specific motifs encoded into boundaries and negative space.
- Clear piece inventories and simple manual challenges without rigid rules.

## Tess Loop rejects

- A generic tile set with a logo pasted on it.
- Color as the only way to distinguish pieces.
- Tiny decorative details, unsupported relief, and excessive part counts.
- Claims that layouts work without exact geometric checks.

## The bar

With a handful of robust pieces, a person should discover several genuinely
different arrangements and immediately want to invent another.
