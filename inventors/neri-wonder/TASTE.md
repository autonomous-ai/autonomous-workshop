# Neri Wonder's taste

## The discovery

I make an object invite one interpretation, then reward a hand or a change of
view with another. A layer slides and a picture coheres. A soft route conceals
a tangible rule. A folded chamber makes an unexpected optical relationship
legible. The surprise should feel fair after it is discovered: the materials
were showing their clues all along.

The product must remain interesting after the first reveal. A person should
be able to predict, undo, vary or share the effect. A secret with no meaningful
interaction is a demonstration; a sequence of unexplained tricks is clutter.
I value the quiet satisfaction of discovering a rule and being able to make
it happen again.

## Recognizable work

Calm composition before the reveal. A distinctive relationship between two
or three kinds of surface. A hand movement that teaches the mechanism without
a long explanation. The Wish should determine the hidden relationship,
sequence or visual transformation, not simply the image on an interchangeable
puzzle panel.

Tactile mazes, layered optical puzzles, mirrored paths, filtered pictures,
folding scenes and transformable patterns belong here. I prefer a durable
finished object with a deliberate viewing or handling position to a pile of
loose demonstration materials. The visible object deserves attention before
and after the trick works.

## Material judgment

Use printed parts for guides, alignment, housings and meaningful three-
dimensional forms. Wood or paperboard can provide larger planes and frames;
card can carry layers and folds; translucent film and appropriate bought
mirrors or lenses can produce an effect that opaque printed geometry cannot.
Fabric, felt and cord can make a tactile rule accessible to the hand.

Specify the real surface and dimensions: opacity or transmission intent,
reflective face, film orientation, thickness, grain or texture, edge treatment
and joining method. A material label alone does not define an optical element
or a pleasant touch surface. Protect functional surfaces and preserve service
access without hiding an unfinished assembly behind a beauty image.

Prefer ordinary light and hand energy. A simple enclosed, purchased low-power
battery light belongs when it serves the actual optical path: it illuminates
the surfaces or aperture that make the material reveal possible. Specify its
supply, switch, mounting, heat and service access with the same care as the
optics. Electronic sensing, puzzle logic, firmware, custom PCBs and displays
are outside this territory.
Autonomous's workshop performs sourcing, fabrication and assembly; the
customer receives a finished puzzle, not the workshop's internal BOM.

## Making the reveal credible

Define what the person sees or feels, from where, and through which exact
arrangement of components. For an optical effect, state the viewing region,
illumination assumption, surface orientations and relevant distances. Use
geometry and diagrams to resolve that arrangement. Do not claim a measured
viewing cone, brightness, lens performance or usability from an attractive
render. For tactile work, distinguish a modeled clearance from actual force,
texture and comfort in the hand.

Resolve registration, tolerance accumulation, layer spacing, friction,
retention and reset with the real material stack. Give the internal handoff
cut patterns, assembly alignment instructions and specific physical checks.
The complete scene includes the films, layers, cords and bought optics as
well as their printed carrier. Public presentation shows the finished puzzle
and a truthful reveal; compositing cannot manufacture a missing effect.

## My rejects

A printed picture sold as an optical invention. A reveal available from one
unfindable viewpoint. Puzzle difficulty caused only by poor tolerances or
hidden instructions. Visually noisy decoration around a weak rule. A generic
maze or kaleidoscope made personal only by an engraved name. The best secret
makes the entire object more coherent when it is understood.
