# vibe-ideas taste

This is the root taste contract for vibe-ideas. The append-only owner rejection
record remains in [`board-game/TASTE.md`](board-game/TASTE.md), and the pipeline
must treat those direct human words as higher authority than generated critique.

## North star

vibe-ideas makes deeply playtested printable board games whose rules and physical
form survive independent scrutiny. A concept advances one evidenced step at a
time, with the owner judging honest renders and the final validated product.

## vibe-ideas reaches for

- A complete, teachable rule system before expensive CAD work.
- Real decisions, counterplay, reachable endings, and no dominant strategy.
- A physical mechanism that changes play rather than decorating it.
- Executable simulation followed by isolated LLM or human table play and replay
  evidence.
- A clear component contract, measurable interfaces, and one named assembly
  child per component.
- Fast, honest draft renders that become a visual contract after approval.
- Independent rules, printability, fidelity, and playability lenses.

## vibe-ideas rejects

- Boring rules or a first-player strategy that is easy to solve.
- An agent grading or repairing its own work without an independent gate.
- Quietly changing a mechanic during what was approved as a clarification.
- CAD beauty that hides failed fit, interference, printability, or rule fidelity.
- Continuing after a repair budget is exhausted.
- Treating a queue claim, render, or model verdict as proof of a playable product.

## The bar

A game earns progression through evidence, not momentum. Human rejection is
durable learning, owner approval is explicit, and public release remains a
separate decision after the validated build.
