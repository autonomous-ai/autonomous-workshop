# text2game taste

This is the root taste contract for text2game. The detailed mechanism vocabulary,
dials, and accumulated rules remain in
[`taste_boardgame.md`](taste_boardgame.md); DISCOVER and BRIEF use that document
directly, while BUILD and REPAIR use engineering lessons instead.

## North star

text2game makes an adult board game whose printed object is the reason it exists.
The product should explain itself quickly, do something satisfying on camera,
and contain purposeful physical complexity without becoming rules-heavy.

## text2game reaches for

- One signature component that the turn loop actually uses.
- A visible fifteen-second action: cascade, flip, fold, launch, reveal, sort, or
  another motion that needs no caption.
- Hidden information, randomness, state change, and scoring embodied in printed
  geometry rather than cards or an app.
- Familiar rules only when the physical object creates a genuinely new play
  pattern.
- Few distinct designs, each earning its place as a rule or interaction.
- Self-containing storage, engraved guidance, fast teaching, and honest bed fit.
- Physics-first tradeoffs where mass, friction, balance, or geometry creates the
  decision instead of a compensating price table.

## text2game rejects

- Roll-and-move, trivia, memory pairs, generic deck builders, reskinned classics,
  and theme-swappable loops.
- Printed parts that are merely deluxe replacements for cardboard.
- Decorative complexity, exposed-infill aesthetics, generic organizers, and
  easy-to-model slop.
- Rules added to compensate for a weak or untested physical mechanism.
- A still-life product with no memorable action.
- Unspecced purchased components; only explicitly allowed load-bearing commodity
  parts belong in a design.

## The bar

The object must be novel in the market, printable on the declared process, and
worth holding. Every important physical claim remains unproven until measured.
