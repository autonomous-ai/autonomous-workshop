## Why

The two recent Forge runs for the same Ho Chi Minh City landmark chess-set Wish created a rushed generic early proof and then treated that proof geometry as mandatory final-source input. The result preserved simple shapes that did not resemble the requested buildings. That proof phase was introduced to create durable CAD before short native turns expired, but current deep runs already freeze the later 256,000-token compaction ceiling. The obsolete liveness workaround is now constraining design quality.

Main has no separate Concept stage in the active lifecycle. Forge and Quest pass a sealed `NativeInvented` JSON result directly from Invent to Make, so current Make should use that structured design authority and begin coherent final-product work immediately.

## What Changes

- Freeze a new immutable deep-v14 capability for new Forge and Quest runs.
- Preserve v13 Invent economics, 256k compaction, later-stage settings, recovery, one Wish-wide session, and all final gates.
- Start current Make at high reasoning with its ordinary 60-minute boundary and normal `agent-outcome.json` finalizer.
- Remove the current route's 16-minute medium early-proof turn, `review/early-proof/` deliverable, `.make-proof-ready.json`, private proof receipt, 15-minute source handoff, and proof-geometry reuse instruction.
- Keep all proof machinery reachable for frozen v13 and older runs.
- Update authenticated Forge/Quest acceptance coverage and rerun the exact prior Wish for a three-way evidence-backed quality comparison.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `workshop/direct-deep-make`: versioned direct Invent-to-Make behavior for new Forge and Quest runs, with unchanged authoritative final gates and frozen historical compatibility.

### Modified Capabilities
