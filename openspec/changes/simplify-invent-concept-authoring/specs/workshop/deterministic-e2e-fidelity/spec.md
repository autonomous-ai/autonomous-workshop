## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Deterministic fidelity covers simplified authoring and normalization

The required offline route matrix SHALL include new marked Forge and Quest fixtures whose executable native double authors one consolidated Invent source and one adaptive visual plan, invokes the materialized finalizer once, and leaves every normalized contract, image effect, gate, checkpoint, and Make handoff to production code.

Coverage SHALL prove no standalone Concept lifecycle event exists and SHALL include minimal one-piece roles, multipart adaptive roles, accepted 20-role plans, rejected 21-role plans before effects, missing required roles, invalid dependencies, ambiguous effects, revised role sets, normalized component mismatch, copied pixels, and frozen v1 compatibility.

#### Scenario: Simplified marked route succeeds
- **WHEN** a valid v2 Forge or Quest fixture completes
- **THEN** its trace remains Invent to Make, every adaptive role is host-rendered after native exit, and Make receives the exact normalized sealed Concept

#### Scenario: Frozen v1 fixture resumes
- **WHEN** the same installed host processes a historical v1 checkpoint
- **THEN** the fixture retains its six authored files, fixed role set, identities, and downstream behavior

### Requirement: Deterministic doubles do not hide authoring complexity

The native double MAY provide fixed creative content but MUST write only the two agent-owned v2 inputs and invoke the public finalizer. It MUST NOT write normalized source projections, descriptor paths, derived Wish records, hashes, sealed images, receipts, gates, or Make bindings directly.

#### Scenario: Fixture writes a derived artifact
- **WHEN** the executable native double creates a host/finalizer-owned normalized or sealed artifact
- **THEN** the fidelity ownership guard fails even if the route otherwise completes
