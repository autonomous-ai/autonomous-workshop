## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Simplified Invent researches only concrete factual uncertainty

For a run with the simplified authoring capability, external research SHALL be required only when a factual uncertainty can materially change a physical constraint, compatibility decision, safety boundary, mechanism, component, or other build-critical commitment. A wholly original design whose remaining choices are deliberate inventions MAY carry an empty external-source list and MUST NOT fabricate a source merely to satisfy a nonempty schema.

#### Scenario: External fact controls the design
- **WHEN** a fit, standard, material limit, known object dimension, or safety fact affects the physical brief
- **THEN** the consolidated source records bounded support before that fact becomes binding

#### Scenario: Remaining choices are original design decisions
- **WHEN** no external fact is needed to decide the physical concept
- **THEN** the source records deliberate decisions and may finalize without browsing or a synthetic citation

### Requirement: Provenance is proportional and canonical identities are derived

The consolidated source SHALL explicitly distinguish externally grounded build-critical constraints from deliberate design decisions. Source provenance SHALL include the origin and bounded supporting content needed to audit each externally grounded constraint. The finalizer SHALL derive canonical excerpt hashes, source identities, and normalized research records from those exact bytes.

The capability SHALL NOT require a separate attribution ledger for the object name, category, original feature names, or every creative component merely because those values exist. It SHALL reject an externally grounded constraint without support and a deliberate numerical constraint without a reason.

#### Scenario: Build-critical sourced constraint is recorded
- **WHEN** the product must obey a sourced external dimension
- **THEN** the normalized Concept binds that constraint to its exact bounded source content and derived identity

#### Scenario: Creative component is invented
- **WHEN** the selected Inventor creates an original component and explains its purpose and interfaces
- **THEN** no fabricated external source or duplicate per-field attribution entry is required

### Requirement: Research failure remains an Invent need

If a necessary factual uncertainty cannot be resolved safely, the native session SHALL return a concrete Invent need rather than guessing, substituting a placeholder, or allowing normalization or image effects to proceed.

#### Scenario: Required source is unavailable
- **WHEN** a build-critical compatibility fact cannot be established from available trustworthy evidence
- **THEN** Invent waits or fails with that missing condition and Make remains unreachable
