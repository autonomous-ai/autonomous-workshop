## Why

The active compound Invent/Concept contract asks one bounded native Invent turn to author a four-field creative source and then restate the same design and research across five additional strict files, including mechanical hashes, copied Wish fields, canonical paths, mandatory research, and a fixed `N + 4` image set. This makes structural serialization compete with invention and finalization even though Make consumes a normalized sealed Concept rather than those raw authoring choices.

## What Changes

- Add a new frozen Invent/Concept authoring capability for newly created marked Forge and Quest runs. One Invent Goal authors exactly two creative inputs: the existing consolidated `invent-source.json` and one adaptive `visual-plan.json`.
- Make the consolidated Invent source the single authority for the physical brief, signature interaction, anti-generic signature, stable components, interfaces, build-critical constraints, decisive Make proof target, research findings when needed, deliberate decisions, assumptions, and unresolved risks.
- Replace mandatory per-field attribution and always-nonempty research with targeted provenance on externally grounded or build-critical constraints. The finalizer derives excerpt hashes and other canonical identities; it never supplies missing research or design judgment.
- Replace fixed `front`, `top`, `bottom`, `exploded`, and per-component rendering with adaptive roles capped at 20 images per Concept. Every visual plan requires a primary-form role and a signature-experience role; exploded, state, alternate-view, and isolated-component roles are added only when they communicate a named construction or interaction need.
- Deterministically normalize the two authored inputs into the durable pre-render and sealed Concept contracts before any host effect. Canonical paths, descriptor leaves, routed-Wish preservation, manifests, hashes, and compatibility projections are host/finalizer derivations, not agent-authored design work.
- Keep Make on the normalized sealed boundary. Make continues to receive the exact sealed Concept, effect evidence, and `required_product_component_keys`; bind `NativeMade` to the Concept identities; use normalized numerical constraints as authority; treat adaptive images as non-evidentiary design direction; require exact component correspondence; reject copied Concept pixels; and preserve the existing Make-to-Invent revision edge.
- Version the capability and all materialized instructions. Frozen `invent-concept-v1` runs retain their exact six-authored-file protocol and current Make reader; Spark and every unmarked historical run remain unchanged.
- Add deterministic and authenticated real-Codex acceptance evidence for first-pass authoring, bounded recovery, adaptive role execution, same-session Make handoff, and v1 compatibility before enabling the new marker.

## Capabilities

### New Capabilities

- `workshop/invent-concept-authoring`: Defines the versioned two-input native authoring contract, deterministic normalization boundary, adaptive visual-plan shape, and frozen v1 compatibility.

### Modified Capabilities

- `workshop/concept-stage`: Replaces the five-file agent-authored sub-boundary for new marked runs with two authored inputs normalized inside the existing Invent boundary without adding a stage, Goal, turn, checkpoint, or transition.
- `workshop/wish-research`: Makes research proportional to concrete factual uncertainty, limits explicit attribution to externally grounded or build-critical constraints, and moves canonical hashing out of cognitive authorship.
- `workshop/concept-images`: Replaces the fixed overall/component image set with adaptive primary-form, signature-experience, and need-driven roles while preserving same-design consistency and exact sealing.
- `workshop/concept-image-integration`: Executes the validated adaptive dependency graph and canonical derived paths without composing or repairing agent-owned instructions.
- `workshop/make-concept-adherence`: Keeps Make bound to a normalized sealed Concept while removing dependence on raw v2 authoring files or an agent-authored derived Wish and teaching Make how to consume adaptive visual roles.
- `workshop/deterministic-e2e-fidelity`: Adds marked v2 authoring, normalization, adaptive-role, compatibility, rejection, wait, revision, and downstream Make coverage.
- `workshop/effort-aware-codex-mock-session-e2e`: Requires real Codex to complete the simplified Invent source and visual plan, finalize within the bounded source-handoff behavior, and resume the same session at unchanged Make proof and release gates.

## Impact

- Affects the frozen product-run Invent/Concept capability, `STAGE.json` packet fields, materialized finalizer, Concept v2/v3 source and sealed contracts, host Concept effect planning, Make packet wording and normalized readers, revision/archive handling, package assets, deterministic fixtures, and real-Codex acceptance.
- Preserves the executable Spark/Forge/Quest topology, one-session/one-Goal architecture, host-only credentials and effects, current CAD/Playtest/Release gates, `NativeMade` Concept bindings, component equality, pixel exclusion, and publication behavior.
- Requires additive readers and marker selection so existing materialized runs remain byte-compatible and resumable. No provider, credential, CAD format, Factory API, or public product claim is broadened.
