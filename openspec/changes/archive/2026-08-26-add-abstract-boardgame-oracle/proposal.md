## Why

Workshop's `invented-games` lane has the strictest evidence bar in the repository — `_playtest_policy_needs` (`src/inventor_workshop/workshop.py:168`) refuses Instructions unless a run returns `game-simulation` evidence with `executable=true`, `completed_games >= 1000`, and all four of `optimizing`/`social`/`exploratory`/`adversarial` player styles — and nothing in this repository can produce it. Leo, the bundled `invented-games` inventor, is two `raise WaitingFor` statements (`inventors/leo/profile.py:26`, `:41`); `docs/ADOPTION.md` lists implementing them as adoption slice 3 and has since 0.5.

`reinSPQR/vibe-ideas` is already recorded in `upstreams.json` as an `inventor-snapshot`, and its `board-game/` tree is a working, evidenced version of precisely that missing half: an ideator that invents complete original games, a deterministic rules-versus-bill checker (`rules_check.py`), an agent that compiles rules into an executable engine that must refuse rather than guess where the rules run out (`board-game-rules-engineer`), a seeded simulation harness that plays that engine thousands of times and measures termination, seat balance, forced fractions, branching and a skill ladder (`playtest.py`, 1,556 lines), a model-per-seat table harness with no agent in the loop (`table_run.py`, 1,228 lines), and a deterministic STEP-first manufacturing gate (`gate.py`) that writes source-closure-hashed evidence. It has carried a real game — `overcap-v3`/Hubstone — from proposal through rules, engine, 400 seeded games, table play, CAD draft and preview.

It cannot be adopted as-is. It is queue-driven rather than Wish-driven, it has two human owner gates and a Telegram approval channel, its 400-game default is well short of the 1,000-game floor, its scripted policies are `random`/`greedy`/`first`/`lookahead` rather than the four styles Workshop names, and its taste is not Leo's. That taste is the reason this is a new inventor rather than a transplant into Leo: `board-game/DESIGN_FOCUS.md` steers hard toward abstract strategy — minimal theme, one or two piece types, Go/Hex/Chess depth from combinatorial structure, and it explicitly killed a design (Loomery) for component count *after* it cleared every mechanical gate. Leo's `TASTE.md` requires the opposite, that a Wish's "people, obsessions, memories, places, constraints, or private jokes should become mechanism". Both are legitimate `invented-games` tastes, which is exactly the case the open catalog and the semantic Manager exist to serve.

## What Changes

- Add **Abstract Boardgame Oracle** (`abo`), a `custom-playtest` inventor in the `invented-games` lane, beside Leo. Its `TASTE.md` is a new creative constitution derived from `board-game/DESIGN_FOCUS.md` and the owner rejections in `board-game/TASTE.md`: abstract structure over theme, few piece types, depth from combinatorial complexity rather than added rules, shape-only distinction because the pipeline assigns no colour, and the skill ladder — not a model's opinion — as the test of "hard to master". Leo is untouched; his seams keep waiting.
- **Invent the game at Concept, not at Make.** For an abstract game the pieces *are* the rules, so a brief that locks a component breakdown before the rules exist locks the wrong bill. ABO supplies a lane-specific `WishResearcher` that invents the game — concept, complete rules with per-step `uses` declarations, the component bill, and art direction in pure form language — runs the deterministic rules-versus-bill consistency check over it, and returns that as the researched breakdown the `ConceptBrief` is derived from. The rules become sealed concept facts under `concept_sha256`, and the brief's components are the game's actual pieces, so `_assert_product_follows_concept` becomes a real check instead of a formality.
- Add ABO's `MakeContext -> Made`. It compiles the sealed rules into an executable engine written **into the product artifact** so Playtest binds to its exact bytes, and builds STEP-first build123d CAD to the brief's millimetres using the repository's locked `skills/cad`. The engine is a pure translation of the locked rules: where the rules do not say what happens it either refuses or registers the reading as a declared, testable assumption. It never invents a rule to make a game runnable, and it never repairs a broken game.
- Add ABO's `PlaytestContext -> Playtested`, returning all four results the lane requires — `agent-playtest`, `game-simulation`, `mechanical-test`, `print-test` — each `evidence_class=ai-simulation` and bound to the exact artifact hash.
- **Close the 1,000-game gap honestly.** The ported harness defaults to 400 seeded games plus a 120-game ladder and took 728 seconds on the reference game. ABO's `game-simulation` result SHALL NOT pass on fewer than 1,000 *completed* games, and SHALL report a truthful `Need` rather than a pass when its deadline is reached first.
- **Name the four player styles for real.** `optimizing`, `exploratory` and `adversarial` map onto the existing lookahead/Monte-Carlo, randomized and seat-edge-seeking policies; `social` has no scripted equivalent and is supplied by the model-per-seat harness, which is also what satisfies `agent-playtest`'s requirement of two or more distinct roles. A style with no executing policy behind it is not declared.
- Map the ported gates onto Workshop `Feedback` rather than onto a queue. The vibe-ideas repair/rework/clarify budgets and their `clarify` versus `rework` disposition become `Feedback` severity and area: a rules ambiguity is `improve` against the rules area, a broken mechanic or a failed manufacturing check is `block`. Workshop's bounded `playtest_rounds` loop replaces all three budgets.
- **Not ported:** `QUEUE.json` and its claims and leases, `pipeline_queue.py`, the two human owner gates, `telegram.py`, `dashboard.py`, `game_site.py`, `publish.py`, `journal.py` and `improve.py`. Workshop is one-Wish and request-driven, its Instructions job owns the box paper and the Factory handoff, and its Deliver job owns production and carrier evidence. Owner approval of a published page already lives outside the six jobs.
- Record the import as reviewed provenance: an `UPSTREAM.md` in the inventor folder naming `reinSPQR/vibe-ideas` at the commit already pinned in `upstreams.json`, plus a `snapshots.lock.json` entry so `tools/verify_snapshot_locks.py` proves the imported tree byte-for-byte and the vendored files cannot be edited silently.
