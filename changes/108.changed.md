Mesh validity, interference and insertion order are caught before assembly.
Every `make_round` round now runs `check_mesh` beside the thickness and
overhang gates, and a non-manifold Component fails its round with each edge
named (`check_mesh` prints `[edge ]` rows). `make_round --interface` first
checks that the Coupled Interface's Components do not interfere where they
are placed, then runs its pose table together with every insertion path in
`measure/motion.json` that moves only those Components; a clash, a collision
or a blocked path unlocks the yielding Component with its evidence. In Broken
God attempt 18 each of these first failed at an assembly round or final
verification. See ADR 0088.
