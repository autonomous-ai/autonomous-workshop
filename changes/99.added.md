- `design-a-toy` Stage 3d checks every travel stop the contract names
  (issue #99): at each extreme it ends, the host Component must hold
  material outside every moving part's swept space plus the clearance, in a
  stopping part's way within 4 degrees past the extreme, and printable in the
  host's stance (grown from the bed or from material the contract already
  prints, within the overhang limit, at least 0.8 thick). When none exists
  the contract names another stop (another Component, the gear train or a
  detent) before it is sealed. `scripts/travel_stop.py` computes it from
  explicit slices for parts turning about one axis, reusing the swept-area
  helpers of `scripts/swept_ceiling.py`. The worked example is Broken God's
  open extreme at attempt 17: neither the sector nor the boss layer leaves a
  printable site, so amend-l named the detent. Nothing in the run pipeline
  changes.
