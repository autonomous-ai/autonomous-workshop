- `design-a-toy` takes its print minimums from the print-details library's
  `limits()` at the contract's nozzle (a rivet, boss or dome 2.0 mm across at
  0.4 mm, not 1.0 mm), checks every detail drawn on another detail or on a
  curved host, and replaces "never left out" with "enlarged, or left out and
  named": a detail that does not fit its spot at the minimum is left out and
  named in its Component's requirement row, with the Broken God helm as the
  worked example. `CONTRACT-FORMAT.md` states the same rules. Nothing in the
  run pipeline changes.
