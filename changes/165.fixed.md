- `gen --write` no longer judges a part's old STEP export current after a
  Colour Module edit, and no longer reports a current export without its
  B-rep identity. Each export is recorded with its bytes' sha256, the
  entry's source closure, the toolchain and the identity of the shape it
  holds; it is current only while all of them match, and then reports that
  identity. A recolour now re-exports in the new colour at the locked
  identity and carries the review, and a `gen --force` or
  `verify_project --fresh` re-export no longer breaks a recorded pass,
  charges a Shape Round or stales Interface checks (ADR 0073, issues #165
  and #166). The first `--write` after upgrading rebuilds each export once.
