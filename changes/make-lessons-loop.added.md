- Make outcomes now teach the design vault the way Playtest rounds do
  (`workshop.make.vault_lessons`): a failed CAD gate, a refused proposal, a
  Make-to-Invent revision request, a parked need, or a token-budget stop
  becomes a classified evidence row on an anti-pattern node, and every Make
  posts the product's `games/<wish-id>` page with a `make-*` verdict. Every
  Invent and Make `STAGE.json` carries `make_lessons`, the ten newest rows
  for the concept's mechanisms and the Make failure classes, with fixes. New
  vault anti-patterns: `likeness-wall`, `unbounded-repair-loop`.
  See ADR 0055.
