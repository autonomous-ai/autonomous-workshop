  inspired by one current Trend. Five designers, drawn by seed from a pool
  of thirty personalities, draft through `design-a-toy` Stages 1-2. A gate
  requires a Trend Hook, a Signature Motion and a coupled Interface. Fresh
  blind judges then compare every pair in both orders on originality,
  beauty and motion, and the winner is finished and handed to `build-a-toy`
  under the new `trend-lab` Inventor. It replaces the unfinished
  `brainstorm-reskin` skill and its `reskin-lab` Inventor.
