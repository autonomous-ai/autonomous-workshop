- `build-a-toy` has an unattended mode: one supervising session launches each
  attempt, watches it, diagnoses every stop from evidence (Contract
  Contradiction, reference mismatch, harness defect, budget stop while
  progressing, or other; camera needs keep their path), fixes a Contract
  Contradiction at the root by amending the contract and re-auditing the
  whole contract, fixes and merges harness defects it opened itself, and
  launches the next attempt. Only a Visible Change waits for the owner, and
  the loop stops and asks when a fixed contradiction comes back, three
  attempts make no progress, the toy passes 100 cost units, a harness fix
  fails or conflicts, or a Visible Change awaits approval. The toy's ledger
  gains the loop's fields (`LEDGER.md`), and `scripts/ledger.py` validates it,
  classifies a stop and prints the next action, so a new session resumes from
  the ledger alone. `watch.sh`, `tally.py` and `tokens.py` move under the
  skill with no machine-specific paths. `design-a-toy` Stage 3c checks every
  ceiling over a moving part's sweep (`scripts/swept_ceiling.py`), with Broken
  God's spine housing as the worked example. `CONTEXT.md` defines Visible
  Change. Nothing in the run pipeline changes.
