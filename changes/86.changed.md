- Make reports every Detail Refusal of a Component build at once. Inside a
  build the print-details library records a refused feature, leaves it out
  and goes on; the build then fails once with every refusal, each naming its
  feature, call site, reason and the value that would pass at that spot (the
  limit, the largest `d` or `width` the surface takes, the least height that
  clears it, or the spacing between copies). `make_round` prints each on a
  `refuse` line and flags a detail refused at the same line again under
  `repeated_detail_refusals`, counted apart from repeated print defects. The
  Component Worker leaves out a detail refused at the same spot in two rounds
  and names it; the Component Reviewer's limits add placement rules (a host
  0.5 mm wider than the detail each side, no raised detail on a concave
  surface deeper than its height, the min cut width between copies).
  `CONTEXT.md` defines Detail Refusal.
