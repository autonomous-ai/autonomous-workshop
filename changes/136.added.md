- Add Make's Assembly Tree gates for schema 7 kits (ADR 0092 decision 8),
  in `make-round/scripts/kit_tree.py`. The Workshop Manager installs the
  sealed tree with `kit_tree.py --install` (`tree/kit.json`). Each printed
  Part is one solid labelled with its Part id and coloured with its
  catalogue filament; each Bought Part is one labelled body from the
  Component's `bought_parts()`. Every body, Print Piece and cited Bought
  Part must match exactly one leaf. Each input of a joining step is swept
  alone from outside its Sub-assembly to its seat (`check_motion`
  `assembly_sequence` / `linear_motion_collision`, Manual order, overlap
  above 0.001 mm3 fails, no coarser than the thinnest wall); simultaneous
  moves, non-parallel joints and closed lead routes fail. Component rounds
  check the steps inside their Component; assembly rounds and final
  verification check the joins and write `measure/tree-report.json`, which
  the host checks before Make is accepted. The `paper` gate routes a Print
  Piece by its host Part or Sub-assembly the same way.
- A contract tree defect (the sealed tree itself inconsistent: a Print
  Piece or Bought Part it cannot account for, a leaf in two steps, a leads
  step below its lamps) stops the run with a need for the owner and refuses
  every later round of that tree (ADR 0092 decision 10); build-a-toy logs it
  in the toy's `TREE-ISSUES.md`. A labelling slip (built bodies that do not
  match a sound tree: a wrong label, a missing, extra or duplicated body) is
  an ordinary failure the next round repairs (owner decision Q28).
- Bought Part bodies carry the reserved not-printed magenta `#ff00ff`; the
  catalogue refuses a filament of that colour and the colour gate refuses a
  printed Part in it or a Bought Part without it. Final verification writes
  `measure/not-printed.json`, every Bought Part body with its catalogue id;
  the host checks it, the receipt and CLI list it, and Release ships it at
  `make/models/cad/not-printed.json` (owner decision Q29).
