# Frozen Forge and Quest economics profile v1

This immutable marker freezes a 32,000-token automatic context-compaction
ceiling and a 30-minute native turn boundary for new Codex Forge and Quest
runs. Their one Wish-wide Manager session retains **high reasoning effort**
across Invent, Make, optional Playtest, and Release. A timeout safely reaps only
the current launcher process; Workshop resumes the exact checkpointed session,
stage packet, Goal, and workspace through the existing bounded recovery path.
It does not create a replacement session, restart a stage, or weaken a gate.
One `workshop wish` or `workshop resume` invocation may launch at most eight
native turns across all stages, including timeout recovery, unfinished Goals,
and rejected proposals. A later explicit resume continues the exact session;
the cap prevents one unattended command from converting repeated invalid
proposals into an open-ended spend loop.

Thirty minutes is a critical-path boundary, not a quality budget. Forge and
Quest earn their extra work by finding a distinctive concept and proving one
memorable signature experience. Novelty must come from the relationship and
interaction, not gratuitous part count, mechanism count, or decorative
geometry. Before committing to detailed CAD, prove the hardest causal or
kinematic relationship with the smallest exact geometry that can falsify it.
Simplify a concept whose signature cannot be proven cheaply; do not spend a
full turn polishing an unproven mechanism.

Treat exact workspace bytes, `STAGE.json`, sealed contracts, and concise review
evidence as durable memory. Batch independent reads and deterministic checks.
Do not regenerate unchanged artifacts, repeat completed research, or use the
full verifier as an iteration loop. A recovery turn begins from the precise
remaining failure and existing bytes rather than rebuilding passed work.

This profile changes cognitive spend, not product authority. Forge and Quest
still use the selected Inventor's exact Taste, preserve the Wish, pass every
full-tier CAD and manual gate, and require authenticated Factory publication.
Quest still requires passing Playtest evidence bound to the current Made
revision. A failed artifact is repaired; no requirement or truthful limitation
is waived to meet the boundary.
