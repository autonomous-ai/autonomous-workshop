# Dustlight Crossing

A compact astronomy-themed Jungle Chess set for two players, preserving the exact Leiden Dou Shou Qi rules. The eight celestial silhouettes carry visible ranks; ivory circular bases move first, and blue clipped-square bases form the other team.

Read **RULES.pdf** for setup, the complete rules and the astronomy key. RULES.md contains the same rules as searchable text.

## Contents

- Four board panels: southwest, southeast, northwest and northeast.
- Sixteen playing pieces: one of each rank for each team.
- Twenty separately addressable STEP solids in `parts/`.
- The complete starting-position assembly in `assembled.step` and its assembly descriptor.
- Editable parametric sources and digital verification evidence in `cad/`.

The board measures 222 × 282 mm and stands 5 mm high. Cells have 30 mm pitch. Bases occupy 22 × 22 mm or less; complete piece heights run from 18 to 32 mm. The assembled starting position is 37 mm high.

## Printing and placing

The design assumes PLA, a 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layers and a 220 × 220 × 220 mm print bed. Print each part in its supplied orientation with its flat underside on the bed. The assembly is a placement reference, not one print. The largest panel is 126 × 156 mm.

Abut the four panels on a level table, matching the straight grid edges. There are no clips or retaining joints. The circle in the border marks White's end; the square marks Black's. The full board has seven columns and nine rows. Long separated cell strokes mark water, four short bars mark traps, and closed frames mark dens. The rules diagram provides coordinates and setup.

Color is a secondary cue; team base shapes remain distinct in one color. The celestial names describe an observation fiction, and the game ranks are not scientific brightness measurements.

## Evidence and limits

The package records digital geometry, fit, wall, overhang and visual checks. Its finite rules audit compares 54,956 cases using two locally authored rule models and explicit fixtures; this is not exhaustive proof over all game states. Physical printing, tactile comfort, durability and human play have not been tested. Motion is unverified under the selected run policy. Panel retention is not claimed.

Traditional game methods are combined with original sculptural geometry and rules wording. Source art was not reproduced. No affiliation with Leiden or a commercial game edition is implied.
