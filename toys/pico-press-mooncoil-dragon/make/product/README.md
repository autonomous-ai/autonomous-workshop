# Mooncoil Dragon

Mooncoil Dragon is one printable solid. It is modeled in millimetres as a constant 16 mm extrusion and prints on its broad XY face with Z beginning at the bed. The outer profile carries two straight resting facets joined by a broad convex arc. The fully penetrating window reads diagonally in the sleeping-dragon pose and becomes a bright crescent with a materially connected three-tower castle when rocked clockwise.

The designed envelope is approximately 100 × 90 × 16 mm. Broad silhouette landmarks—three back spines, swept horn, blunt lowered muzzle, wing notch, and a thick tucked tail—are intentionally larger than decorative detail. The aperture ligaments and castle attachment are several nozzle widths thick.

Suggested starting process: PLA, 0.20 mm layers, 0.4 mm nozzle, three perimeters, 15–20% infill. These are starting settings, not evidence of a successful print. This digital Make stage does not claim physical stability, durability, print success, or human playtest results.
