# Pearl Pulse

Use the complete instructions and print quantities in [cad/README.md](cad/README.md).
Parametric source and isolated print orientations are in `cad/`.

## Manufacturing units

The addressable solids in `parts/` describe individual material bodies. Print
each tentacle as the complete nine-body arrangement in `cad/part_chain.step`;
make eight copies. Do not separate, repack, or print its captive beads alone.
Print the three bell colour regions together at their shared registered origin.
The combined `assembled.step` is a placement reference, not a print layout.

Use four colours of the same PETG family, a 0.4 mm nozzle and the documented
orientations. The motor and return loops and damping grease are consumables;
they are specified in the instructions and are not printed solid parts.

## Evidence limits

Motion verification is disabled for this run. The pressed illustration is an
authored static pose; it does not establish a naturally settled shape. No
successful physical print, physical fit, durability, silence or measured
30-second runtime is claimed. The design requires physical calibration of its
rubber loops and damping. See the dated CAD reports for checks actually run.
