# Pocket Eclipse Menagerie CAD

One-piece shadow caster, millimetres, built as the union of two broad 4 mm
silhouette slabs. `pocket_eclipse.step.py` is the single printable entry.

- Overall design envelope: about 94 × 56 × 78 mm; palm-sized.
- Print stance: Z minimum on the bed; no assembly, supports, or loose parts.
- Owl state: phone flashlight centred 250–600 mm from the toy on the −X side,
  aimed along +X toward a wall 300–1000 mm beyond the toy.
- Rabbit state: rotate the whole toy 90° about Z; light then travels +Y.
- The broad base/feet and intersecting slabs are the single hand control and
  the registration cue: keep Z vertical and a silhouette face square to wall.
- Digital orthographic projections establish geometry only. Brightness, edge
  softness, recognition by people, and physically cast performance are not
  observed here.

Printer declaration: `--bed 220x220x220`, 0.4 mm nozzle, rigid opaque filament.

Rebuild with the materialized CAD skill's `verify_project --fresh --exports`.
