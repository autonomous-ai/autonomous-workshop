# CAD brief — Pocket Eclipse Menagerie

- Model: one-piece held sculpture and dual-axis shadow caster.
- Units/axes: mm; Z up; origin at slab intersection on the bed.
- Signature: fox beneath crescent in hand; owl along +X; leaping rabbit after
  quarter-turn around Z, along +Y.
- Construction: two intersecting broad silhouette masks, each 4 mm thick;
  shallow +X fox/crescent relief contained inside the cross-axis silhouette.
- Manufacturing: one rigid opaque FDM print, no support, assembly, hardware,
  text, or moving interface. Minimum designed slab thickness is 4.0 mm.
- Outputs: STEP primary; STL secondary; RGB hero and signature renders.
- Validation: one positive-volume connected solid; bed contact; bbox within
  palm-sized/220 mm bed; manifold STL; 0.4 mm nozzle thickness gate; exact
  binary +X/+Y projection evidence and silhouette landmark audit.
- Optical limitation: directional orthographic projection is a digital proxy
  for a phone light at the declared finite distances, not physical evidence.
