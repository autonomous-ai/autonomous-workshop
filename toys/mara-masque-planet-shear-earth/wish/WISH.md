# Wish

The rank-four piece of the matter side: a stylised Earth on a flat blue disc, continents standing a full millimetre proud of the ocean so you read the coastlines with a thumb as well as an eye, white ice over the pole, and the rank picked out on the disc wall in seven raised bars where a seated player looks. It is one printed body, 34 mm across the base and 25 mm tall, sized to stand in a board square rather than on a shelf.
