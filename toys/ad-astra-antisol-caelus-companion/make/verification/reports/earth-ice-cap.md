# Earth's northern ice, measured on the built solids

Sampled 0.10 mm under the sphere, in the planet's own frame, carried
into the piece by the same obliquity the part uses, and classified
against every colour body of the world. `I` ice, `L` land, `D`
dryland, `.` the bare globe -- ocean.

## The channel near the date line

A polar render shows a narrow dark slot reaching in toward the cap near
longitude 180. It is open water, and it is in the supplied atlas rather
than in the boolean: arctic lobe 4 ends at 176 east and lobe 5 begins at
178 west, so between them the sea reaches north to the cap boundary.
One degree of longitude, 60 to 74 north:

Read left to right, that is longitude 168 east through 180 to 168 west.
The gap closes at 72 degrees, exactly where the cap starts.

This is the point of the lobes. A bare cap ends on an exact circle of
latitude and reads as a lid laid on the globe; lobes with real water
between them read as an ice field.
