The files named for assembly occurrences are the exact colored regions of assembled.step. The sixteen component-role STEP files are fused reference solids copied byte-for-byte from their verified CAD entries. They are alternative component representations, not extra pieces to add to the toy. Print the arranged files in ../../engineering/prints: do not independently arrange the captive chain links or coupon halves. The four coupon roles instantiate three pins and one of each receiver.
