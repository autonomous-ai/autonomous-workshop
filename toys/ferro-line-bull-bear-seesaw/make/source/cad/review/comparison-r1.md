## Focused improvements

1. Give the left character a bovine silhouette: distinct broad muzzle, horns that project laterally then curve upward, and separate small ears below the horn roots. Keep the horn tips blunt. Add a lowered brow/head attitude and discernible paired hoof shapes or a forward-leaning shoulder profile so “charging bull” is plausible. Do not rely on labels to explain it.
2. Position each blue tear immediately under its corresponding eye, with a short trail connecting it to the eye region. Use recognizable rounded drops with a narrow upper end, avoiding two isolated triangular shapes near the mouth. The face should read as crying at normal front-view size without the brief. Maintain fully plain, tear-free eyes at level and bull-high.
3. If these corrections expose more backing, trim or hide blue cover protrusions at the ears and outer sides. Their present visibility dilutes the intended tears-only reveal. This is secondary to the two blocking issues.

The central visible linkage and boxy bodies are finish opportunities, not independent blocking departures from the explicit Wish. The product has meaningful volume and an understandable seesaw composition. Approval should wait for the bull species/charging cues and eye-associated crying cues to become independently legible. No conclusion here claims working motion, print success, physical fit or physical stability.
