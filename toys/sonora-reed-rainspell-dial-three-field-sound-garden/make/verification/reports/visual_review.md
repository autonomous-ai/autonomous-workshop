# CAD visual review

The generated assembly STL was rendered from isometric, top, and front viewpoints in `renders/`. Visual inspection confirms a low circular 120 mm plinth, a guarded 108 mm broad ring, three open spokes, a central service cap, the opposed counterweight/follower-head pair, and three visibly different density/grouping regions on the stationary deck. The side view confirms the guarded overlap and low terrace silhouette; the top view confirms the working annulus remains visible through the wheel.

The render is geometric inspection only. It does not establish print success, fit, motion under friction, cadence perception, sound character, loudness, durability, or safety. Triangle edges are an inspection overlay, not product texture.
