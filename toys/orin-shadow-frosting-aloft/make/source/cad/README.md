# Frosting Aloft CAD project

Two separately printable parts form a hand-operated shadow caster. Each part prints broad face down. In use, the base is stood upright and the frosting cap travels 27 mm along two guides.

- Units: mm
- Printer bed: `--bed 220x220x220`
- Printed parts: 2
- Assembly: slide the cap's open rear guide cheeks down around the base guides; this digital model does not claim measured physical fit.
- Light: broad side parallel to a pale wall, phone flashlight 200–350 mm behind, wall 300–700 mm ahead.

CAD brief:
- Lowered envelope: 78 W × 22 D × 85 H maximum.
- Raised envelope: 78 W × 22 D × 113 H.
- Shadow-critical faces remain within a compact depth band.
- Guide stems: 5 mm broadside width at x = ±15 mm; 27 mm visible travel.
- Validation: separate printable part entries, one combined upright entry, endpoint state exports, strict fit/mesh/thickness preflight, exact slide manifest, and final geometry checks.
