# Orbit Cradle CAD project

The self-contained CAD project is in `cad/`; its single combined entry is
`cad/assembled.step.py`. Root-level assembled exports remain the required
Workshop product artifacts.

One printable solid, nominal envelope 84 × 84 × 18 mm (X × Y × Z), units mm.
The XY silhouette carries the moon-and-star reading; Z is print height. Print
the broad back at Z=0 without supports, then stand the object on its continuous
outer circular arc to rock it. The visible bridge is intentionally structural.

The three signature-sheet poses are -26°, +26°, and the returned -26° about Z.
They are exact rigid-body presentation poses, not evidence of measured physical
dynamics or a claim that the object contains mechanical detents.
