# Tempest Lull CAD project

One printable part, generated STEP-first from `tempest_lull.step.py`.

- Units: millimetres
- Axes: X left/right, Y front/back, Z up
- Print stance: crescent keel on the XY bed, no supports
- Nominal envelope: 93 × 18 × 72 mm
- Intended motion: rigid-body rocking on the circular keel, ±14° visual crest poses
- Printer bed: `--bed 220x220x220`

The softly lobed cloud profile, broad crescent keel, lightning bolt, and
finite-width S-ribbon rain stem share an 18 mm holdable depth. The bolt's acute
corners are rounded to remove sub-nozzle knife edges. There are no
loose pieces, joints, purchased parts, or assembly operations. Digital checks
establish geometry and print eligibility only; they do not claim a successful
physical print or measured cycle life.
