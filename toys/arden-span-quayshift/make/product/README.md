# QUAYSHIFT

**Build the town. Let the ferry through.**

A small canal town made from eight loose architectural modules, a water tray and one hand-moved ferry. The same pieces rebuild into different journeys. Three edited starter challenges are included; this is not a completed 24-card collection.

![The assembled town](cad/snap/iso.png)

## Start

[See the empty tray and its engraved coordinates](cad/snap/tray.png).

Put the tray on a level table with letters A–E along the near edge. Rows 1–5 run away from you. The opening at C1 is the entrance; C5 is the exit. Open `challenges/cards.html` for the three setup cards. Print at actual size using the page's A4 layout, or read on screen. Solutions are separate in `challenges/solutions.html`.

Place the pieces shown in a card's initial clues. Keep those placements while solving. Add every remaining architecture module upright, turning by quarter-turns if needed. Match the grid reservations in the diagrams: an arch reserves its entire three-cell strip, including its opening. Other pieces reserve their complete L, two-cell or one-cell footprints. Do not overlap reservations or stack pieces. Colors help identification but impose no adjacency rule.

## Try the journey

Set the ferry on the floor at C1. Move it by hand along the centers of neighboring cells, one horizontal or vertical step at a time. Keep the cabin’s long axis pointing toward the far edge and slide sideways on horizontal steps. At a turn, reach the cell center before changing direction. Travel straight through each high arch, perpendicular to its strip; do not turn inside an arch cell. Visit both high bridges, reach C5 and leave through that opening. Do not lift the ferry, shift a building, revisit a cell or leave the board elsewhere. Unused water is allowed.

The low gate is deliberately too low for the cabin. Stop at contact; do not force it or tip the ferry. If the trip fails, return the ferry to the entrance, lift and reposition the loose buildings, then try again. Between bridges, change from pushing behind the cabin to receiving at a corner or side. The short passages leave space for this handoff. Lift buildings by their substantial upper mass when resetting.

The first challenge has multiple valid completions. A different route drawing does not necessarily mean a different construction solution. Challenge counts and evidence scope are recorded beside the solutions.

## Kit and care assumptions

Ten physical parts: two high bridges A/B, low gate C, courtyard D, two-cell buildings E/F, towers G/H, tray and ferry. Each part is one material/color solid. All architectural details stay inside their placement reservations. No drive, magnets, gears, people, animals, trees or small loose ornaments.

Use an ordinary protective box with internal space 220×220×105mm and fitted paperboard/foam protection. Pack the tray flat, support each building separately in its illustrated town position, protect the ferry in its own pocket, and cushion the roof masses against the lid. Packaging is not another printed kit part. Keep the kit dry and avoid bending bridges or forcing the ferry.

## What the evidence means

The CAD sources, exact STEP parts, placement data and deterministic reports are included. Source print checks do not establish a successful manufactured sample. No physical fit, human handling, enjoyment, durability, market demand or sale price has been verified. This is an adult construction-puzzle prototype; the roughly US$49 complete-gift price remains a commercial hypothesis. Manufacturing and material assumptions are reported separately.
