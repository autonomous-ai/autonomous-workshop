# Waterloo Command

A framed battlefield, painted armies and a battle shaped by your decisions. Form infantry into lines, columns and squares; coordinate horse and guns; hold the farms or commit your reserves.

The opening display interprets Waterloo at approximately 11:30 on 18 June 1815. French and Anglo-Allied formations occupy the field; the cooperating Prussians wait in the labelled reserve area for their later arrival. The terrain selectively compresses historical distances into a 104 × 86 cm tabletop landscape.

Two players command the French and coalition sides. The supplied finite roster supports the main eight-round battle and three shorter, explicitly counterfactual scenarios. Finished troop pieces move by hand; the landscape and scenery remain fixed.

Read the [illustrated play guide](customer-guide.pdf), then choose a [scenario sheet](scenario-sheets.pdf). The [command aids](command-aids.pdf) reproduce the supplied cards and counters. A [quick reference](quick-reference.md) supports play after learning the rules.
