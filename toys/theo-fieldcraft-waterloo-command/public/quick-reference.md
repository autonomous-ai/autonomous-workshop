# Waterloo Command • Quick reference v1.1
Use with the full rules and one scenario sheet.

**Round:** arrivals → clear activations/reset orders → alternating activations → end check. French starts odd rounds, coalition even. One activation per unit; one supporter also activates. Pass is permanent for that round. One free commander relocation of up to two cells per side at its first activation.

**Orders:** one per action. Square Advance and infantry Enter cost two. Support costs one extra. No carried orders.

**Move:** Advance one cell, choose facing; square costs two. Quick march exactly two straight cells, then +1 stress; column/horse/skirmisher only, stress below 2. Road march: column/gun two straight along E, no stress. March endpoints cannot be enemy-adjacent; no entered farm/rough or sunken-lane crossing. Occupied cells block movement.

**Other actions:** Reform formation/facing; Fire without moving/turning; Assault front-adjacent, no preliminary move; Rally −1 stress if no adjacent enemy and friendly commander within two; Withdraw one empty cell farther from nearest enemy, retain facing, +1 stress (illegal at stress 2); Hold chooses facing.

| Type | Fire | Assault | Defence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Line | 3 | 2 | 3 |
| Column | 2 | 3 | 3 |
| Square | 1 | 1 | 3 |
| Cavalry | — | 3 | 2 |
| Skirmisher | 2 | — | 2 |
| Gun | 3 at 1; 2 at 2–3 | — | 2 |

**Modifiers:** subtract own stress; defender F5 +1 versus Assault only; square +2 D versus cavalry Assault only; nonsquare side/rear −1 D; cavalry +1 A against open nonsquare infantry/skirmisher; terrain at most +1 D. One eligible facing, adjacent, unactivated supporter below stress 2 gives +1 A for one extra order.

**Results, A−D:** Fire below 0: nothing. Fire 0–1: defender +1 stress. Assault ≤0: attacker +1 stress. Assault 1: defender +1 stress. Either attack ≥2: defender +1 stress then retreat directly away. Stress 3: remove immediately. Blocked/off-board/illegal retreat: remove. Successful Assault must enter the vacated cell; Fire never advances.

**Terrain:** B4/E3 infantry column/square or skirmisher only; H3/H6 column or skirmisher only. Farms +1 D. B5/J5–J7 rough excludes horse/guns, +1 D versus Fire only. A2–H2 ridge +1 D versus Assault from south. Artillery south of ridge cannot shoot A1–H1. Units/farms between gun and target block its cardinal ray. Lane A–H between ranks1/2: no march, horse gains extra stress crossing on any move. No voluntary horse crossing at stress2.

**Victory:** use sheet; objectives require physical combat occupancy at final scoring. No on-field units and no pending arrivals means immediate loss. Fixed round cap always applies. No commander scores or fights.

**Reset:** all stress0, clear activations, restore sheet positions/facings and round1; excluded pieces on apron. Main display keeps P1/P2/Blücher on labelled reserve apron.
