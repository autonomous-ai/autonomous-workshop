# Wish

You supply two ordinary six-sided dice and a cup each; everything else is in the box. A printed backgammon set built as a small Sun: twenty-four radial lanes in two tones of orange fan out from a raised centre bar, and the rim is a crown of thirty-two separate curled flames standing off the disc's own circular edge. Each is broad where it meets the disc and narrows into a small blunt point, and they flick left and right in short irregular runs the way fire does, never all one way. No two are the same length. Thirty teardrop drops, fifteen cream and fifteen dark brown, travel the lanes under the ordinary 1911 rules, which come with it in full.
