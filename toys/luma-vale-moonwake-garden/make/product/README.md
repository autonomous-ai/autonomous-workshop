# Moonwake Garden — Make round 2 product tree

Moonwake Garden is a three-part STEP-first rotary optical toy. Its rear chassis carries the spindle, broad guide, isolated annular detent, thrust pads, and four split retention posts. A repaired70 mm opaque rotor carries one bounded110° annular-sector window. The fixed front mask contains eighteen petal apertures arranged as three simplified constellation readings and exposes only a small rotor-backed polar grip portal.

At rotor poses0°,−120°, and−240°, deterministic planar checks open exactly the5,6, and7 petals of Cassiopeia, Cygnus, and the Little Dipper reading within Ursa Minor. Intermediate partial blooms are intentional; the checks find no pose where two named beds are complete together.

## Tree

- `product.json` — bounded product facts and evidence limits.
- `cad/moonwake_garden/` — parametric source, combined/part STEP and STL outputs, exact build brief, motion proxies, and deterministic measurement evidence.
- `assembled.step` / `assembled.stl` — root review assembly exports, not a one-piece print target.
- `assembled.step.json` — exact source/output identity and checked assembly facts.
- `cad-verification.json` — hash-bound summary of commands that actually ran and their evidence files.
- `views/` — state silhouettes plus shaded, edge-separated product-derived exploded, rear-path, portal/trench, snap, vine/petal, and detent views; they establish visible geometry only.

## Evidence boundary

The final CAD pipeline can establish closed positive-volume geometry, exact dimensions, nominal clearances, absence of home-pose clashes, rigid proxy paths, mesh soundness, thickness sampling, and print-bed stance. It cannot establish elastic force, a successful print, physical fit, durability, ambient-light brightness, recognition, delight, or any human response. Those require same-material coupons, physical observation, and the host-authorized Quest Playtest stage.
