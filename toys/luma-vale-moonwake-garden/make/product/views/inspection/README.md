# Product-derived inspection views

These shaded, edge-separated views are generated directly from the same
`moonwake_garden_lib.py` part builders as the verified STEP files. Run
`../sources/render_inspection.py` with the Workshop CAD environment and a
workspace-local `XDG_CACHE_HOME` and `MPLCONFIGDIR`.

- `exploded-stack.png`: rear, rotor, and front in assembly order.
- `rear-optical-path.png`: rear field and rotor with the front removed.
- `portal-and-trench.png`: assembled right-side portal and home grip trench.
- `detent-and-notch.png`: rear annular detent beside the home rotor notch.
- `snap-detail.png`: one exact split snap and collar.
- `rounded-vine-petals.png`: front-mask petal apertures and rounded vine ends.

The views establish inspectability of digital geometry only. They are not
evidence of a physical print, elastic force, optical brightness, or recognition.
