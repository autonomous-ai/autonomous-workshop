# CAD brief: Comet Pebble

- Model: one-piece gravity-driven desk wobbler; new part.
- Units: millimetres.
- Coordinates: broad landing ellipse on XY at Z=0; +Z points toward the
  smiling star in the intended rest orientation.
- Envelope: palm-size, approximately 68 x 45 x 34 mm.
- Body: ten smoothly lofted, laterally offset and progressively rotated
  elliptical stations. The lower contact axes turn +10 degrees while the
  upper sections reach -17 degrees; the exact uniform-density principal
  inertia axis must differ from the lower-rail axis by 10-20 degrees.
- Rest: a 34.4 x 20.4 mm effective planar landing ellipse after the 0.8 mm sole
  edge fillet. The center of mass
  must project within it with at least 5 mm radial margin.
- Reveal: a rounded 14 mm star relief crosses the convex upper crown, with two
  flat-topped eye posts and a six-post smile fused deeply into the surface. The loft closes on one
  offset 8.4 x 6.4 mm crown cap above every cosmetic feature; the uniform-density
  COM projection lies outside that tiny cap, so it cannot serve as an inverted
  rest. The finite cap replaces a zero-radius crown tip that failed thickness;
  finite-width posts replace tangential sphere caps that also failed thickness.
- Print intent: one closed positive-volume solid; flat support-free stance;
  no internal void, loose part, hardware, glue, or purchased component.
- Edge construction: the loft continues with constant 36 x 22 mm sections
  below and above the bed plane, then is trimmed at Z=0 without a boolean
  overlap seam. A 0.8 mm sole-perimeter fillet and 0.8 mm crown-cap fillet
  remove the remaining exposed knife edges.
- Outputs: parametric source, STEP, STL, RGB presentation render, dimensional
  and stability checks.
- Validation: one solid; Z minimum zero; envelope within 70 x 46 x 36 mm;
  positive volume; COM landing margin; full fresh CAD pipeline including STEP
  validation, fit, mesh, and 0.4 mm nozzle thickness.
- Honest limitation: deterministic CAD checks establish geometry and balance
  intent, not a successful print, surface-dependent motion, durability, or
  repeatable human-observed settling.
