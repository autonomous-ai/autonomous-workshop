# Comet Heist: Twin-Pulse Vault Run

## Printed contents

Exactly 24 printed pieces: Tray A and Tray B; two dual-use seam/storage keys; two gate bridges; two gravity blades; two gate keepers; six tactile Sun comets; six tactile Orbit comets; and two two-well READY/SPENT magazines. The vault walls, gate cheeks, bank ridges, and score pips are integral to the trays. No hardware, magnets, elastic, springs, glue, or launcher is used.

## Set up

Join the trays at the center lap. Put one gate bridge, gravity blade, and keeper at each marked station, with the blade hanging freely and its paddle above the field. Keep the two seam keys across the outer seam lands, above the play path. Put all six comets for each side in that side's READY magazine wells. Each player sits at one end and shoots only toward the opposite vault.

Use only on a stable, level indoor table. Stop if a gate, keeper, key, or tray comes loose, or if any printed part cracks or develops a sharp edge. This digital Make package has not proved physical fit, impact retention, durability, or the target gate oscillation time.

## The six pulse patterns

Every regulation game uses the same schedule. `L` and `R` name the direction from the current shooter's viewpoint.

| Shot | Far gate | Near gate | Printed cue |
|---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 1 | L | L | LL |
| 2 | R | R | RR |
| 3 | L | R | LR |
| 4 | R | L | RL |
| 5 | L | L | LL |
| 6 | R | R | RR |

For each shot, take the top READY comet and place it flat anywhere within your launch bay. Announce ready. With a fingertip, pulse the far blade first, then the near blade, in the listed directions. While both blades visibly move—and before the near blade completes its third center-line crossing—make exactly one forward flick with one bare fingertip. Beginning that flick consumes the attempt. No repulse, replay, tool, launcher, or stored-energy aid is allowed.

## Legal path and dead shots

The comet must pass the near portal and then the far portal in the scoring direction. A backward recross neither cancels nor earns another clearance. Rail and gate contacts are legal. A shot is immediately worth 0 if the comet leaves the field, is touched again, jumps or bypasses a gate, lodges on top, enters the home vault, or dislodges a gate, keeper, key, or tray. After each shot, wait two seconds for the comet to become still.

## Scoring

A comet scores only if it remains flat and lies wholly beyond the target scoring plane inside one vault mouth:

- Wide vault: 2 points.
- Medium vault: 4 points.
- Narrow vault: 6 points.

Slingshot bonus: after clearing the far gate, the comet may touch exactly one of the two eligible three-ridge facets at the target end before entering a vault. Repeated contact with that same facet is still one contact. Touching both eligible facets cancels the bonus. A valid Slingshot adds 2, so the only legal shot values are 0, 2, 4, 6, and 8. Move the used comet to the SPENT side of its magazine.

## Solo run

Take one shot for each pattern, in order: six shots total, maximum 48. Provisional targets for Playtest are Cadet 10, Raider 18, and Master 26; they are not calibrated claims.

## Two-player game

Play six waves. In each wave both players shoot the same numbered pattern toward opposite ends. Alternate who shoots first each wave, so each player leads three times. After six waves each player has taken six shots; maximum score is 48.

If scores tie, compare valid Slingshots, then Narrow-vault successes. If still tied, play at most three paired sudden-death waves using patterns 1, 2, and 3 in order. Each player may move any one SPENT comet back to READY for each paired wave. A paired wave ends the tie only after both players have shot. If still tied after all three pairs, record a draw.
