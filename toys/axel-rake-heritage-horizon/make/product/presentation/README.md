# Cradle withdrawal states

These exact STEP states sample the declared remove-cradle path at 0, 10 and 20 of 20 steps, with the cradle moving downward by 0, 15 and 30 mm relative to the fixed motorcycle. They are presentation files, not extra physical pieces. The motorcycle needs a guiding hand once separated from the stand.

The source entries read the main CAD assembly and its motion manifest. Regenerate them with the supplied CAD gen tool and --write. The signature sheet renders all three at one fixed isometric view. Wheel motion is separately reconstructed from the main project motion manifest by the motion-presentation tool.
