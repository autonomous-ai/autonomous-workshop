# What this correction carried forward, and what it rebuilt

`MAKE-OPTIONS.json` for this run sets `carry_unchanged: true`, which is the
default for a correction. It permits exactly one thing: evidence about a part
whose STEP is byte-identical to the published set's may be carried forward,
because a gate is a pure function of the STEP it reads. It lowers no threshold
and skips nothing about the assembly.

**The warrant is the hash proof, and it is computed fresh over every part.**
Every one of the 24 printable entries was rebuilt from source with
`scripts/gen --write` and hashed against `make/made.json`'s `product_manifest`
in `revision-source.zip`.

| byte-different | 11 | `part_belt_cell.step`, `part_corona_cell.step`, `part_den_plug.step`, `part_panel_northeast.step`, `part_panel_northwest.step`, `part_panel_southeast.step`, `part_panel_southwest.step`, `part_world_saturn_anti.step`, `part_world_saturn_sol.step`, `part_world_uranus_anti.step`, `part_world_uranus_sol.step` |

**No printable part changed geometrically, and that is measured rather than
argued.** `parts/world.build_world` fuses the disc, the globe and the ring and
never the markings, so a marking correction cannot move a printed surface.
`measure/revision-part-hashes.md` imports all 11 byte-different STEPs from both
trees and compares them on exact volume and bounding box: the largest volume
difference anywhere is **1.13e-8 mm3** and every bounding box agrees to a
nanometre. What moved is the serialisation -- this project's generator does not
write identical STEP bytes for these parts between a warm multi-target
generation and a single-target one, and six of them come out with a different
entity count for the same shape. None of those generators is in
`measure/source-diff.md`, which is the whole difference between the two source
trees and holds six files.

**The two parts the correction is about are in the identical column**, which is
the check it turns on: `part_world_neptune_sol.step` and
`part_world_neptune_anti.step` came back byte-for-byte as published.

## What this run did NOT carry forward

Nothing about the print gates or the rounds. Because the byte comparison could
not clear every part, **all 24 components were given a fresh isolated
`make_round` of their own** -- build, 0.80 mm wall gate and 45-degree overhang
gate at a 0.4 mm nozzle, three rendered views each, and a native visual
inspection recorded against the exact packet hash. The published set's round
histories were sanitised for publication (absolute paths redacted), so their
tool logs no longer hash to what their own state files record; they are
reference data in `revision-source.zip` and none of them is in this product
tree. `measure/component-rounds/` holds this run's rounds and only this run's,
and `measure/thickness-*.md` and `measure/overhang-*.md` are this run's copies
of them rather than the archive's redacted ones.

The assembled-object round, `refs:assembly`, `validate:assembly`,
`interfere:assembly`, `verify_project`, the whole-set renders and the
independent blind review were all run fresh, as the policy requires. The
assembly changes whenever any part does, and interference is a property of the
whole set. The review was run fresh twice: the first round found a blocking
defect, the geometry was repaired, every canonical image was re-rendered and
the second round read the new ones.

## One report that could not be re-run, and why

`measure/uranus-bare.md` is carried rather than regenerated, and for a
different reason from the rest of the carried list. Its arithmetic is the
previous edition's: it proves Uranus's globe came back at its old volume PLUS
the two polar hoods that edition removed, by looking the hood occurrences up in
the published assembly. The published assembly this edition corrects is the one
that already has no hoods, so those occurrences do not exist in the baseline
and the script exits on a missing occurrence. Re-running it here would produce
a FAIL that says nothing about this edition.

It is carried under its own heading as the previous edition's evidence, and the
statement a reader actually wants about Uranus on THIS edition is measured
elsewhere: `measure/occurrence-geometry.md` compares all 202 occurrence labels
of this run's assembly against the published one and finds nothing outside
`neptune_sol_*` and `neptune_anti_*` that gained, lost or moved a solid, Uranus
included, and `measure/revision-part-hashes.md` measures both Uranus printed
STEPs at the published volume and bounding box.

## What is NOT evidence

`measure/retired-hood-evidence.md` and the reports it names measured a feature
an earlier edition removed. They are carried under their own heading as
history. No carried report, and no file anywhere in `revision-source.zip`, is
offered as proof that anything in THIS edition passes.
