# AI Players plan

Playtest means AI agents examining and simulating the exact pre-delivery product.
It is not a human print-and-play. Human owner feedback begins later as Reviews.

The loop may end early if every gate passes. It will not invent failures merely
to consume three rounds.

## Players

- First-time chess player — identifies pieces and sets up without labels.
- Competitive chess player — checks glance readability and chess fidelity.
- Low-color-vision player — tests side and rank identity without color.
- NYC design critic — rejects souvenir, cliché, and pasted-on architecture.
- Print breaker — hunts thin features, supports, seams, rocking, and weak forms.
- Adversarial player — rotates pieces, crowds the board, and probes ambiguity.

## Round 1 — Read the skyline

Test the first complete CAD revision in grayscale and from both seats. Require
exact inventory and setup; 100% king/queen recognition; ≥95% aggregate rank
recognition with no rank below 90%; 100% side recognition without color; and an
unmistakable structural NYC trace.

## Round 2 — Play on the object

After any Round 1 repairs, simulate initial setup, seeded midgames, captures,
castling, promotion, board reset, vertical pickup paths, crowded recognition,
board orientation, rotated pieces, and reversed-board misuse. Bind measured base
clearance, rank hierarchy, flat contact, and stability proxies.

## Round 3 — Release regression

Use fresh views and seeds. Rerun every gate: rules, recognition, inventory,
STEP reimport, mesh topology, placement, lift paths, bed packing, slicer, exact
NYC-to-geometry trace, Instructions, and unresolved feedback.

## Evidence rule

Each round lives under `round-0N/` with evaluator identity/version, artifact and
configuration hashes, measured findings, requested changes, source-record
hashes, timestamps, and the four Workshop capability result IDs. Generic empty
feedback is not acceptable evidence of a serious round.
