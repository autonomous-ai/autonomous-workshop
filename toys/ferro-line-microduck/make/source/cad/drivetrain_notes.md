# Elastic rolling drive — explicit build specification

This is a knob-wound rolling adaptation of the observed standing character. Both front wheels turn with one hollow axle; the rear rollers turn independently through ground contact. It does not walk. CAD and conditional engineering calculations are complete evidence of the declared geometry, not evidence of a printed running prototype.

## Consumable, routing and operation

Use one **4.0 mm solid silicone cord, nominal 60 Shore A**, from the MVQ Silicones stock cord range. Cut approximately 240 mm to allow the 81.2 mm working span, accessible ties and tails. This replaces the earlier unspecified rubber-band loop: two 4 mm strands cannot fit this bore. The supplier states hardness tolerance ±5, but supplies no torsional spring constant or repeated winding rating. The selection therefore has a calculated design window and remains physically unqualified.

Route a single strand through the rotor's 5.4 mm axial bore. The strand's centre follows Y=-6, approximately Z=10.5 through the narrow section. The straight-core radial reserve is 0.2 mm at this 0.5 mm offset; when centred the reserve is 0.7 mm. Pull through using a temporary thread before tying. Tie around the accessible transverse bar of the external left ring and the right knob. A single round turn around each bar with backup half-hitches outside the outer face keeps knots outside the narrow bore. Leave at least 12 mm tails and secure them against their own standing cord, clear of the floor, frame and revolving wheel surfaces. Do not force a completed knot through the bore. The rigid audit reserves a one-strand bend envelope, not the unknown final shape of hand-tied backup knots. Inspect the tied assembly for free rotation and knot creep before loading it.

The eye windows admit one 4 mm strand with 0.7 mm vertical reserve on the anchor and 0.8 mm at the knob. The anchor's 2×2 mm bar and knob's 2×2.4 mm bar give a cord centreline bend radius of approximately 3 mm. Silicone can be laced around them, but local cyclic bend stress, knot slippage and tear life are not established by the supplier data. Smooth printed bar edges and bore before assembly; a sharp layer seam is unacceptable at a cord contact.

Working bar-centre span is 81.2 mm, from X=-40.2 to X=41. Fit just taut, with no more than **0.5% axial extension** (0.406 mm over the working span). The long cut length is allowance for ties, not an instruction to stretch excess cord into the axle. Adjust the ties to remove sag without appreciable preload. Place the toy on a level surface, hold the body and turn the knob **clockwise viewed from the right end** for at most **two complete turns from relaxed**. Keep hold of the knob until release. Unwinding rolls forward toward -Y. There is no ratchet: the user supplies restraint during winding. For tool-free reset, restrain the knob, free the wheels from the surface and let it unwind under control. Replace the cord by untying the exterior bars; the bonded frame joints stay assembled.

## Transmission, retention and calculation evidence

Rear roller motion is a no-slip ground assumption at angular ratio10/6, not an invented shaft connection. Cosmetic parts, frame, anchor and pins are all explicit operating obstacles. Only integral shoulder retention directions are rigidly provable. The opposite rotor direction relies on the bonded keyed left wheel; rear pin and anchor retention also rely on permanent adhesive. No manifest calls these removable parts genuine fixed retention roots or claims to measure adhesive strength.

`measure/check_drive.py` verifies a diameter4 straight core and conservative external return-wrap boxes against actual source solids. It uses solid round-shaft torsion T=GJθ/L and γ=rθ/L, J=πd⁴/32. At two turns, d4 and L81.2, J=25.133 mm⁴ and surface shear strain is0.310. An approximate Gent hardness/modulus correlation with an incompressible elastomer assumption gives initial torque3.86..5.76 Nmm across55..65 ShoreA. A further arbitrary 30% reduction gives the lower design scenario2.704 Nmm. Neither the correlation nor the reduction is measured cord data; the65A upper case slightly extends beyond the cited study's tested hardness62.9.

The check recomputes mass and support loads from every current appearance solid at full PLA density1.24 g/cm³. It evaluates journal, thrust and rolling losses across friction assumptions, including preload from0.5% extension. This demonstrates sensitivity, not a promised travel distance. At the current20-part revision with8.4 mm journals, assumed journal/thrust friction0.1 and rolling coefficient0.01 give loss1.318 Nmm, positive initial margin1.386 Nmm and quasi-static travel64 mm; friction0.2 leaves only14 mm and0.25 predicts a stall. No physical test has measured release torque, friction, cyclic life, knot creep, bond strength, layer adhesion, traction or running distance. Those limits remain explicit. A physically stalled specimen must be repaired within the stated two-turn limit; additional winding is not a permitted workaround.
