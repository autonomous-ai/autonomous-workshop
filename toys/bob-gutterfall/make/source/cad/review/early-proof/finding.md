# Early proof finding

The first exact render exposed a globally rotated belly cylinder and slab-like
wings. Both were rejected. The rebuilt source keeps one solid, opens a 31 mm
rear-entry C-throat, carries its upper and lower jaws through a concealed thick
bridge, rounds the rear hook, and uses thick swept wing masses. The mechanism
is now legible as a gargoyle wrapped around a furniture edge; physical rolling
and catch reliability remain unproven until a real print is tested.
