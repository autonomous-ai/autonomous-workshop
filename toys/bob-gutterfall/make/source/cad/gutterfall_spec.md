# Gutterfall CAD brief

One rigid, one-piece, palm-sized gargoyle uses an ordinary table edge as both
its exposed rolling fulcrum and opposing catch surface. In the ready state the
convex belly is above the tabletop and the tail's lower pad is below it. A push
past the balance point lets the complete body rotate approximately 70 degrees;
the broad inner tail pad then meets the underside.

All dimensions are millimetres. X points from tail toward nose, Y spans the
wings, and Z is upward in the printable model. The table extends toward -X and
its edge runs along Y. The functional throat is open toward -X.

## Dimension ledger

- Verified nose-to-tail envelope: 113 (close to the approximately 105 target).
- Verified wing span: 84; wings are broad, rounded thick solids.
- Open vertical throat: 31, accepting the Wish's 18–28 edge range with 3 mm
  modeled vertical allowance at the thickest edge.
- Belly rolling crest radius: 18.
- Tail underside stop: 24 x 34 broad contact pad, 6 minimum thickness.
- Horn tips are blunt cones with 3 mm terminal rounding volumes.
- Minimum decorative member target: 2.4; primary load path is 6 or thicker.
- Intended bed: 220 x 220 x 220, 0.4 mm nozzle.

The digital state study places the same rigid exported mesh against a neutral
table-edge witness in ready and 70-degree caught poses. It is geometric
evidence only; friction, furniture finish safety, impact, and a successful
physical catch remain unverified until tested on a print.
