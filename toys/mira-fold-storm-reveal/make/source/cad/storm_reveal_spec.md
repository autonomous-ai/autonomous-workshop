# Storm Reveal CAD brief

- Model: three-part pocket puzzle and deployed tabletop storm scene.
- Task type: new multi-part moving assembly.
- Units: millimetres.
- Coordinates: the cloud silhouette lies in XY; +Z runs from the cloud face toward its back. The reveal pivot is `(0, 11)` in the silhouette plane. Printable entries sit on XY with minimum Z = 0.
- Envelope: generated deployed assembly 86.98 x 65.00 x 12.80 mm; the clean closed silhouette stays within the same 87 x 65 mm cloud footprint.
- Parts: one cloud receiver, one rainbow/drive rotor, and one lightning/guide rotor. No other printed or purchased components.
- Interaction: assemble the square drive through the lightning rotor and seat the drive plus guide pin in the cloud. Turn the outer rainbow clockwise 90 degrees from the sleeping stop to the storm-scene stop; reverse the same motion to reset.
- Mates: 5.0 mm square drive; lightning socket derived with 0.10 mm clearance per side; cloud round pivot pocket clears the square drive corners; 3.2 mm guide pin runs in a swept slot derived with 0.20 mm clearance per side.
- Print stance: every part prints flat. The rainbow and lightning print motif-side down with vertical pins/drive rising from the bed-facing plate; the cloud is flipped so its blind pockets and sleepy-face grooves open upward.
- Minimum functional thicknesses: 3.0 mm motif plates, 3.2 mm guide pin, 5.0 mm square drive, 6.0 mm cloud body, and 5.0 mm rainbow band.
- Materials: one common rigid FDM filament such as PLA or PETG; colour is optional and not required to identify the forms.
- Supports/hardware: none.
- Validation targets: three printable entries; positive-volume closed solids; 87 x 65 mm closed footprint; no seated interference above contact tolerance; clear 90 degree reset/reveal sweep; hard stops encoded by the guide track; fit equations audited from shared parameters; watertight/manifold STLs; minimum thickness checked for a 0.4 mm nozzle.
- Assumptions: the square socket is a removable snug fit, not a snap; digital checks cannot prove printer-specific feel, friction retention, fatigue life, or successful physical printing.
