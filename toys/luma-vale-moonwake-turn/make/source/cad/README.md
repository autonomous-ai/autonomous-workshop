# Moonwake Turn CAD

One printable part, generated by `moonwake.step.py`. The piece prints on the broad `z=0` wave/keel datum; all whale and moon openings run vertically through the part and require no support.

- Nominal envelope: 94 × 40 × 68 mm
- Printer envelope: 220 × 220 × 220 mm
- Process: single-color FDM, 0.4 mm nozzle, 0.2 mm layer, four walls
- Assembly: none
- Supports: none intended
- Finish: restrained 0.8 mm rounding on the mast-cap depth edges and long base-top edges

Rebuild with the CAD skill `scripts/gen cad/moonwake/moonwake.step.py --write`, then export the fresh STEP to STL.
