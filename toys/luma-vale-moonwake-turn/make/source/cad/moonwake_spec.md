# Moonwake Turn build specification

## Intent

One immutable connected geometry carries two orthogonal visual truths. Along Y, the outer XZ contour is a tiny sailing ship crossing a wave. After a 90° roll, the viewer looks through Z and sees a leaping whale, identified by two upward-splayed blow jets, beside a crescent as bright negative space.

## Dimension ledger

- 94 mm width, 40 mm depth, 68 mm height — assumed palm-sized envelope.
- 12 mm nominal wave/keel band — assumed structural and stability reserve.
- 4.4 mm mast — assumed four-wall printable neck.
- Aperture features use 5 mm or larger minor dimensions, except intentional tapered transitions.
- 0.8 mm fillets finish the mast-cap depth edges and the long top edges of the base without changing either orthographic silhouette.

## Manufacturing

The single body is printed on z=0. Aperture walls are vertical, avoiding roofs, bridges, and trapped support. No component, fastener, color registration, or assembly is required.

## Truth boundary

CAD and mesh checks establish digital geometry only. They do not establish a successful physical print, real-world stability, durability, or human response.
