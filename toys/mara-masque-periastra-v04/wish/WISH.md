# Wish

Lift a domed observatory roof off the box and the game is underneath. The dome's shutter is open the whole height of the dome, from the observatory floor over the apex and down the far flank, and a long telescope stands on that floor on its own footing and lies across the opening, most of its length visible down the slit with only its mouth clearing the shell. Under the roof is English draughts between the Sun and the Moon, on a chequerboard whose dark squares are inlaid tiles in a second colour. Every counter carries its mark raised out of a sunk field on both faces alike, so there is no wrong way up, and a king is crowned the way an ordinary draughts set crowns one - by stacking a second counter of the same colour.
