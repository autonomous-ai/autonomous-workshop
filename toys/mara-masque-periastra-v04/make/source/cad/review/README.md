# Reviews in this tree, and which of them is evidence

**`revision-d/` is the only current evidence.** It is the independent blind review
of Periastra Zenith: one critic, given the canonical images in `../snap/` and
nothing else, recording its unprimed reading before it was told anything about the
object, and only then comparing every requirement — including the negative ones —
against what it had already written down.

`revision-c/` and `source-set/` are **history**. They are the reviews of earlier
builds of this object, carried into this tree so the record of how it got here is
not lost. They are not offered as evidence for anything in this revision, they
describe geometry that has since changed, and no claim in
`../snap/SIGNATURE-REVIEW.json`, `../GEOMETRY-NOTES.md`, `../README.md` or
`../../README.md` rests on them.

A review is bound to the exact image bytes it read. `../snap/SIGNATURE-REVIEW.json`
carries the sha256 of the concept and of the two canonical images the current
review passed on.
