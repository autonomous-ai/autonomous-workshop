# Geometry and verification limits

The current design has seven planets per arm, with 14/15/18/20/22/23/26 teeth, a stationary 36-tooth sun and a black annular carrier field. There are 44 sculpture leaves, 21 printable roles and 49 delivery pieces including the separate five-piece coupon kit.

Static validation covers all current roles: nine changed or re-exported components passed fresh native validation, and twelve unchanged component STEP files match their earlier validated bytes exactly. The 44-leaf assembly passes full validation. Nominal-pose interference considered 946 pairs, tested 96, rejected 850 by bounding boxes and truncated none, with zero clashes at the unchanged 1 mm³ tolerance. Initially stale reference identities were resolved using exact raw STEP copies without changing caches.

Targeted measurement produced 81 successful native responses and 117 passing numeric checks. All 40 spoke webs exceed 2.4 mm, with a minimum of 2.600805465 mm. The nominal 14 mm sightline is clear against all 44 original solids at 0.000001 mm³ tolerance. All eight gear forms have five windows and approximately 3.25 mm root rims. All 174 measured tooth arcs show approximately 0.18 mm per-flank thinning, within 0.15–0.25 mm. This thinning was measured on the specified nominal pitch circles; it does not independently establish pitch diameter.

Current geometry establishes a 4 mm orange-to-spoke gap, 6 mm bezel radial width, 5 mm gear faces, 0.35 mm radial journal clearance and 0.3 mm face standoffs. Knurl grooves are 0.65 mm wide and 0.30 mm deep; raised lands have approximately 1.18258 mm arc width. Groove and land widths exceed the nominal 0.4 mm nozzle width. The depth is reported separately and is not claimed to exceed 0.4 mm. The coupon’s post spacing measures 21.75 mm, matching its production 14/15 gear pair.

Continuous bounds from measured centers and tooth-tip radii, including stated journal play, give a swept diameter of 189.898236 mm, overall height of 214.949118 mm, pedestal clearance of 11.050882 mm and nonneighbor planet gap of 3.194162 mm. Actual STEP bounds establish 19 mm axial separation from the stem throughout rotation about the Y axis. The separate sun-to-nonneighbor gap is not claimed to meet the 2 mm wrap-clearance requirement. Ideal digital balance is established for the 32 moving solids and separately for their black and gold material groups; physical balance is untested.

The 2e-5 mm tolerance used to reconcile rounded native coordinates does not replace or relax the unchanged 1e-8 mm source pitch-distance assertion. Per-part wall/overhang reports pass for all 21 roles. All 49 delivery STEP files and their raw native packages are hash-audited, with 98 passing refs/validation responses. All master print-pose bounds fit a 220 × 220 mm bed. These findings do not establish physical print readiness.

Formal motion remains **UNVERIFIED**: current round 4 returned exit 3, reported missing or invalid motion condition evidence and supplied no condition results. No precise failure cause is inferred. All 238 fixed-center contact samples passed across the fourteen gear pairs (seventeen samples per pair). The maximum measured gap was 3.331498934e-7 mm and the maximum overlap volume was 1.453713623e-6 mm³, within the unchanged 1e-6 mm and 0.001 mm³ thresholds. This is discrete contact evidence, not a continuous or complete coupled-motion pass. Current exact stills and the official 48-state animation are complete. Independent round-2 review passed the modeled form and presented action. This does not establish physical performance or complete numerical motion. The animation establishes declared presentation states, not a mechanical or physical motion pass. Earlier round-3 results, old animation and the historical exit-137 attempt are preserved as history, not current passes.

This is an unprinted prototype, **not print-ready**. Physical fit, friction, coasting, balance, durability, translucency and printer-specific performance remain untested. These disclosures do not introduce a new blanket physical-testing gate. Integrated verification and final status remain the Manager’s and verifier’s responsibility.
