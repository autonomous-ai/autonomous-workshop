# Packaged current numeric evidence

This folder preserves exact copies of completed numeric evidence. `packaging-manifest.json` maps every copy to its original workspace-relative path, SHA-256 and scope. Reports retain their exact bytes and historical wording; packaging has not upgraded any result.

Start with `geometry/summary.json`, `dimensions/SUMMARY.json`, `feature-probes/web-summary.json`, `final-features/SUMMARY.json` and `production/inspection/summary.json`. Native requests/results and execution/hash bindings are included beside them. Canonical print-report bindings are in `geometry/canonical-print-report-bindings.json`; the reports themselves remain in the product's measure directory.

`refs-supersession.json` marks seven stale refs rows in `geometry/results.jsonl`: carrier, coupon, gears18/20/22/26 and assembly. Use the named raw-component/raw-assembly replacements. Separate original validation/interference results remain preserved; stale refs are not current facts.

Preparation manifests may still say PREPARED or PENDING; earlier prose may describe subsequently completed inspection as pending. Those are exact historical snapshots. The later `production/inspection/summary.json` records98requests and49delivery files passing its scoped raw-file checks. `final-features/production-audit.json` reconciles later receipt annotations in product manifests with the exact pre-annotation manifest hashes used for inspection; delivery bytes and identities still match. Do not extend any PASS beyond its scope.

Formal motion remains UNVERIFIED in the retained current static report. Contact and presentation evidence are excluded and remain for the Manager to package separately. No print-ready, physical fit, printing, balance, coasting or durability claim is introduced.

Excluded: caches/meshes, STEP copies/witness solids, generator/measurement helpers, progress logs, contact and presentation. Their hashes/original paths may remain inside copied provenance. This is a self-contained record of numeric results and identity bindings, not an executable reproduction bundle or duplicate CAD archive.
