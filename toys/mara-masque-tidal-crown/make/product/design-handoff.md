## Frozen rules and evidence

Exactly standard chess, with the [FIDE Laws effective January 2023](https://handbook.fide.com/chapter/e012023) as normative rules reference. Original physical expression and explanatory prose only. Two players, 8×8 alternating field, orthodox initial position, White first, alternating legal moves, no randomness. Preserve each role's movement, obstruction, capture, king safety, castling rights/conditions, en passant timing, promotion to Q/R/B/N without stock restrictions, checkmate, resignation, stalemate, dead positions, agreed draws, threefold/50-move claims and automatic fivefold/75-move draws. Standard scoring and competitive clock/touch-move procedures remain unchanged when applicable; a clock and recording materials are optional external accessories, not new printed components. No local scenario or extra score. This compact ledger incorporates the normative rules rather than attempting a replacement rulebook. Every source role, square and action has a one-to-one counterpart; zero intentional rules differences.

The [US Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-games.html) excludes game ideas and playing methods from copyright while distinguishing protected literary/pictorial expression. This supports implementing standard chess mechanics independently; it does not license FIDE prose, commercial sculptures, artwork, marks or layouts. No such expression is copied. This is the documented public-domain mechanics basis, not a claim that all chess publications are public domain.

Independent recognition: [UN World Chess Day](https://www.un.org/en/node/78933) reports regular worldwide play on the scale of hundreds of millions (retrieved through indexed page after direct open failed). The grid is an abstract encounter, not a model of literal orbital paths. Fiction never determines legality.

## Construction: board

One solid board, bounding box 196×196×12 mm; bottom Z0, common playing support plane Z12. Field is exactly [-72,72]² with 18 mm cells and centers -63,-45,-27,-9,9,27,45,63. File a is X=-63, h X=63; rank1 Y=-63, rank8 Y=63. h1 is a light square. Central 152×152 support square with only small 2 mm corner rounding, so every 144 mm grid corner remains supported. A Ø196 circle alone cannot hold this field (field diagonal 203.65 mm), so do not cut the corners to a disk.

Form two broad, point-symmetric curled arms outside the grid: use a 12 mm-wide rounded planar ribbon along center points (74,-66),(86,-44),(92,-14),(88,20),(76,52),(53,77),(22,90),(-10,92), then the 180-degree counterpart. Interpolate smooth tangent arcs/spline, cap the terminal ends with R6; outer extrema ±98 set the final 196 mm envelope. Fuse overlapping ribbons into the 152 mm core through their full 12 mm depth. The 14–20 mm gaps between core and exterior curl in exposed regions provide a readable spiral silhouette. Do not add disconnected decorations or needle tips. Root may smooth the explicit centerline while preserving width, envelope, two curled-arm silhouette and grid clearance.

Alternation without color: on dark cells subtract a 2 mm-deep, 2 mm-wide square perimeter recess, leaving a centered 14×14 plateau at Z12 and a 10 mm minimum board substrate. Light cells stay flat at Z12. Thus every piece rests on the same plane; rigid flat bases span the 2 mm recess during sliding. Recesses are 2 mm features, not 0.2 mm graphics. Dark cell ring extends from local ±7 to ±9 in X/Y. Adjacent dark rings meet diagonally at grid intersections; make the complete recess union before boolean subtraction to avoid coincident seams. Continuous flat h-file travel is not required; flat base spanning 2 mm slots preserves the finger-slide action. The physical slide still requires fabrication verification before claiming friction or comfort. Optional two-color rendering may aid presentation but cannot replace relief.

## Construction: six role solids

Build every man upright on its own base. Use tapered stem transitions (radius growth per vertical rise ≤1) so no expanding feature begins at an unsupported horizontal ledge. Heads should be extruded thick silhouettes or supported lofts; no suspended rings, separate balls or bridges. All narrow positive limbs and open slots are ≥2 mm. Numerical starting profiles below are fully specified; small blends are allowed only if they preserve minimum thickness and source recognition. Heights include base.

- Pawn 12 mm: base Z0–3, central stem Ø7 from Z3–6, tapered expansion Ø7→10 from Z6–8, upper round knot Ø10 from Z8–10 tapering to Ø6 at Z12. This faceted rounded knot avoids the unsupported bottom of a sphere. Round the top using an inward fillet up to R1 if valid.
- Rook 18 mm: base Z0–3, square bar stem 8×8 Z3–13, top expands to 10×8 from Z13–14 using a supported loft; solid battlement block Z14–18, with central 4 mm-wide notch from Z16 to Z18 through its 8 mm depth, leaving two 3 mm-wide merlons. No unsupported lip; broad squared crown remains recognizably rook.
- Knight 21 mm: base Z0–3 and 8 mm-wide support to Z6; extrude a 5 mm-thick XZ side profile centered Y0. Closed polygon points in (X,Z): (-4,5),(3,5),(2,10),(5,14),(5,17),(2,19),(2,21),(-1,21),(-4,17),(-4,13),(-2,10). Bevel the lower nose slope so outward growth never exceeds 1 mm per 1 mm rise (the points already do). Keep two blunt upper ear corners at least 2 mm apart; no fine ears or eyes. The head projects +X and is mirrored in placement to face each opponent. Rear hook is the tidal tail; profile must read as neck/head from both player sides rather than an abstract token.
- Bishop 23 mm: base Z0–3, stem Ø8 Z3–10, upright lens silhouette extruded 5 mm in Y. XZ polygon (-3,9),(3,9),(5,15),(4,19),(1,23),(-1,23),(-4,19),(-5,15). Cut a 2 mm-wide diagonal open slot entering upper right edge and terminating at Z16; retain ≥2.5 mm solid on both sides and below slot. Slot axis from (X=1,Z=16) toward (X=5,Z=22), through the Y thickness, open to the outer upper edge. Do not cut the head into disconnected halves.
- Queen 26 mm: base Z0–3, stem Ø8 Z3–16, taper Ø8→10 from Z16–18. Solid crown disk Ø10 Z18–21 supports two opposed curved crown arms, each ≥2.5 mm radial thickness with no undercut; model 90-degree planar annular sectors at radii 2.5–5, one spanning 0–90 degrees and the other 180–270, extruded Z20–24, plus broad terminal rising lobes Z24–26 contained directly over the sector. Add central Ø4 upright crown lobe Z20–25. The two curved arms must read spiral rather than just two separated pegs; give one terminal per arm a continuous 2 mm tangential return toward the center at its supported top. Never use a free-floating helix. Three blunt crown points distinguish queen from king and rook. Root may replace return with a continuous supported spiral sector to improve visible curling while keeping all features ≥2 mm.
- King 28 mm: base Z0–3, stem Ø8 Z3–18, central core 6×6 Z18–23. Top cross is 3 mm thick in Y. Stem 3 mm wide X, Z22–28; horizontal bar width9, height3 Z23–26. Support bar ends with 45-degree rising haunches from width3 at Z20 to width9 at Z23, so the cross has no unsupported lower arms. The horizontal ends and central pole retain a visible cross silhouette; paired horizontal jets are the astronomical cue. All bar tips blunt and ≥3 mm.

Make geometry may revise submillimeter transitions or silhouette vertices to satisfy measured printability, but must preserve each stated semantic cue, 12–28 mm heights, base/board envelope and minimum features. Those are implementation repairs, not unresolved design choices.

## Final implementation amendment

Independent visual review rejected the first queen crown's concentric battlements. The delivered crown instead has two open radial spiral arms, each sweeping 120 degrees from radius 2.2 to 4.9 mm, on the same broad cup. It retains the 26 mm height and original side-specific base. The former three-lobed crown styling is superseded; the immutable requested spiral-arm form is preserved. Exact STEP section clearance is 2.072 mm. The final parametric source and CAD brief govern all implementation refinements recorded in the design-change history.
