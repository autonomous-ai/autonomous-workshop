# Prior-design comparison, accessed 2026-09-07

This is bounded design research, not a worldwide novelty search. No existing game's rule text, graphics or CAD is incorporated.

- Ravensburger, Labyrinth official English rules, https://www.ravensburger.org/spielanleitungen/ecm/Spielanleitungen/Labyrinth_Labyrinth_26032_GB.pdf?ossl=pds_text_Spielanleitung . Publisher search extract describes pushing an extra maze tile to change corridors before movement. Shared changing geography is established prior art. Our game must distinguish its collective simultaneous drive and contested rewards, not claim that moving boards are new.
- Czech Games Edition, SETI official English rules, https://czechgames.com/files/rules/seti-rules-en.pdf , p16; official product https://www.czechgames.com/games/seti-search-for-extraterrestrial-intelligence . Publisher-indexed rules describe rotating layered discs, with probes carried or bumped by cutouts; research and passing trigger rotation. This is the closest physical precedent. Our proposed rings cannot establish novelty alone: the difference must lie in players simultaneously choosing a boat and an exhaustible shared-movement contribution, then scoring the resulting harbor, while the resulting transport benefits opponents and landing congestion changes reward. Direct PDF open failed in browser, but the publisher PDF search index returned p16 text and the publisher product independently confirms rotating discs. No claim about the entire SETI rule system is made.
- Blue Orange, Photosynthesis official rules (2017 copyright), https://blueorangegames.eu/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/PHOTOSYNTHESIS_rules_UK.pdf , pp2–4. The sun advances on a fixed schedule and shadows govern light income; players spend light to grow trees. A changing common environment affecting everyone's rewards is established. Our players directly choose the collective environment shift and whether to exploit it, rather than growing a resource engine under a scheduled sun.

Source rights: publisher rules remain copyrighted; these short descriptions are our paraphrases for comparison, not adaptations or copied assets. Final design differences must be reconciled to the frozen implemented rules. Digital simulations can check rules and policy weaknesses, never prove human enjoyment or perceived novelty.
