# Rendered legal round

The 16 exact CAD meshes show a five-player round from the assembly demonstration arrangement. The selected directions are player 1 boat B +1, player 2 boat B +1, player 3 boat A +1, player 4 boat A -1, and player 5 boat A +1. Thus the inner carrier moves one harbor clockwise and the outer carrier two. All passengers move with their carrier.

Frames 00–05 show that turn. At frame 05, the selected boats score: player 1 B, player 3 A, and player 4 A share harbor 6 across both rings and each receive one of its three points; player 2 B at harbor 2 earns four; player 5 A at harbor 1 earns two. The final awards are therefore [1,4,1,1,2]. Unselected passengers do not dilute scoring.

Frames 06–15 depict hand transfers after scoring: selected boats lift, move to the other carrier one harbor clockwise, and seat again. Player 1 B and player 3 A end stacked on inner harbor 1; player 2 B ends on outer harbor 3; player 4 A ends on outer harbor 1; player 5 A ends on outer harbor 2. Unselected boats stay at their scoring locations. Displayed simultaneous lifts and straight travel visualize the simultaneous rules result, not a self-actuated mechanism or a required collision-free hand trajectory. In use, players move the boats individually from a remembered snapshot as taught in the rules.
