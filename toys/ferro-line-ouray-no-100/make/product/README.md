# SRR No. 100 Ouray — 1:87 printable CAD

Thirty named one-shell parts follow the sealed prototype seams. `model.py`
imports the preserved early proof directly for wheel, gauge, axle-station, and
hook/bar parameters. `measure/check_fit.py` checks the 30-part inventory,
single-shell output, bed datum, 200 mm bed bound, 3.0/3.4 mm axle journal pair,
and 2.0/2.4 mm fleet coupling pair. Assembly order is locomotive rolling gear,
locomotive fittings, tender trucks and keepers, tender frame/tank, then the two
transverse coupling bars. CAD checks are digital only; physical fit, rolling,
strength, and surface finish remain unclaimed.
