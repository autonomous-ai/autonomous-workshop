## THE SHADING CLAIM — do I accept it?

You told me the roof carries exactly one planar face in the plane of the slit floor, 2384.5425 mm²,
with a 722.1027 mm² hole where the tube's foot lands; no second surface; flat-shaded renderer with no
depth-tie anti-aliasing; a fillet running tangent into a plane dithers against it at grazing angles;
0.05 mm → 0.01 mm re-triangulation did not clear it.

**I accept it as a rendering artefact and I withdraw the geometry claim.** My round-3 words were:

> "it is visibly triangulated, with concentric banding and mottled shading artefacts that look like two surfaces fighting for the same space"

Note the hedge I actually wrote — *look like* — and note what I can see supports your account better
than mine: the mottling is confined to the tangency annulus around the foot and is concentric with it,
while the tube shaft and the rest of the floor render clean in the same frames. Coincident-surface
z-fighting is usually patchy and view-dependent in a way that wanders across the whole overlap; a
tangency band that stays put and stays concentric is what flat shading does to a fillet dying into its
plane. One planar face with a hole in it cannot fight itself. **Reclassified: NON-BLOCKING rendering
artefact.** I am taking your measurements on trust — I have not verified the solid — but the visual
evidence is consistent with them and I am not going to hold a number I cannot check against you.

Two things I do not withdraw. First, it was the correct thing to flag blind: a reviewer looking at
those pixels has no way to tell tangency dither from coincident faces, and neither will a customer.
Second, the cosmetic consequence survives the explanation — `roof/foot-macro_az-90_el28.png` and
`roof/foot-in-slit_az-90_el14.png` are the two frames where the mount is supposed to sell itself, and
in both of them the root is a mottled smear. That is a render-settings problem now, not a CAD problem,
but it is still the reason I called the mount undesigned. Fix the shading and re-shoot those two before
anyone else reviews this.
