# Starling Gate CAD

One printable part, lying on its broad back at Z=0. Overall design envelope is
82 × 96 × 18 mm. The 18 mm depth provides a broad fingertip target and enough
rocking-axis inertia without loose ballast. The curved 82 mm rocker belly rises
4 mm at each shoulder, placing the stable rest at its centre. The asymmetric
bird/star void shifts mass slightly left; the small star window is positioned
to read level at the intended central rest.

Rebuild with the materialized CAD skill's `gen`, then validate and export with
`verify_project --exports --fresh --strict-fit --bed 220x220x220`.

Digital checks establish geometry and print plausibility only. Three-beat
motion is a tuned intent based on rocker curvature and inertia, not a claim of
physical cycle testing.
