# Physical test plan — not performed

This is a digital prototype. No printed fit, holding torque, snap force, durability or support-free result has been physically established. Geometry checks cannot establish those results.

Print the supplied joint/magnet coupon first with the intended filament, a 0.4 mm nozzle and 0.2 mm layers. Keep supports off and use its stated orientation. Inspect for fused slots, drooping internal surfaces and cracks before trying to snap it together. Do not force a joint that whitens or cracks.

Test the actual 8 x 1 mm magnets dry in the 8.15 mm diameter, 1.2 mm deep pockets. A seated magnet should be approximately 0.2 mm below the mating surface before the glue bondline is considered. Confirm opposite exposed poles on the head and every matching face plate. Verify all three locations before bonding; the rim aligns the plate, it should not need a friction press fit.

Measure articulation and holding force on the final printed material. Check each ball joint separately for the intended bend and twist, then test the complete body under its own weight. Leave it posed for one hour and record angle drift. Repeat face exchanges and body posing at least 50 times, then inspect the socket fingers and magnet bonds. Record outcomes and actual slicer settings; this document contains no passing test result.

Small parts and loose magnets require controlled adult assembly. Secure each magnet permanently with an adhesive compatible with the chosen plastic; do not use the toy if a magnet becomes loose.
