# Pearlturn CAD brief

- Model: two-piece loose-roller kinetic toy; one shell and one pearl drum.
- Units: millimetres.
- Coordinates: X spans the 92 mm clam width, Y spans the 30 mm depth, Z is up in the low pose.
- Shell: 92 W × 30 D × 42 H nominal, a rigid open C-form with broad lower and upper lips joined by a rear cheek/web.
- Pearl: 24 mm diameter × 22 mm axial length, centered within the mouth with 2 mm side clearance to each depth face.
- Functional geometry: two open terminal saddles cut at 12.7 mm radius, a continuously open mouth, broad press ridge, flat low landing face, and a second exact pose at 65 degrees.
- Printed parts: shell and pearl only; no bought parts, connectors, axles, or hidden ballast.
- Print stance: shell on its broad Y-normal side; pearl on a circular end. No enclosed support-dependent cavities.
- Validation: exact two-solid combined STEP; per-part STEP/STL; 0.4 mm print preflight; exact 0° and 65° state STLs/renders; minimum dimensions and clearance ledger.
- Honest limitation: CAD cannot prove real-world friction, balance, tactile fit, cycle reliability, or that a physical print vaults successfully.

Dimensions are Wish-observed where stated (92 width, 30 depth, 65° state change, two pieces) and otherwise conservative Make assumptions.
