# Rainward Lowflow

A Sun-shaped backgammon set with flowing plasma counters, broad shallow playing strips and an open low capture circle. This separate correction preserves Rainward Flow's rules, components and 190 × 190 mm board outline.

The set contains one Sun board, fifteen single-tail counters, fifteen split-tail counters, two dice and two open dice cups. Each lane holds fifteen counters as five radial positions with three aligned layers. Keep tails toward the center and place each counter flat on its broad lane. The short rounded markers sit in the four wider gaps, separating groups of six; they are not playing positions. The center is the capture bar.

Read [RULES.md](RULES.md) for the unchanged historical rules, setup and orientation. The rules document retains its historical title to preserve its exact text. This edition adds no rule, power, component or endpoint dot.

The Sun's center is 17 mm above the table, down from 28 mm; lane tops are 16.8 mm above the table. Gaps have a 16 mm floor. Most gaps are 0.8 mm wide, while four group boundaries are 1.6 mm wide and contain short rounded markers. All board relief uses one material color. Dice retain their modeled recessed 1–6 pips and contrasting dark recess treatment. Fill those recesses with dark paint if that appearance is wanted in a physical copy; no pip positioning is required.

Counters remain 12 × 8 × 4 mm with matching flat tops and bottoms. Their curved tails can slightly overhang a lane edge or the board perimeter. The current contact report measures this explicitly; full footprint contact is not claimed. Keep stack layers aligned. The CAD checks do not establish physical handling, printed fit, real stack stability, durability or statistical dice fairness. No physical tests have been run.

Each separate production solid is under `parts/`. The editable source, per-component STEP files, canonical images and current measurements are under `cad/`. `assembled.step` is the complete displayed set; print the separate components rather than this layout. The 190 mm board requires a bed with suitable margin. A 200 × 200 × 200 mm bed and 0.4 mm nozzle are the declared digital verification conditions. Actual material, printer calibration and surface finish still require practical evaluation.
