# Form and beauty review

Review geometry at the same fixed views and scale. Keep the target/reference
and generated CAD renders separately hashed.

Score with written evidence:

- silhouette clarity at thumbnail size;
- proportion rhythm among primary, secondary, and detail forms;
- landmark completeness and placement;
- intentional negative space and visual weight;
- continuity/tangency where forms blend;
- restraint and consistency of fillets, chamfers, ribs, and texture;
- seam and connector placement relative to the visual hierarchy;
- ergonomic affordances: grip, reach, visibility, orientation, and feedback;
- material/color breaks that follow construction rather than hide it;
- coherence across front, side, top, three-quarter, and assembly views.

Flag likely render tricks: geometry visible in only one view, impossible
contacts, coplanar overlays, paper-thin shells, hidden disconnected pieces,
texture standing in for form, and decorative detail that harms printing or use.

Do not collapse the review into one scalar. Record blockers and the smallest
geometry change that could address each one. A separate engineering reviewer
decides whether a proposed aesthetic repair preserves fit and manufacture.
