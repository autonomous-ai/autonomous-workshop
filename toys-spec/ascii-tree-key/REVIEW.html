Where the build will differ from the pictures

  • The screen is flush with the frame, not sunk. A 4 mm plate has room for the tag pocket, the cream layer and one top layer, and no step.
  • Each asterisk's inside corners are rounded by 0.5 mm so the black between the spokes prints cleanly. At this size you will barely see it.
  • The rows sit slightly closer together (6.4 mm) than the asterisks within a row (6.8 mm), as the picture draws them.
  • The separate base picture draws the bit about 1 mm higher, and the cream-layer picture has slightly rounder corners. The build follows the whole-key picture.
  • Six colours need a printer with two AMS units. The print pauses once to drop in the NFC tag.
