# Key: new shape concepts

Concept images only, for picking a direction; no contract yet.

The current Autonomous Key (autonomous.ai/key) is a 79 x 38 x 2.5 mm PLA plate
shaped like a key, with a passive NFC tag sealed inside. Its only job is to be
tapped by a phone, which unlocks the apps it blocks for up to 60 minutes. It
lives on a keyring or somewhere away from the phone.

What the new shape must do:

- **Be easy to tap.** The NFC antenna needs a clear area of about 25 mm or more;
  smaller tags read poorly on many phones. Every concept keeps one round area
  of 26–30 mm as its tap target and marks it.
- **Be small and light.** About a third to half of today's plate area, 2.5 mm
  thick (K6 is 4 mm at its centre), roughly 2–4 g of PLA.
- **Hang on a keyring** without a separate attachment.
