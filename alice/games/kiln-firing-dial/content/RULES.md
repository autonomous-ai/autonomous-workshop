# KILN: the Firing Dial — rules

**2 players · 20 minutes** · an original 3D-printable board game.

Two potters share one square kiln. A printed firing dial holds sixteen bands
in a secret order; each turn you turn it, obey the band, and glaze one well.
But only you know the two glaze rituals you are secretly completing — so the
same open board hides a different game for each player.

## In the box

- 1 shared **kiln tray** — a 4×4 grid of sixteen glaze wells.
- 1 shared **firing dial** — a rotating wheel with sixteen band pips and one pointer.
- 16 **glaze discs** — 8 in each player's colour.
- 2 **ritual screens** — one per player.
- 6 **objective tiles**.

## Setup

1. Place the tray between you, the dial at its corner.
2. Shuffle the sixteen firing bands behind a screen, then load them onto the
   dial in that order. **The order is now public** — both players see every
   revealed band as the game goes.
3. Each player takes 8 discs of their colour, one screen, and draws **two
   distinct objective tiles** from the six, hiding them behind their screen.
4. Choose the first player at random.

## On your turn

Turn the dial forward one band and read it aloud, then place exactly **one**
of your discs in an empty well under that band's constraint.

**The firing bands**

| Band | You must glaze |
|------|----------------|
| OPEN | any empty well |
| SHY | a well **not** orthogonally adjacent to a rival disc |
| REGION | a well orthogonally adjacent to **one of your own** discs |
| FAR | a well **not** orthogonally adjacent to any of your own discs |

If a band has no legal well for you, glaze any empty well instead. A disc,
once placed, never moves. There is no pass. After sixteen placements the tray
is full — eight discs each, by construction.

## The objective tiles

You are completing two of these shapes. Each is worth its bonus **only if it
is fully satisfied on the finished tray** — all its wells are yours:

- **CORNERS** (8 pts) — own all four corner wells.
- **CENTER** (6 pts) — own the four centre wells.
- **BLOCK** (7 pts) — own a full 2×2 block.
- **ROW3** (5 pts) — own three consecutive wells in a row.
- **COL3** (5 pts) — own three consecutive wells in a column.
- **EDGE3** (4 pts) — own at least three wells on the outer rim.

## Scoring and winning

1. Score your **regions**: for each maximal group of orthogonally connected
   own-colour discs, add size × size.
2. Reveal your two objective tiles and add each satisfied bonus.
3. Highest total wins. **Ties** go first to the higher region-only score
   (objectives removed); if still tied, the win is shared.
