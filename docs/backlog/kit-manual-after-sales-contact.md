# Kit Manual after-sales contact

Status: backlog (owner, 2026-10-29). Deciding the contact will take a while.

The Kit Manual that Make generates for a kit (ADR 0092) has an after-sales
contact section. It is filled from `make-round/company.json`, which still holds
a placeholder. The Night Charms cabinet pilot
(`wish-20261028-051153-3f1061cd`) shipped its 6-page Manual with that
placeholder.

To do once the owner decides:

- the contact channel or channels (email, web form, support page);
- the wording and language;
- any warranty or replacement-part promise the Manual may make.

Then set it in `make-round/company.json` and regenerate the Manual of any kit
due to be published. Until then, no kit Manual should be published with the
placeholder.
