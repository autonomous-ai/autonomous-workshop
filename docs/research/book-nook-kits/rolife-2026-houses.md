## 1. Materials
No manual states a total part count; parts show IDs and quantities only.
- DH201 (bags 1–5): pre-printed laser-cut boards, sheets DH201-A..T, parts like B17, G10, P42 (DH201-03..05). Paper sheets K, L (leaves, flowers), M (labels, paintings, menu, boxes, bags), N (stone and brick wraps). Other parts: metal/brass lamp shells T18–T30, bell, cup T16; ceramic cups T44; glass/plastic jars T50–T54; wires T11 (green florist), T12 (black), T27 (silver), black tube T57; floral fabric T40, crepe/raffia T34, foam flock T45, lavender flock T56; 3-D plastic wire-mesh chairs (DH201-07, -24..27, -44). Electrics: LED leads T2, battery box T3, bulbs T4 (DH201-36..39).
- DH102: boards A..K; acrylic boxes U3/U4/U6 with peel film; white plastic greenhouse frames U35–U41; 1 and 2 mm tubes U33/U34; LED strip U11; paper sheets S, L, M, N, P, R (DH102-03..05, -19..21, -28).
- DG186: boards A..G printed black, orange-brown or full colour; paper K/M/N (shelf liners, signs, candy wrappers); glow-in-the-dark resin pumpkin, candy, ghost (DG186-03); plastic candy, beads, basket P30–P64; USB-C driver board P16, LED string P17.
- TGB19 (bags S-1..S-4): battery box P1, touch switch, LED beads P2/P3, foam-block grid P29 (blocks Y1–Y5); prints V, G, T, M, H (TGB19-01, -14).

## 2. Colour and surface detail
- Colour from the material: factory-printed board faces (red cabinets, blue coffee machine, wood grain, shingles: DH201-04, -16; black/orange shelves: DG186-04). A green "printed side" icon shows the outward face.
- Large textures are paper wraps over plain boards: stone/brick N8–N20 (DH201-12, -17, -35); shaped shelf liners (DG186-05); floral fabric on a cabinet front (DH201-15).
- Small graphics are cut-out paper prints, sometimes layered (M29 "Coffee" over wooden letter T10, DH201-47..50).
- Glass, ceramic and metal props come pre-coloured. Nothing is painted; the only colouring is darkening laser-cut edges with a marker (U29, DH102-10).

## 3. Books (TGB19)
- Single books: blank foam block Y1–Y5 (grid P29) wrapped in a printed paper cover (V10/V11/V25/V24/V30–V34), four drawn folds (TGB19-14..17).
- Display boxes, not shelf rows: H24 is a flat printed lid showing a box of colour pencils, taped onto box S18; H25 ×2 are smaller pencil sets on S/2 bases; S22/S23 are printed blocks showing trays of pens, stacked with tape. All sit on the sloped display L34/L35 (TGB19-13, -14). No printed strip of book spines was found (corrected 2026-10-26).
- Stacks: C4 blocks wrapped with V13/V14 (TGB19-20).
- Scrolls: paper rolled round a short rod (V37–V40 on L17, V35 on L12) (TGB19-11, -17). Booklets: folded paper G1–G5 (TGB19-11).
- No open books; nearest is a flat printed newspaper or menu M18/M36 on a table (DH201-05, -12).

## 5. Structure
- Plywood tab-and-slot, and every joint glued (glue icon on nearly every panel, DH201-03). Double-sided tape for paper and small parts, S/M/L sizes pre-cut on one sheet (DG186-04). (Contrast: the TGB18/TGE05 book nooks are glue-free, see ANALYSIS-BOOKNOOK.md.)
- Furniture: 3–12 flat parts, a box or two-sided bracket (DH102-03, DG186-04).
- Shell: floor board with printed stone-floor paper; walls with paper wrap; walls slotted onto floor; columns and parapets (DH201-35..37).
- Order: every prop and furniture item first, each its own named section; grouped as "Parts A–I" and placed into the finished shell (DH201-31, -37..43); then the overhead light framework, roof, plants and vines (DH201-39..52).
- Part IDs: sheet + number; material-list box shows bag 4 with part P63 (DH201-02).

## 6. Doors, drawers, windows
- Working hinges: silver wire T27 through a black tube sleeve T57 (14–18 mm) glued to the door edge, a pin hinge (DH201-04/05 large cabinet; DH201-12 table lid).
- Other doors glued shut with brass knobs T28/M43 (DH201-17); DH102 double doors fixed, plastic handles U19 (DH102-05..08).
- Windows: clear film behind cut frames (DH102-04/05). Drawers drawn but fixed.

## 7. Lighting
- DH201: 2×AAA box with slide switch T3 hidden at the back, "wrap the wire twice" strain relief; LED leads T2 and bulbs T4 in red pendant shades T10; wires in grooves of the overhead framework; troubleshooting box for switch, polarity, wiring (DH201-39, -43).
- DH102: 12 cm LED strip in a white channel U41; wire inside tubes U33/U34 along frame legs (DH102-19..22).
- DG186: USB-C board P16 (5 V 500 mA), one-way JST plug with orientation tick; fairy-string LEDs through lantern shells; light test before assembly (DG186-03, -28).
- TGB19: battery box with touch switch, two bead leads; tested first, kept bagged for later (TGB19-02).

## 8. Manual layout
- Front matter: warnings, tools (included and self-supplied), icon legend, assembly tips, glue notes, how to read the part-number paper and list (DH201-02/03).
- Each section: orange tab naming the sub-assembly with a finished picture, and a three-column Material list box: Board (bag badge, sheet DH201-B:, silhouettes with ID bubbles), Paper-cutting (yellow sheet tag), Other parts.
- Steps: orange numbers; » arrows between sub-steps; green 1/2 pins for order. Red dashed callouts for details or 1:1 templates; red *n quantity; red ! attention.
- Icons: glue, tape, perspective changed, printed side, peel film, wait to dry, tweezers, scissors, pliers. Page numbers in an orange circle; ends with a "Completion" photo (DH201-50).

## 9. Recommendations for Night Charms Hall (3D-printed, one filament colour per part)
- Print as single-colour parts: case shell and arch (navy); gallery, stair, desks, cabinet carcass (walnut); railing (brown or gold); lancet tracery (black); hat (purple); crystal ball (translucent); ink bottles (black); torch brackets (black); flames (translucent amber). Fewer, larger parts per colour, like Rolife's 3–12-part furniture.
- Supply as paper or stickers: inside stone wrap; night-sky sheet behind the lancets under clear film; one printed spine strip per alcove shelf row (a Workshop choice; no Rolife example found); open-book pages on a printed open-book base; portrait and notices; jar labels; floor and desk-top prints; loose and stacked books as plain printed blocks in 2–3 colours with sticker covers (TGB19 Y-block method).
- Bought parts: feathers or paper quills on wire, wand rods, a clear jar (like DH201 T50).
- Cabinet hinge: printed knuckles or a 1 mm wire pin through printed eyes (T27 + T57 method).
- Lighting: two torch LEDs on one lead to a USB-C board or 2×AAA switch box behind the case; printed wire groove along back wall and gallery; 1:1 wire-length template; light test first.
- Bags: 1 shell/arch/floor/walls; 2 gallery/stair/railing; 3 desks (6 sets); 4 teacher's desk and props; 5 cabinet and hinge; 6 electrics. IDs as plate letter + number (A1, B7) embossed on a hidden face, with a 1:1 part-ID sheet.
- Manual: front matter → light test → one section per prop or furniture item (Printed parts / Paper and sticker / Other parts box, then steps) → "Parts A–n" placement grid → shell → wiring → gallery and stair → torches and window → completion photo.
