# Rolife book nooks: TGB18 Sunset Isle and TGE05 Nostalgic Corner Shop (2026 manuals)

Tiles: `sources/manuals-2026/tiles-booknook/<kit>/<kit>-NN.png`, about two manual pages each. [TGB18-16] cites a tile. Neither manual states outer size, piece count or a full parts list; counts below come from the highest letter codes.

## 1. Overall form
- TGB18: tall shelf-insert slab with a pitched roof cap (D1/D2 printed tiles, J4/J5 gables) [TGB18-01, -19]. TGE05: wide cube shop, flat corrugated roof (R1 over D1) [TGE05-18].
- Shell: thin laser-cut wood, tab-and-slot, no glue; printed side/back panels; small wood clips (TGB18 F20, TGE05 E5/E6) hold wires [TGB18-08, -16; TGE05-17].
- TGB18 builds up from a hollow base box (C1 + E13–E16) that holds the battery [TGB18-04]; floor modules are sub-assemblies dropped in [TGB18-05]. Square outline held by full-height sides/back, front picture frame E18, pergola grid E11, front base board E17 [TGB18-15, -16].
- TGE05 order: floor and back wall → side walls → ceiling grid C1 → roof → facade [TGE05-13 to -19].
- Front is a framed opening, not an arch. TGB18: E18 frame, balustrade gate F5, "OPEN" sign L12 [TGB18-16]. TGE05: wood frame with sliding glazed doors, striped awning on wire brackets, flag, sign H9 [TGE05-19, -22, -23].
- Faked depth (TGB18) [TGB18-07 to -17]: foreground balcony; mid-ground reduced-scale hillside village of tiny printed wood houses and card trees [TGB18-10, -11]; sea sheets P26, P41 and reflective film P44 on K1 [TGB18-08, -14, -17]; printed sunset back sheet P45 [TGB18-17]; card clouds, birds, tree silhouettes Q5 between layers. No mirrors. TGE05 is a real room: depth from shelves and lattice ceiling.

## 2. Materials
- Wood boards A–K (+R in TGE05). TGB18 up to A7, B18, C4, D6, E28, F54, G16, H10, J17, K22; TGE05 up to A13, C18, D9, E33, F20, G10, H10, J27, K21.
- Card/paper: TGB18 L = printed card cut-outs (L1–L53), M = stickers (to M25); TGE05 J/K/N = folding card and paper (boxes, magazines, snack packs; N to N37).
- Film: TGE05 L = window films with peel liner [TGE05-18, -19]. Q sheets: diffuser/backing films (TGB18) [TGB18-18], adhesive spacers (TGE05) [TGE05-06].
- Bagged P-parts (blue badges; TGB18 to P46, TGE05 to P77): pre-coloured resin/plastic minis (cat, cushion, vases, flowers, cups, teapot, lucky cat, phone, fan, jars, bottles), snap plastic baskets, fabric square P18, polymer-clay canes P31–P39/P45, bead candies, clear bags + twist ties, twine P16, wire P30, stems P15, sticks P25, double-sided foam tape sheet P5, small screwdrivers [TGB18-02, -03, -19; TGE05-05 to -15].

## 3. Colour and surface detail
- Colour is printed, never painted: boards come pre-printed (floorboards, brick, grass, roof tiles, stucco, boarding) [TGB18-02, -05, -19; TGE05-03, -12].
- Green square symbol marks the blank (colourless) side of a board [TGB18-02].
- Stickers wrap 3D forms (M1 tablecloth round a table, M3 cap) [TGB18-02]; strips such as brick edging M19/M20 and signs go on flat [TGB18-13].
- Moulded minis come pre-coloured; no step calls for paint.

## 4. Books and props
- Books: wood block wrapped in a printed sticker (B16 + M7, B17 + M8) [TGB18-02]. Open book: accordion-folded paper strip M24 taped to a wood cover E4 [TGE05-12]. Newspapers/magazines: folded paper J15, J23 [TGE05-04].
- Signs/posters: sticker or card on thin wood backers (N20/N21 on E28) [TGE05-04].
- Product boxes: folded card K [TGE05-02, -21].
- Bagged candy: clear-film pouch cut to a 1:1 gauge (45 × 22 mm), beads inside, gold twist tie [TGE05-09].
- Plants: artificial sprigs cut to marked lengths into resin pots [TGE05-11]; wire stems with leaves [TGB18-03].
- Wire props cut against 1:1 rulers and bent on a wood jig (D8 "TOOL") [TGE05-12, -13, -22].

## 5. Lighting
- TGB18: 2×AAA battery box P17 with touch PCB in a slide-in base drawer; touch pad on the front base, three modes (all on / ceiling off / all off) [TGB18-19]. LED strip P18 up the side wall, lamp-post LED P25 under shade P24, P39 and P42 LEDs in the pergola ceiling [TGB18-08, -13, -15]; a strip in a groove on J17 backlights the sunset sheet P45 [TGB18-17]. Joints twisted and taped (P43) [TGB18-15, -18].
- TGE05: battery box + touch PCB P1 in a bottom side cabinet [TGE05-11, -17]; touch switch behind a facade-corner sticker (M19/M20) [TGE05-19, -23]; two pendant LEDs under shades P62/P63/P65 and one on a wire arm [TGE05-16, -17]; manual opens with a light test [TGE05-02].
- Both: wires along wall edges in wood clips. No USB.

## 6. Assembly order and conventions
- Big orange step numbers; green numbered pins for order within a step.
- Bag boxes with orange tabs ("TGB18 3-1/3-2/3-3", "TGE05 7-1…7-7") show P-parts at the step that uses them.
- Black dots name wood/paper parts, blue dots P-parts; sub-assemblies get lettered tags (blue squares TGB18, black squares TGE05).
- Symbols: S/M/L foam-tape pad sizes cut from P5, peel-film icon, rotate-view icon, red "!" alerts, 1:1 cut rulers, "keep in the box for later", correct/incorrect marks.
- Joining: press-fit plus foam tape; no glue in either kit [TGB18-02; TGE05-03].
- Order: furniture and props → modules → shell → lighting test → facade and roof.

## 7. Moving parts
- TGE05: glazed doors slide in wood rails E12, E23 [TGE05-19]. TGB18: battery box slides out of the base drawer [TGB18-19]. No hinges in either.

## 8. Differences and common ground
- TGB18: vertical, scenic balcony over a layered vista; mostly cut card before printed sheets; base box holds the battery.
- TGE05: horizontal room crowded with props; dozens of folded paper items, clay/bead/wire crafts, sliding doors.
- Common: pre-printed tab-and-slot wood, colour from print, foam tape instead of glue, bag-coded P-parts, 2×AAA touch-switch lighting, hidden wire runs, same manual layout.

## 9. Implications for the 3D-printed Night Charms Hall (140 × 170 × 230 mm)
- Keep printed (FDM, one colour per part): case shell, front arch, floors and gallery deck, stair, railing, desks, cabinet with hinged door, torch brackets and frames, each in its base colour.
- Move to printed paper/stickers: stone texture, floorboards, night sky behind the lancets, portrait, notices, book-spine faces. Alcove books as printed blocks or ribs with spine-sticker wraps (TGB18 B16 + M7); desk papers and open books as folded paper (TGE05 M24); a printed sky back sheet plus one layered silhouette sheet for depth; window glazing on film.
- Shell: five tab-and-slot panels with snap clips instead of glue, plus a printed front frame carrying the arch that locks them square; battery and switch box in a base plinth or roof cavity, touch pad on the front base; wire channels along back edges with printed clips.
- Lighting: two warm LEDs in the torches, optional backlight behind the sky sheet, the same 2×AAA touch module, a light-test page first.
- Manual: props → desks and cabinet → gallery and stair → shell → lighting → front frame; bag-coded part boxes; letters by material (printed A–K, paper/stickers M/N, bagged P); mark the sticker face; 1:1 tape and cut gauges; lettered sub-assemblies.
