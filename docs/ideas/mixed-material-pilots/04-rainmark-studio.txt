Rainmark Studio

Make a finished hand-cranked rhythm machine whose repeating mechanical program strikes a small set of wooden/metallic notes and stamps corresponding beat marks into a slowly rotating shallow sand disc. A large phase control changes the rhythm. The child hears the motif, inspects its circular score, sweeps the disc clean and changes the pattern. Design one coherent mechanical cause for both the audible and visible events, not two independent toys sharing a housing. If a proposed linkage cannot support both reliably, revise its implementation and explicitly record any remaining capability gap.

Use a CNC wood housing; printed cams, gears, disc and linkages; wooden hammers; sourced brass tubes or other explicitly specified metal resonators; formed-wire suspension; return springs; rubber drive surfaces/feet; felt acoustic damping; a specified sand/play medium; a silicone reset blade; a transparent plastic guard; paper experiment cards; hardware, paint and compatible adhesives. Keep this product mechanical. Tube cutting, wire forming, acoustic adjustment, sand selection and containment must have actionable internal fabrication/assembly instructions. Choose the medium by the needed marking and reset behavior, rather than assuming every sensory-sand formulation behaves alike.

Signature experience: changing one rhythm changes both what the child hears and the visible trace left by those same events. Repeat play consists of setting phase, predicting the pattern, cranking, comparing sound and marks, erasing and experimenting again. The marks should be a genuinely observable record, not merely decorative sand movement.

Internal human prototype checks: count missed/double strikes and unclear marks; compare note timing with sand marks through repeated patterns and phase settings. Measure crank effort, mark visibility and reset quality. Test containment, cleanability, resonator retention and separation of sand from the drivetrain. Do not substitute a generated animation or imagined soundtrack for evidence that the proposed mechanism behaves as described.
