# ADR 0047: Match evidence to motion and persist native execution budgets

- Status: Motion evidence implemented; clock policy superseded by ADR 0049
- Date: 2026-09-07
- Scope: new materialized product runs; frozen older instructions are unchanged

## Problem

Mooncalf passed printable preflight but exhausted its two blind reviews. A
calf/hippo styling mismatch and inability to infer reverse walking from still
images blocked it before final integrated verification. This was not proof of
a failed mechanism, nor proof of a working one. Four of its five native turns
lacked terminal usage, so aggregate tokens were only a measured floor. Repeated
explicit resumes of prior products also granted fresh command clocks.

## Decision

Keep the schema-v6 signature review and its one-critic/two-round limit. Evaluate
appearance from exact renders and operating motion from exact-state animation.
For a declared coupled mechanism, the deterministic `motion_presentation.py`
tool renders ordered state STLs with common framing and hashes the sources,
states, motion manifest and GIF. The same independent critic first observes
the animation without the desired answer, then compares it to the Wish and
current concept. A separate canonical motion-review record binds that read to
the evidence and the same critic, concept and review round. Final verification
and the Make finalizer reject missing/stale evidence before accepting it.
Assembly-only insertion/retention paths do not require operating animation.
The existing motion gate still tests the declared exact mechanical conditions.

This is provenance plus a native-agent judgment, not a deterministic proof of
physical dynamics or an image-recognition judge. An agent-authored pose table
alone is not evidence that a mechanism drives its output. Existing drive,
collision, retention, printability, isolated rebuild and publication gates
remain required. GIFs and simulation cannot claim physical testing.

In Spark, unsealed creative styling choices may be revised before the final
review, with prior choice and reason preserved in `research.design_changes`.
The Wish's constraints, function and distinctive experience remain mandatory.
The final concept and product must agree; no failed review may be rewritten
as passing. Forge/Quest sealed concepts retain their authorized revision path.
Exposed working geometry is evaluated for the actual product, not automatically
rejected as unfinished.

New Codex runs freeze `lifetime-budgets-v1.md`: 40 minutes of native execution per
stage and 60 minutes per product, across all resumes. The host reserves the
bounded launch allowance in private state before launch, then settles actual
elapsed time after a safe return/unwind. An uncatchable crash conservatively
leaves the reservation spent. Missing or malformed resume budget state fails
closed; status reports its availability and consumption. Explicit resume never
replenishes it. Existing `budgets-v1`-only runs and other adapters retain command clocks.

The budget is a native execution-time ceiling, not a dollar or token cap. Host
CAD verification, remote effects and stopped time are outside that allowance.
Usage without terminal events remains explicitly unmeasured rather than
estimated. This change does not claim complete billing observability.

Unknown nonzero native exits without terminal events now fail closed rather
than entering missing-terminal recovery. Only clean exits may use that
compatibility fallback. Explicitly recognized transport failures and valid
checkpoint-bound proposals retain their existing handling. A stale test
fixture exposed the former retry behavior by repeatedly failing its Make
finalizer instead of stopping on the process error.

## Acceptance

The canonical native Python executable also retains the host's single external
package directory when starting the isolated PDF checker. The worker keeps
`-I`, resource limits, sanitized environment and no working-directory, user-site
or `.pth` imports. This is dependency transport, not an acceptance shortcut:
the host independently revalidates the PDF in its own environment without the
override. Older materialized validators are unchanged.

- Deterministic tests cover stale source/state/animation/review, invalid or
  mismatched critic identity, static versus coupled motion, fixed framing,
  durable reservation, corrupt/missing state and no replenishment on resume.
- Legacy clock and recovery tests retain their original semantics.
- A small cam-driven product must independently complete Spark Make and
  Release through the normal CLI within its declared allowance before the
  revised workflow is described as live end-to-end validated.

### Builder validation, 2026-09-07

The maintained full-run and CLI subprocess suite passed 23 tests using fakes;
the installed-wheel CLI acceptance also passed. Focused motion/host, runtime,
budget, finalizer and Release checks passed, including canonical-interpreter PDF
dependency and isolation coverage. These are software checks, not evidence of
manufacture or live publication. The older opt-in deterministic-fidelity
fixture remains stale (missing current Make review artifacts); its attempted
run was stopped and is not reported as passing.

After explicit publication approval, the single Spark/Sol/high Soren cam trial
ran two native turns and stopped at its forty-minute Make allowance. Peekabud
produced five-part CAD and motion states but did not pass Make or publish.
Its mechanical verifier rejected an axle-removal collision; the attempted
retainer repair then hit a missing CAD-library constant before timeout. This
was not a successful end-to-end acceptance. ADR 0049 replaces normal time
budgets with persistent product-wide observed tokens. The user requested a
separate, deliberately static one-piece trial; Peekabud remains stopped.
