# Scenes of many repeated pieces

Read this file when the reference shows repeated, individually visible pieces —
a board and its men, a tray of parts, a rack, a tiled or populated surface.

Such a scene can match its archetype while missing most of what defines it,
and the single likeness number in Step 8 will not say so. Why, and how to
reproduce a layout: `skills/wiki/pages/image-reading/repeated-scene-likeness.md`
(`wiki show repeated-scene-likeness`).

**Multi-component scene landmark gate.** Before CAD starts:

- inventory the visible landmarks by category (frame tiers, region boundaries,
  site density and exclusion zones, piece count and distribution, one
  silhouette checklist per defining module, required accessories absent from
  the hero image), each with a count, placement rule or ratio where the image
  supports one;
- give the operation table a row for every landmark, and score the categories
  separately in the verification checklist; assign no single 90-95 % likeness
  number until every defining category has its own finding;
- for up to roughly 50 individually visible pieces, record observed normalised
  centroids or a traceable placement table; a procedural distribution only when
  the reference is procedural or the user asked for a new setup, recorded
  `[assumed]`;
- when pieces occupy legal sites, map each centroid to one unique valid site,
  enforce every exclusion zone, record the displacement or maximum snap
  distance, and run a local audit proving count, uniqueness, site membership
  and exclusion-zone clearance before geometry;
- give every defining landmark under roughly 15 % of the scene's bbox a local
  verification target.
