# Decided Early

## Definition
The result is fixed before the last round or the last session.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/catch-up-mechanism]], [[rule-patterns/back-loaded-scoring]]

## Notes
- Reduce positive feedback when an early advantage also improves a player's capacity to gain future advantages.
- If the state becomes irreversible by design, end the game promptly instead of requiring players to execute foregone rounds.
