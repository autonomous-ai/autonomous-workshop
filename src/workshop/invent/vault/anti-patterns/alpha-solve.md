# Alpha Solve

## Definition
One confident player solves the co-op out loud for everyone.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/private-information-boundaries]], [[rule-patterns/simultaneous-reveal]]

## Notes
- Alpha Solve is most likely when a shared puzzle has fully public information, unrestricted discussion, and actions that any one player can optimize for everyone.
- Simultaneous commitments reduce control by the dominant player only when players choose before seeing one another's decisions.
