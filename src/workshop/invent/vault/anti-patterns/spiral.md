# Spiral

## Definition
One loss makes the next loss likelier, with no floor.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/catch-up-mechanism]]

## Notes

- [yt:ZSVREGmO1Xw] medium: Co-op legacy design feared losses compounding into more losses (no leader-bashing brake like competitive games); a 'lost 4 in a row' safety-net envelope backstopped this psychologically. (GDC Festival of Gaming 2021)
