# Turtling

## Definition
Pure defence dominates: the player who refuses to engage wins by letting the aggressors bleed each other. Rational players converge on never attacking, and the conflict game contains no conflict.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/forced-engagement]], [[rule-patterns/diminishing-returns]]

## Notes
Attacker-pays-defender-profits cost structures cause it; make aggression profitable or contact mandatory.
- [yt:HXA00ZCzSZ4] low: In Scythe: Winning combat costs popularity and losing forfeits units built over many turns, so the dominant strategy is to claim a small area early and avoid further expansion or fights. (No Pun Included 2016)
