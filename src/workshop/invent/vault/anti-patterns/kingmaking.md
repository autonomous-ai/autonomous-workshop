# Kingmaking

## Definition
A player who can no longer win decides which of the others does — by targeted attack, trade, or gift. The finale is adjudicated by the loser's mood instead of the winners' play.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/hidden-scoring]], [[rule-patterns/simultaneous-reveal]]

## Notes
Worst in open-information games with direct attacks and exactly three players.
