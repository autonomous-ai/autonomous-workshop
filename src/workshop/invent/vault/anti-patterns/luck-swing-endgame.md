# Luck Swing Endgame

## Definition
A high-variance random event in the final round decides the winner, wiping out an hour of accumulated decisions. Players remember the die, not the game.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/hidden-scoring]]

## Notes
Front-load luck: variance early (players can adapt to it) is excitement; variance last (nobody can respond) is a lottery. Hidden scoring softens the sting by hiding the exact flip.
- [yt:av5Hf7uOu-o] medium: In long games, push randomness toward the start: a die roll deciding a 3-hour game late feels like it erased the skill just displayed, hurting immersion. (IGDA Denmark 2013)
