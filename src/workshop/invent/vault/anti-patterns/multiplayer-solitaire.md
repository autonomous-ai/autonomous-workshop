# Multiplayer Solitaire

## Definition
Players share a table but not a game: nothing anyone does changes what anyone else should do. Interaction is reduced to comparing scores at the end.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/forced-engagement]]

## Notes
Not always a defect (some audiences want it) — an anti-pattern when the box promises interaction the rules don't deliver.
- [yt:HXA00ZCzSZ4] low: In Scythe: Despite the war-game presentation, combat is rare enough that most turns are spent solely optimizing your own player board with little need to track opponents' plans. (No Pun Included 2016)
