# First Player Advantage

## Definition
The fixed first seat gets the best of every scarce thing each round, and the edge compounds. Statistically visible as seat-1 win rate well above 1/N.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/variable-turn-order]]

## Notes
Compensation payments (extra resource to late seats) are the crude fix; re-deriving order from state is the structural one.
