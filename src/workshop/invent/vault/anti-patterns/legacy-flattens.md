# Legacy Flattens

## Definition
The permanent change makes later sessions poorer for everyone.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/legal-action-floor]], [[rule-patterns/legacy-conservation]]

## Notes
- Additive change is not automatically safe, but repeated subtraction is especially likely to collapse strategic variety.
- Playtest late-campaign states reached through extreme but legal sequences of permanent changes.
