# Unsatisfying Action

## Definition
The signature move lands and the table feels nothing — no cascade, no snap, no reveal.

## Relations
- mitigated-by:: [[rule-patterns/catch-up-mechanism]]

## Notes

- [yt:QHHg99hwQGY] medium: Odyssey's threshold rewarded discarding your whole hand for a payoff, but players avoided it — discarding useful cards felt bad even when it was the stronger play; interesting isn't fun. (GDC Festival of Gaming 2016)
- [yt:F_1YcCcBVfY] medium: Pit Crew's original design (docking a player's own score for mistakes) felt bad to family/kid players even though it was mechanically equivalent to boosting opponents' scores instead. (GDC Festival of Gaming 2018)
