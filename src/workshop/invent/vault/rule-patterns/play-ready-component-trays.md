# Play-Ready Component Trays

## Definition
Store related pieces in removable trays that move directly from the box to the play area and remain there as supply, discard, or collection zones. This replaces repeated sorting and chasing with a few bulk tray movements, at the cost of extra insert volume, manufacturing expense, and fit testing.

## Relations

## Notes
- Design trays around the reset workflow, not merely the packed-box layout.
