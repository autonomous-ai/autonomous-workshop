# Mass Stability Tuning

## Definition
When gameplay depends on WHICH piece topples, control the centre of mass explicitly per silhouette: the tippy piece carries its mass high (COM above ~60% of height - thin shank, heavy head), the stable piece low (COM below ~45% - solid skirted base, hollow above). State the COM band in the part contract and verify it from the mesh, because wall/infill defaults flatten the difference the design depends on.
