# Spool Budget

## Definition
Keep the whole kit inside one 1kg spool and a weekend of printing: measured kits run 381-1112g and 40-97h, and everything over ~800g/70h is dominated by a few large flat plates. When a design brief needs big terrain, budget it in the component contract (thin walls, open coffers, shared rails) instead of discovering a 97-hour kit at slicing time.
