# Private Information Boundaries

## Definition
Give each player decision-relevant information that other players cannot inspect or fully request. This prevents one person from calculating the entire game state, but adds enforcement and communication overhead and can frustrate groups that prefer unrestricted planning.

## Relations

## Notes
- Private information works best when its restriction is justified by the theme and central to play.
