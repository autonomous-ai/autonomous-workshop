# Incentive-Aligned Core Play

## Definition
Make the actions that express the game's intended appeal efficient routes toward victory, then weaken rewards for strategies that bypass them. This may require retuning costs, rewards, risks, or the win condition and can reduce strategically valid but off-theme alternatives.
