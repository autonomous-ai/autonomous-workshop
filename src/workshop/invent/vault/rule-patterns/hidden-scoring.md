# Hidden Scoring

## Definition
Some or all scoring stays concealed until the end (face-down objectives, screened points). Nobody can compute the exact leader, so leader-bashing and kingmaking lose their target.

## Relations

## Notes
Costs table drama at the finish if EVERYTHING is hidden; hide the tiebreaker margin, not the whole race.
