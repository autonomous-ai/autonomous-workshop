# Milestone-Gated Content

## Definition
New rules, pieces, or narrative reveals are sealed away and released only when players hit a specific, thematically justified milestone (a second win, a fourth loss), instead of being available from the start. This staggers complexity and turns rules updates into anticipated reveals rather than an upfront rules dump.
