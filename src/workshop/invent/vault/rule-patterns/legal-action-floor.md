# Legal Action Floor

## Definition
Ensure every active seat always has at least one consequential legal action, even when ordinary options are exhausted or blocked. This may require a fallback move, resource conversion, reset, or escape action, which can weaken spatial constraints and must be balanced against intentional pass states.

## Relations

## Notes
- Test the fallback in terminal board states, not only during ordinary play.
