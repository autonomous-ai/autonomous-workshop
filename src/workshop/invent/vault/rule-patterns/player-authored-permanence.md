# Player-Authored Permanence

## Definition
Require every irreversible campaign change to follow a deliberate, informed player decision rather than a random result alone. Random events may create the dilemma or determine available options, but players choose whether, where, or how the permanent consequence is applied; this adds decision overhead and may reduce surprise or narrative volatility.

## Notes
- Show the lasting consequence before commitment whenever hidden information is not essential.
