# Degenerate-Strategy Counterpressure

## Definition
Attach escalating costs, diminishing payoffs, or exploitable vulnerabilities to safe strategies that avoid the game's central interaction. Counterpressure preserves the strategy as a situational choice while preventing it from becoming the default; its cost is additional balance complexity and the risk of making restraint artificially unattractive.
