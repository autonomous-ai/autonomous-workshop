# Forced Engagement

## Definition
A clause that makes ignoring other players illegal or costly: mandatory trades, must-attack-if-able, shared spaces that must be contested. Converts parallel solitaire into an actual multiplayer game.

## Relations

## Notes
The heavier the interaction tax, the more the game needs conflict to feel fair; pair with clear retaliation rules.
- [yt:QHHg99hwQGY] medium: Playtesters wouldn't attack with a prized creature, so its ability was changed to force attacking each turn — bluntly forcing an action taught players its value faster than hinting at it. (GDC Festival of Gaming 2016)
