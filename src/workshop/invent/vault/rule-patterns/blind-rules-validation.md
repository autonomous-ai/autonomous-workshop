# Blind Rules Validation

## Definition
Have testers learn and play solely from the written materials while recording every conflicting interpretation. Reconcile each conflict in the canonical rules and repeat after revisions; this costs additional test cycles and requires testers who have not learned the designer's intended ruling.

## Relations

## Notes
- Maintain a decision log so corrected contradictions do not return during later edits.
