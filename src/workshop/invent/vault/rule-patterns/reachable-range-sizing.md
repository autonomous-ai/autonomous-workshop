# Reachable Range Sizing

## Definition
Set a track’s endpoints from the minimum and maximum states permitted by the complete rules, including setup, player count, bonuses, and campaign duration. Confirm the bounds through calculation and adversarial playtesting, then trim unused spaces or revise the progression economy; this costs analysis time and may require component re-layout.

## Notes
- Leave only a small, intentional buffer for rare but legal outcomes.
