# Simultaneous Reveal

## Definition
All players commit a choice secretly, then reveal together. As a *clause* (not a whole game structure) it kills both downtime and last-mover advantage inside a single decision point, e.g. bids or votes.

## Relations
- variant-of:: [[mechanisms/simultaneous-action-selection]]

## Notes
Use for isolated decision points inside an otherwise sequential game; making the WHOLE turn simultaneous is the mechanism, with its own risks.
