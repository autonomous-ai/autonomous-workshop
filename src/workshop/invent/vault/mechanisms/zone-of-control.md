# Zone of Control

## Definition
A zone of control is an area of influence (typically the hexes or squares adjacent to a unit) that impedes enemy movement passing through it. Depending on the variant, units either must stop upon entering, pay increased costs, or cannot transit between two ZOC spaces. Tension arises from the interplay between fast units seeking to bypass defenders and the defensive value of positional presence—getting there first and locking down space gives you defensive leverage without active combat.

## Notes
Feels unintuitive in turn-based systems where inactive units somehow prevent enemy passage; works more naturally in real-time games where ZOC reflects formation constraints.
Weak units blocking powerful ones (peasant stopping a dragon) creates logical friction unless justified by the game's fiction.
