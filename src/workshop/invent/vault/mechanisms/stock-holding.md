# Stock Holding

## Definition
Stock Holding is a mechanism where players buy, sell, and retain fractional shares in shared in-game assets—companies, commodities, or nations. Share values fluctuate based on player decisions and game progress. The tension arises from balancing when to buy (investing for growth), sell (cashing out), or hold (betting on future value), while competing against other players' investment choices and facing uncertainty about market movements.

## Notes
Enables both passive income generation (dividends) and active control (majority holder privileges), creating distinct strategic paths.
Success depends on reading opponent intent and market timing—requires players to stay engaged with shared game state rather than isolated calculations.
