# Deck Construction

## Definition
Deck construction is a mechanism where players start with a minimal deck of weak cards and gradually build it by purchasing additional cards from a shared market during play. The core tension stems from the conflict between acquiring powerful cards for immediate advantage versus maintaining deck efficiency—acquired cards are shuffled into your hand pool, so every new card increases either your power or the dilution risk. Skilled players manage deck composition to create card synergies while avoiding bloat.

## Notes
Requires a shared card market or acquisition pool; without it, players cannot construct.
Negative feedback loops (e.g., victory points diluting your draw like in Dominion) are critical to prevent early-acquirer snowballing.
