# Victory Points as a Resource

## Definition
Players spend victory points as a currency to activate choices or abilities during gameplay, creating strategic tension between preserving points for the final score and converting them into immediate game advantages. The mechanic blurs the line between scoring and resource management—a player who spends VPs for tactical gain bets they'll recoup more through those gains than through conservation alone.

## Notes
Naming shapes player intent: 'coins' signals spending; 'points' signals accumulation. Smallworld's 'Victory Coin' dual-naming explicitly authorizes spend.
Tension is prevention of runaway leaders (traditional VP non-convertibility) versus encouraging economic depth (allow spends). Most VP-as-resource systems leak slightly to enable gameplay.
