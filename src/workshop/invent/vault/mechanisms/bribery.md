# Bribery

## Definition
Bribery allows players to spend currency to persuade other players to take specific actions or decisions that benefit the briber. The mechanism's tension emerges from mismatched valuations: the briber must offer enough to overcome reluctance without wasteful overpayment, while the recipient weighs the immediate gain against reputation damage or long-term disadvantage. When bribe amounts are hidden (as in Sheriff of Nottingham), negotiation becomes central; when public, it becomes calculable.

## Notes
Sheriff of Nottingham uses hidden simultaneous bids to prevent bribery from becoming pure arithmetic.
Bribery amplifies kingmaking in multiplayer games—a losing player can bribe a second-place rival to attack the leader.
