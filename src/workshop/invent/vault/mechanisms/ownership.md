# Ownership

## Definition
Ownership is a mechanism where players acquire control of specific game assets (spaces, properties, or resources) and earn benefits either through their own use or when opponents use those assets. The core tension emerges from deciding which assets to acquire, balancing direct personal benefit against passive income generated by opponents' forced use of your owned entities, and managing the risk that strong early acquisitions might compound into dominance.

## Notes
Ownership creates indirect player interactions where opponents' forced use of your assets generates passive income streams—the balance between personal and passive benefits determines how much agency players retain.
The value asymmetry between owned and unowned entities is critical; if owned assets are too strong, early acquisition decisions lock in the game outcome, but if too weak, ownership becomes a non-choice.
