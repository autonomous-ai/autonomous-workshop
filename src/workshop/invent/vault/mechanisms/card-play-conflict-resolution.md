# Card Play Conflict Resolution

## Definition
Players play one or more cards from hand to resolve conflicts, with each card providing a value, power icon, special ability, or modifier that shapes the outcome. Tension emerges from the hidden-information aspect of simultaneous blind card plays and the resource scarcity of cards in hand—you must weigh whether to spend a powerful card now or preserve it for a future conflict.

- risks:: [[anti-patterns/analysis-paralysis]], [[anti-patterns/decided-early]], [[anti-patterns/first-player-advantage]], [[anti-patterns/runaway-leader]]

## Notes
Simultaneous blind card play (as in Star Wars edge battles) dramatically reduces analysis-paralysis vs. open sequential play; hand size limits are critical.
Card complexity (power icons, costs, special abilities) can make the mechanism feel high-stakes but risks information-overload and teach-time.
