# Resource to Move

## Definition
Players expend a limited resource pool—such as fuel, money, or action points—to move their pieces across the board, making movement itself a strategic cost rather than a free or fixed action. This creates tension by forcing players to weigh whether a particular move is worth the resource expenditure, and prevents players from freely repositioning whenever they wish.

## Notes
Pacing breaks if resources are too abundant (mechanic becomes cosmetic) or too scarce (players become risk-averse and immobilized).
Works best when competing resource demands create genuine trade-offs—spending on movement must genuinely compete with other valuable uses.
