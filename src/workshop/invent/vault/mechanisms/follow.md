# Follow

## Definition
One player selects and executes an action, then other players may perform that same action or a modified version of it in sequence. Tension emerges from the lead player's choice constraining or enabling the options available to followers, and from followers deciding whether to copy, adapt, or pass on the offered action.

## Notes
Follow inverts typical agency: the lead player shapes the turn while followers react to that shape.
Risk of degenerate cascades where followers always copy the lead action, flattening decision space.
