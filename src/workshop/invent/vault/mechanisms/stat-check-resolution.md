# Stat Check Resolution

## Definition
Stat Check Resolution is a system where players roll dice and add a numerical modifier based on their character's attribute score, then compare the total against a predetermined difficulty number to determine success or failure. Tension emerges from the interplay between randomness (the dice roll) and determinism (the stat modifier)—a high-stat character has better expected outcomes but never guarantees success, while a low-stat character faces progressively worse odds as difficulty increases.

## Notes
Success probability is entirely predictable once stats and difficulty are known; most uncertainty is rolled away early in a campaign.
Works best when difficulty numbers are hidden or when stat differences between players are intentionally balanced.
