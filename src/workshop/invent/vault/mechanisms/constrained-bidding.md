# Constrained Bidding

## Definition
Constrained bidding is an auction system where players bid using a fixed, shared or personal resource pool that depletes with use—typically irreversible card bids or limited tokens. Tension emerges from competing pulls: when to spend valuable resources on contested items versus conserving them for future opportunities, combined with uncertainty about what opponents will bid and when they'll exhaust their capacity.

## Notes
The constraint is what separates this from open-pool auctions—depletion creates an economic pressure curve that sharpens endgame decisions.
Common in games where bids are cards drawn from hand (Ra) or tokens that cannot be recovered once placed.
