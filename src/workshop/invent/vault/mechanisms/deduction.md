# Deduction

## Definition
Deduction is the process of discovering hidden information through systematic logical reasoning and elimination of possibilities. Players gather clues by asking questions or observing events, then narrow down candidates until only the correct answer remains. Tension emerges from incomplete information—the race to deduce correctly before opponents, combined with the risk of misinterpreting clues or reaching false conclusions.

## Notes
Social deduction variants shift the core mechanism from pure logic toward bluffing and player psychology.
Tension can collapse early if deductions become mathematically inevitable before all players have equal opportunity to solve.
