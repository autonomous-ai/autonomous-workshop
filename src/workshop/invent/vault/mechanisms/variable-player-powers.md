# Variable Player Powers

## Definition
Each player controls a faction or role with its own distinct abilities, starting resources, or rule exceptions instead of everyone sharing one identical toolkit, so the game effectively runs several different rulesets in parallel under one shared board and win condition. The tension is twofold: designers must keep wildly different ability sets roughly equal in strength across countless matchup combinations, and players must plan around opponents' powers they don't control and may not fully understand, especially before they've played every side themselves.

## Notes
A common patch for a power that's too strong is making it fire only once per game rather than repeatedly.
Asymmetry alone doesn't guarantee replayability or depth; a power needs multiple viable lines of play, not just flavor, to pay off.
