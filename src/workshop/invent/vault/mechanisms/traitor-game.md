# Traitor Game

## Definition
A traitor game assigns one or more players a secret adversarial role while the rest work cooperatively. The traitors secretly sabotage the group's efforts while loyalists must deduce and eliminate traitors before losing parity or failing their collective objective. Tension emerges from asymmetric information: loyalists hunt based on behavioral tells and accusations, while traitors balance winning with avoiding detection.

## Notes
Strongest appeal is social paranoia and deduction theater—falls flat with disengaged groups or when roles resolve too early.
Downtime for eliminated players is a persistent design hazard unless eliminated players retain voting/information power.
