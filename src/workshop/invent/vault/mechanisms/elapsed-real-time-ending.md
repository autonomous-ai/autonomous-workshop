# Elapsed Real Time Ending

## Definition
The game concludes when a preset amount of real elapsed time passes, typically enforced by a timer or soundtrack. This creates constant time pressure that forces players toward faster, more intuitive decision-making rather than analysis. Tension derives from the shrinking window of opportunity and the knowledge that time will arbitrate the game's ending regardless of current game state.

## Notes
Effective only when time pressure is a primary design focus; as a secondary constraint it frustrates deliberate players more than it engages.
Works best in simultaneous or highly interactive games where downtime and extended analysis aren't core to the experience.
