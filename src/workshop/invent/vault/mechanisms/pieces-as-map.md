# Pieces as Map

## Definition
Pieces themselves form the game map or spatial playing area while retaining mechanical agency as pieces. Unlike tile-placement where tiles become inert board elements, pieces-as-map pieces continue to influence gameplay after placement, creating a dynamic board where the structure of play also constrains future moves and interactions.

## Notes
The continued agency of pieces after placement (pieces remain active game elements, not passive map) distinguishes this from pure tile-placement.
Map geometry directly constrains and rewards piece placement decisions in subsequent turns.
