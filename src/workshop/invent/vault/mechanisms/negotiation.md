# Negotiation

## Definition
Negotiation permits players to make verbal agreements, trades, and deals with each other during competitive play. Players propose exchanges of resources, control, or promises—which may or may not be binding—to achieve mutually beneficial outcomes. The tension stems from balancing personal advantage against the need to offer terms attractive enough for partners to accept, coupled with the risk that negotiation partners may breach agreements or form coalitions against you.

## Notes
Social capital and player reputation become game rules in practice; enforcement depends on group norms, not mechanics.
Unstructured negotiation magnifies player-skill variance; dominant personalities can dictate outcomes independent of strategic position.
