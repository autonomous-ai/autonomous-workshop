# Narrative Choice / Paragraph

## Definition
Players encounter narrative situations presented through text (typically numbered paragraphs or cards) and select from multiple response options. Each choice branches to a different narrative outcome, which directly affects the player's game state or resources. The mechanism creates tension between meaningful decision-making and uncertainty—players must commit to choices without knowing full consequences, and the narrative unfolds conditionally based on accumulated choices.

## Notes
Requires pre-authored branching narrative paths; outcomes are static once written, not procedurally generated.
Players' sense of agency and story ownership depends heavily on choices feeling meaningful rather than illusory (puzzle-like).
