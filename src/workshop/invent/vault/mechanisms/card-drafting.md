# Card Drafting

## Definition
Card drafting has players repeatedly take one card from a shared pool or a hand that gets passed around the table, leaving the rest for others to pick over next. Because a card taken is also a card denied, tension comes from weighing what you personally need against what would help a rival if left behind, all while adapting your plan to a pool you never fully control. Closed pick-and-pass drafts (hand shrinks each round) and open-pool drafts (cards refill from a market) produce slightly different flavors of this same push-pull.

## Notes
Pick-and-pass formats standardly alternate passing direction each round specifically to cancel out first-pick advantage.
Hate-drafting (taking a card to deny it rather than to use it) is strongest at 2 players and risks reading as griefing as player count rises.
