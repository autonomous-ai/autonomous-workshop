# Betting and Bluffing

## Definition
Players with incomplete information wager resources or make strategic claims to misrepresent their position's strength, creating psychological tension between deception and detection. Tension arises from the cost of wrong reads: believing a false claim wastes resources, while incorrectly doubting a true claim lets opponents gain unopposed. The mechanism hinges on players observing behavior patterns, calculating odds, and managing both reputation for honesty and the value of individual bets.

## Notes
Effectiveness scales with player count (2–4 optimal; larger tables dilute opponent-reading signal).
Susceptible to play-style dominance: aggressive bluffers can exploit cautious tables and vice versa.
