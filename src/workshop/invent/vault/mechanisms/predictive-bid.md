# Predictive Bid

## Definition
Players commit to a numerical prediction of their performance before a round or phase begins, then score points based on how accurately their actual result matches the prediction. Tension arises from the risk-reward calculation: ambitious predictions offer high rewards but risk failure, while conservative predictions ensure safe achievement but yield minimal points.

## Relations
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/analysis-paralysis]], [[anti-patterns/decided-early]], [[anti-patterns/silent-calc]], [[anti-patterns/luck-swing-endgame]]

## Notes
Prediction locked before play creates strategic depth through self-assessment vs. self-sabotage tension.
Outcome variance must be sufficient for predictions to meaningfully differentiate; trivial predictability eliminates the mechanism's purpose.
