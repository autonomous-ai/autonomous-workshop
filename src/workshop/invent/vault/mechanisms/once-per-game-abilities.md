# Once-Per-Game Abilities

## Definition
Once-per-game abilities are special powers that a player may activate only one time during the entire game. The mechanism creates tension through decision scarcity: players must choose *when* to deploy each power, balancing immediate benefit against future opportunities. This timing pressure is the core tension—deploy too early and waste the ability's potential; save it and risk never needing it.

## Notes
Trigger timing is critical—'on your turn' abilities are easier to game optimally than 'when condition occurs' reactive powers.
Success depends entirely on the ability's power level matching the information players have when deciding to use it; mismatched visibility causes either trap-option or alpha-solve failure modes.
