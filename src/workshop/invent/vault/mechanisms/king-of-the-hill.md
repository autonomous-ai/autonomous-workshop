# King of the Hill

## Definition
King of the Hill is a mechanic where players compete to occupy and maintain control of a designated position (the 'hill') on the board, earning points or advantages each turn they hold it. Tension emerges from the duality of reward and exposure: holding the hill makes you the target for other players who contest the position, creating a push-pull dynamic where leadership brings both victory points and danger.

## Notes
The mechanic's tension depends on whether players can realistically displace the current holder; if displacement is too easy, the hill becomes fluid noise; if too hard, it becomes a kingmaking target.
Victory condition design matters: awarding points per turn held incentivizes turtling, while rewarding only the final holder encourages late-game surges and reduces leader pressure.
