# Chaining

## Definition
Chaining is a mechanism where players build connected sequences of pieces or trigger cascading actions that flow from one to another. Tension emerges from planning actions strategically to maximize your own chain reactions while anticipating and blocking opponents' chains, creating a puzzle of interconnected consequences where each placement or action ripples through the board state.

## Relations
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/analysis-paralysis]], [[anti-patterns/runaway-leader]], [[anti-patterns/silent-calc]], [[anti-patterns/first-player-advantage]]

## Notes
Strong correlation with runaway-leader problem when early chains compound—players who start successful chains gain snowball advantages through feedback loops.
Open board state and perfect information intensify calculation burden; hidden or limited visibility of chain consequences can reduce analysis paralysis.
