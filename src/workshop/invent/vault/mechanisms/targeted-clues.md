# Targeted Clues

## Definition
One player gives clues strategically intended to help some players reach the target while hindering others. The tension emerges as the clue-giver must simultaneously communicate helpfully to allies and deceptively to opponents, managing the risk that a clue meant to mislead becomes accidentally useful, or one meant to help confuses instead.

## Notes
Strongest in asymmetrical social play where table dynamics and reading player intent matter more than pure logic.
Risk compounds with player count: managing clues for 2 audiences is tactical, for 4+ becomes cognitively heavy.
