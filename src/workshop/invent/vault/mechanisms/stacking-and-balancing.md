# Stacking and Balancing

## Definition
Players physically place and stack game pieces into a progressively unstable structure, risking collapse with each addition. Tension emerges from the escalating risk-reward calculation: play conservatively and forfeit positioning advantage, or place another piece knowing it might cause catastrophic toppling.
