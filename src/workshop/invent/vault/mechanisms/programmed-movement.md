# Programmed Movement

## Definition
Players secretly program a sequence of movements in advance, typically using cards or tokens placed face-down, then simultaneously reveal and execute them in order. Tension arises from spatial prediction—players must anticipate opponents' paths without seeing them, creating collision scenarios, failed plans, and chaotic emergent interactions when programs conflict or change.

## Notes
Mechanism heavily favors players with strong spatial reasoning and mental rotation ability.
The reveal-and-execute phase creates emergent chaos when multiple programmed paths intersect or collide unpredictably.
