# Kill Steal

## Definition
A mechanic where multiple players contribute resources or actions toward completing a shared objective, but only the player delivering the final contribution receives a bonus reward or exclusive benefit. The tension arises from players continuously deciding whether to push progress forward (risking an opponent completes it) or hold back (risking no one benefits and the pool resets).

## Notes
Kill Steal inverts a video-game pain point into legitimate tabletop tension through asymmetric reward structure.
Player psychology heavily influences viability—groups with poor communication or high competitiveness often see it as punishing rather than engaging.
