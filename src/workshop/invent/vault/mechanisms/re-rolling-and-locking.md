# Re-rolling and Locking

## Definition
Players roll dice and selectively lock certain results in place while re-rolling the unlocked dice, balancing the desire for better combinations against the risk of ending up with worse results. Tension arises from the fundamental push-your-luck decision: accept current dice values or gamble for improvements and potentially lose what you've secured.

## Notes
Locking restricts re-rolls rather than enabling them, differentiating this from simple multi-roll mechanics.
Re-roll limits (typically 2-3) are critical to prevent game slowdown; unlimited re-rolls collapse into pure optimization math.
