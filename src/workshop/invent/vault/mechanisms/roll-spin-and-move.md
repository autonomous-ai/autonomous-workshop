# Roll / Spin and Move

## Definition
Players roll dice or spin a spinner and move their token forward by that number of spaces on a track or board. Tension emerges from the uncertainty of landing on different spaces with varying consequences (good squares, bad squares, hazards), creating moments of suspense as the piece travels and adjudicates its final position.

- component:: [[components/pointer-spinner]]
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/idle-player]], [[anti-patterns/luck-swing-endgame]], [[anti-patterns/missing-info]], [[anti-patterns/runaway-leader]]

## Notes
Core flaw is lack of player agency when no meaningful choice follows the roll; mitigation requires optional choices (which piece moves, direction, or move-up-to rather than exact movement).
Works best when board spaces have varied, desirable or undesirable consequences to create repeated moments of small suspense.
