# Paper-and-Pencil

## Definition
A mechanism where players directly mark, draw, or write on paper as the core gameplay action—not merely for scorekeeping, but as integral to board state and decision-making. Moves or decisions are committed through writing (typically without erasing), creating a permanent record. Tension comes from the irreversibility of written choices and the information asymmetry before simultaneous reveals.

Works well in deduction and pattern-recognition games where written marks form the evidence
