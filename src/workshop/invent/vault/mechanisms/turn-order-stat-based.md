# Turn Order: Stat-Based

## Definition
A mechanism where player turn order is determined by comparing a tracked statistic (speed, initiative value, position on a speed track, or character attribute) rather than being fixed or random. Tension arises from the decision of whether to spend resources improving stats to secure favorable turn order, or pursue other strategic goals—and how early stat choices compound through the game.

## Notes
Works best when stat differences remain tightly clustered; wide gaps trivialize turn-order significance.
Mitigated by making stat improvements rare/expensive or obscuring stat values from simultaneous-calculation.
