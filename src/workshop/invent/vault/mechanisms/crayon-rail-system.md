# Crayon Rail System

## Definition
Players draw railroad tracks on an erasable board using crayons or markers to build networks connecting cities. Trains transport commodities along the drawn routes to fulfill demand cards and earn cash. Tension arises from the permanence of drawn routes constraining future expansion, limited starting capital, and the need to balance network coverage with profitable goods delivery.
