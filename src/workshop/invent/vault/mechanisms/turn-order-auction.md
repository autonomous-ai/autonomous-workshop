# Turn Order: Auction

## Definition
Players bid resources to determine their position in the turn order for an upcoming round. The auction creates strategic tension between acquiring a favorable turn position (which may grant first pick of actions, tiles, or workers) and conserving resources for other game decisions. The mechanism transforms turn priority into a tradeable good, forcing players to assess how much positional advantage is worth spending.

## Notes
Most effective in games where turn order position grants meaningful advantage (worker placement, tile selection, territory control).
Sealed-bid variants add uncertainty; open bidding allows strategic reading of player intentions.
