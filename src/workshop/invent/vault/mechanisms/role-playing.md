# Role Playing

## Definition
Role-playing mechanics invite players to inhabit character personas with distinct motivations, attributes, or behavioral constraints, making decisions through the character's lens rather than pure optimization. The tension emerges from balancing narrative self-expression against mechanical win conditions—a player's character concept may clash with optimal strategy, and the quality of immersion depends on whether the rules enforce or merely suggest character behavior.

## Notes
Effectiveness scales with group social contract—guidelines without enforcement work only if players commit to character consistency.
Character progression systems (stat growth, ability unlocks) often amplify engagement but risk creating mechanical overhead that overshadows narrative play.
