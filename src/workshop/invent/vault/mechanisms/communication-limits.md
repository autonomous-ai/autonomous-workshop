# Communication Limits

## Definition
Communication Limits restrict what information players can openly share or how they communicate with each other during play. The tension arises from forcing players to convey intentions through constrained channels—coded language, implicit cues, or hidden knowledge—making coordination itself a strategic puzzle rather than a solved problem dictated by one informed player.

## Notes
Effective in cooperative games to prevent experience-gap domination; rotating leadership or role changes amplify the effect.
Tension depends on calibration—too loose defeats the purpose, too strict creates frustration and meta-gaming between sessions.
