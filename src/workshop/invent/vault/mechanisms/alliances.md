# Alliances

## Definition
Alliances are formalized player partnerships governed by explicit rules, where allied players share victory conditions, cannot attack each other, and often grant each other special powers or benefits for a defined duration. The mechanism's tension arises from the mutual commitment to an ally while knowing that partnerships can shift—players must navigate between the safety of numbers and the risk of being betrayed or locked into an unfavorable pairing.

## Notes
Unlike freeform negotiation, alliances are mechanically enforced—no handshake agreements, only rule-bound partnerships with explicit win/loss conditions.
Weakens runaway-leader pressure by allowing trailing players to form counter-alliances, but only if formation rules permit; poorly scoped alliances entrench whoever allied first.
