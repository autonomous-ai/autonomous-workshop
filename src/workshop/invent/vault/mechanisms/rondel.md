# Rondel

## Definition
A circular wheel with distinct action spaces that players advance their token around on their turn, moving forward a limited distance for free or paying a cost to advance further. Tension arises from forced advancement—you can't repeat actions and must balance the cost of reaching desired options against your available resources, creating a constant push-your-luck question about whether to settle for the next free space or invest in reaching something better.

## Notes
Invented by Mac Gerdts in Antike (2005); mitigates analysis paralysis by constraining choices.
Cost-to-advance system can feel punitive if upcoming spaces offer no value for your position.
