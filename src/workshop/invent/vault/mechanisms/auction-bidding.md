# Auction/Bidding

## Definition
Players compete for a limited set of items, roles, or turn-order positions by publicly or secretly offering value (usually money or resources), with the item going to whoever offers the most. Tension comes from each player privately valuing the item differently while facing a shared, shrinking budget: bidding too aggressively on an early item starves later purchases, while passing risks a rival gaining a powerful asset cheaply. The competitive pressure to outbid others without knowing their true valuation or remaining resources drives much of the psychological push-pull.

## Notes
Spite bidding (driving up a price purely to hurt a rival with no intent to win) is a classic kingmaking vector in open-outcry formats.
Players eliminated from an auction early, or short on cash, can disengage until the next lot comes up, producing idle stretches.
