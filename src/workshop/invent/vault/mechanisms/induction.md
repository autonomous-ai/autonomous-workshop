# Induction

## Definition
Players deduce hidden rules or patterns by proposing examples and receiving binary feedback (match/no-match) from a game master who knows the true rule. Tension emerges from the asymmetry of knowledge—the GM must be consistent and fair, while players balance exploration against the risk of wasting guesses on plausible-but-wrong hypotheses. Success requires collaborative reasoning under imperfect information.

## Notes
GM-dependent fairness: inconsistent rule application or an under-specified rule breaks the mechanism entirely.
Fundamentally asymmetric—designed for cooperative discovery rather than competitive play.
