# Line Drawing

## Definition
Players draw lines connecting points or creating paths on a grid to build routes or networks, with each stroke committed and often irreversible. Tension emerges from balancing the drive to expand coverage against the risk of leaving routes incomplete when new demands or rolls shift the priority, forcing real-time commitment under incomplete information about future constraints.

## Notes
Irreversibility of drawn lines (even on erasable boards) creates psychological weight and commitment.
Works well paired with dice-driven or card-driven constraints that arrive mid-game to disrupt pre-planned networks.
