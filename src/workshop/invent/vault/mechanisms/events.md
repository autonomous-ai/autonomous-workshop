# Events

## Definition
Events introduce a shared, external deck or table that surfaces a new condition, hazard, or rule modifier each round, applying to all players simultaneously rather than being triggered by player choice. Some implementations reveal the upcoming event (or a short queue of them) in advance, turning the randomness into a planning problem; others keep it hidden until it resolves, making it a pure surprise. The tension comes from balancing your own turn-to-turn plans against a stream of state changes you cannot prevent and only partially foresee.

## Notes
Revealing the next 1-2 events in advance (Orleans, Pandemic-style) converts raw randomness into a foreseeable planning puzzle instead of a pure swing.
Each event card is often a bespoke one-off rule, so large event decks tend to accumulate rules-overhead and lose novelty after repeat plays.
