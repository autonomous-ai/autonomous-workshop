# Auction: Once Around

## Definition
Players bid on an item exactly once each as the auction passes sequentially around the table; each player decides to bid or pass with no second chances. The tension arises from irreversible decisions made under incomplete information—you must commit to a bid value knowing what earlier players offered but not what later players will do, forcing genuine strategic commitment rather than continuous negotiation.

## Notes
Elegant mechanism because one-per-player constraint eliminates continuous rebidding sluggishness while preserving tension through irreversible commitment.
Works best when item valuations are asymmetric or subjective so players cannot easily calculate a 'correct' bid from public info alone.
