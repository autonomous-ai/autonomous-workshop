# Action Drafting

## Definition
Players take turns selecting actions from a limited, shared pool where each chosen action becomes unavailable to others. Tension arises from the dual pressure of pursuing your strategy while denying opponents the actions they need—you must decide quickly or risk losing crucial options to faster players, yet hasty choices can harm your long-term position.

## Notes
Simultaneous selection variants (all players choose at once) significantly reduce analysis paralysis and turn-order downtime.
Action reset/refresh mechanics are critical—without them, the game can become lopsided once good actions are exhausted.
