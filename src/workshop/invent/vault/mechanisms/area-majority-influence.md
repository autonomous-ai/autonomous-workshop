# Area Majority / Influence

## Definition
Players commit pieces, cubes, or influence markers into shared regions of a map, and each region's payout is split according to who holds the most presence there rather than who exclusively owns it. The tension comes from having a limited pool of pieces to spread across many contested regions, so every placement is a bet on which fights are worth entering, when to pile on to lock down a lead, and when to abandon a region because catching up would cost more than it's worth. Because a single extra piece can flip who scores a region, players are constantly re-evaluating standings across the whole board rather than in one isolated duel.

## Notes
Multiple scoring rounds are the most common structural fix for runaway-leader in this mechanism.
Three-plus player counts are prone to a weakened-combatants pattern where a player who avoided a regional fight scores it instead (seat-advantage/kingmaking).
