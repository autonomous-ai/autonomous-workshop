# Interrupts

## Definition
Interrupts allow players to respond to or negate opponent actions during their turn, creating reactive counterplay windows that break sequential turn structure. The core tension arises from balancing player investment in planned sequences against meaningful interactive responses. They typically resolve through either hand-based cards with stack resolution, pre-placed face-down traps, or resource-gated interruption systems where frequent reactions exhaust action pools.

## Notes
Stack resolution (Last-In-First-Out) from Magic prevents interrupt chains from deadlocking by resolving most recent responses first.
Cost-based interruption models in wargames create resource trade-offs—each reaction reduces total actions available, separating strategic opportunity from tactical execution.
