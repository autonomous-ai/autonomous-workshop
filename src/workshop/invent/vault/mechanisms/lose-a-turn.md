# Lose a Turn

## Definition
Lose a Turn forces a player to skip their next opportunity to take an action, creating a forced-idle period where they cannot make meaningful decisions. The tension comes from uncertainty (will I trigger this penalty?) and the unavoidable downtime once triggered, making it a harsh, uncontrollable consequence that removes player agency for a full cycle.

## Notes
Creates friction not through interesting constraint but through erasure of choice—stronger penalty mechanics give players ways to mitigate or recover.
Works best in light, social games where downtime is acceptable; fails in longer games or tight competition where one skipped turn swings outcome.
