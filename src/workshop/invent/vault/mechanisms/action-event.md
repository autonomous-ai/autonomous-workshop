# Action/Event

## Definition
Players choose between spending resource points to take an action or triggering an event effect that reshapes game state. The tension emerges from the cost-benefit calculation: taking the action advances your position directly, but triggering the event can disrupt opponent plans—though you forfeit personal progress and may not control the event's outcome.

## Notes
Event design matters more than mechanism—identical or heavily negative events frustrate players; transparency on upcoming events helps planning.
Fairness often requires event effects to impact all players equally or reward the player taking the risk, otherwise it punishes whoever's already losing.
