# Pattern Recognition

## Definition
Players identify visual, logical, or numerical patterns within the game state to complete objectives or outpace opponents. Tension arises from the race to spot patterns first, the cognitive challenge of deducing hidden rules through play, or the risk of analysis-paralysis as players attempt perfect pattern recognition under time pressure.

## Notes
Pattern recognition strength heavily depends on player spatial/visual aptitude, creating skill-based disparity.
Simultaneous pattern-racing (Jungle Speed style) mitigates analysis-paralysis better than sequential deduction.
