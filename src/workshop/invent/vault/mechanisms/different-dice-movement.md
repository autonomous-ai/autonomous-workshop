# Different Dice Movement

## Definition
Different Dice Movement uses distinct dice pools or die types to determine how various units, factions, or game elements move across the board. The mechanism creates asymmetric movement capabilities—where a unit's speed and range depend on which die (or dice) it rolls—generating tension through both unpredictability and the differentiation between actors. Players face uncertainty about whether they'll catch pursuers or escape threats, while different unit types develop distinct tactical identities through their movement profiles.

## Notes
Tension rises from asymmetry: identical movement rules bore players, but wildly different dice pools can feel unfair or opaque.
Works best when die selection is visible or rule-discoverable; hidden die assignments frustrate players who can't predict consequences.
