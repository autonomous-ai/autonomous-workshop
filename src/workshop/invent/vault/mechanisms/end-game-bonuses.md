# End Game Bonuses

## Definition
End Game Bonuses award points at the conclusion of play for conditions accumulated or achieved over the course of the game — completed patterns, majorities held, resources banked, sets finished — rather than scoring them as they happen. The tension comes from players investing turns in positions whose payoff is deferred and only partially visible, so they must gauge how much of their engine to bank on a final tally they can't fully verify until the reveal, while also weighing that investment against moves with immediate, certain value.

## Notes
Designers deliberately lean on this mechanism as a counterweight to runaway-leader dynamics, letting a trailing player's hidden investment pay off at the buzzer.
When end bonuses are large relative to in-game scoring, the finish can read as anticlimactic since the visible mid-game leader isn't necessarily the winner.
