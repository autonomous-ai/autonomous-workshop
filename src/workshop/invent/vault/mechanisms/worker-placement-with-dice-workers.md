# Worker Placement with Dice Workers

## Definition
Dice replace traditional identical workers; their pip values determine both which locations a player can access (many spaces require specific values or value ranges) and the strength of the worker placed there. The mechanism creates tension between the randomness of dice rolls—which force players to work with what they rolled—and the strategic planning that defines worker placement, requiring players to maintain flexible strategies and fallback options rather than committing to single paths.

## Notes
Mitigation techniques (reroll options, value manipulation tokens, conversion mechanics) restore agency but require careful balance to preserve drama.
Sequential placement heightens tension but risks kingmaker dynamics; simultaneous placement reduces angst.
