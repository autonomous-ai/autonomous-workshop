# Catch the Leader

## Definition
A catch-up mechanism that systematically penalizes players who are ahead in the race while rewarding trailing players, making it progressively harder for leaders to extend their advantage. The tension emerges from the dynamic between leaders attempting to maintain position against scaled resistance and trailing players exploiting their granted advantages to close the gap, creating the perception that anyone can still win despite the point differential.

## Relations
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/runaway-leader]], [[anti-patterns/kingmaking]], [[anti-patterns/analysis-paralysis]], [[anti-patterns/idle-player]]

## Notes
Experienced players can often strategically play around the mechanic or choose not to engage with it, reducing its intended balancing effect.
Effectiveness depends heavily on tuning — too aggressive and it swings momentum unfairly; too weak and leaders still run away despite the mechanism.
