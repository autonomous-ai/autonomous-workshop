# Die Icon Resolution

## Definition
Players roll custom dice bearing icons or symbols rather than numbers, with success or specific outcomes determined by matching the rolled symbols to target requirements. The core tension arises from the unpredictability of symbol results—you may fail to roll the icons needed for your intended action, forcing risk-taking decisions about whether to invest more dice, stop, or accept failure. This creates push-your-luck dynamics and makes each roll consequential.

## Notes
Requires custom-printed or custom dice with thematic icons, limiting manufacturability compared to standard numeric dice.
Icon pools can be thematically designed to reinforce game narrative—symbols directly represent actions or outcomes rather than being abstracted through number interpretation.
