# Slide/Push

## Definition
Players push or slide pieces into positions, forcing adjacent pieces to shift and cascade across the board—a forced reorganization of space. You cannot place pieces arbitrarily; instead, each placement triggers chain-reaction movement that compresses or reorders the grid. Tension emerges from this constraint: the same move that reaches your target also displaces pieces you may want to protect, forcing hard positional trade-offs.
