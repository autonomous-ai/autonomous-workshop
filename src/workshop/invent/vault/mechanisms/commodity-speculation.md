# Commodity Speculation

## Definition
Players buy and sell commodities whose prices fluctuate during the game, creating tension between holding for future gains and cashing out at current valuations. The mechanism rewards prediction of market trends and punishes poor timing, making player fortunes swing with their ability to anticipate value changes.

## Notes
Tension peaks when price changes are opaque or happen via player action (collusion variant) rather than deterministic tables.
Games vary on player control: investment variants limit it (prices move semi-independently), collusion variants let players directly manipulate prices.
