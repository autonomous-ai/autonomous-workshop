# Trick-taking

## Definition
Players play one card per round into a shared pile, with the highest card of the led suit (or highest trump) winning the trick. The mechanism creates tension through hand management—deciding which cards to sacrifice now versus preserving for critical moments—combined with hidden information about what other players hold. Winners of tricks earn points or avoid penalties depending on game goals, and play order is tightly constrained by the follow-suit rule, forcing difficult choices when you lack the required suit.
