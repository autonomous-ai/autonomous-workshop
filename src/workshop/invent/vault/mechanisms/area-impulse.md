# Area-Impulse

## Definition
Players subdivide each turn into alternating impulses where one area of units acts collectively before being marked spent, then the other player activates an area. This repeats until both pass or a random event ends the impulse sequence. The tension arises from timing decisions: which area to activate this impulse creates cascading consequences for future activations, forcing players to weigh immediate tactical advantage against setting up stronger follow-up moves.

## Notes
Area-impulse's cognitive load (thinking differently about force groups than traditional I-go-you-go) is its primary design challenge; wargame stakes amplify decision weight and deliberation time.
The mechanic's depth comes from sequencing decisions rather than individual unit moves, making it popular in complex wargames despite playing overhead.
