# Static Capture

## Definition
Static Capture is a direct removal mechanism where an opponent's piece is eliminated when another piece occupies or moves through its space. The tension derives from perfect information about capture zones—players can precisely calculate which advances are dangerous and must balance territorial ambition against material loss.

## Notes
Deterministic capture resolves instantly with no luck component, making positions feel inevitable rather than dramatic.
Best used in abstract/chess-like games; less impactful in games where piece positions are hidden or constantly shift.
