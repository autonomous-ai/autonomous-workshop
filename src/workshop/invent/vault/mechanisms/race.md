# Race

## Definition
A race mechanism puts players in direct competition to reach a defined finish line or goal state first, with the winner being whoever crosses it. The tension stems from visible progress toward a shared objective combined with unpredictability—usually introduced through dice rolls, random draws, or situational timing—that prevents the outcome from being entirely determined by early advantage, keeping multiple players viable until late in the game.

## Notes
Tension naturally intensifies as players approach the finish line, requiring little design scaffolding for climax pacing.
Risk of runaway leader is high if catch-up mechanics are absent; most successful race games include turn-order or resource advantages for trailing players.
