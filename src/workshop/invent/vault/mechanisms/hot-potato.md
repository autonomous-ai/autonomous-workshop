# Hot Potato

## Definition
A player-elimination or endgame penalty mechanism where players continuously pass a negatively-valued object to avoid possession when a trigger condition arrives (timer expires, music stops, round ends). Tension stems from forced participation in an active passing flow under time pressure, creating social urgency and reflexive decision-making rather than strategic calculation.

## Notes
Relies on real-time play synchronization and social pressure, not strategic depth—outcomes primarily driven by reflexes and coordination.
Early player elimination can create observer boredom; asymmetric roles (trappers vs. passers) add social complexity that pure hot potato lacks.
