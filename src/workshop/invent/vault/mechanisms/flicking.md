# Flicking

## Definition
Flicking is a dexterity mechanism where players physically propel game pieces by striking them with a finger flick, using thumb-and-pointer technique to control force and direction. Tension arises from the gap between intended aim/force and actual execution—a player's skill determines precision, but surface friction, hand tremor, and collateral contact with other pieces introduce uncertainty into each action's outcome.

## Notes
Dexterity dominates outcomes more than most mechanics—physical skill ceiling can overshadow strategy if not balanced with goal granularity or positional reset between turns.
Collateral effects (unintended piece contact) can create emergent moments but risk turning strategic control into chaos if not spatially constrained.
