# Hidden Roles

## Definition
Hidden Roles assigns each player a secret identity or allegiance at game start, creating asymmetric win conditions where one coalition works covertly against others. Players use observation, deduction, and bluffing to identify allies and enemies, generating tension through uncertainty about trustworthiness. The mechanic thrives on incomplete information and social dynamics—players must infer hidden identities through behavior and voting patterns while managing their own credibility.

## Notes
Heavily dependent on player count and group dynamics—minimum viable player thresholds vary by role distribution.
Elimination variants often underperform because sitting-out players retain information that distorts remaining play; modern designs favor low-elimination or elimination-free voting systems.
