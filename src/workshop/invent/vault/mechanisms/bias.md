# Bias

## Definition
A directional environmental force that makes movement easier or mandatory in certain directions, simulating phenomena like wind, gravity, or currents. Players must navigate around or leverage this directional constraint, creating strategic tension between following the bias for efficiency versus resisting it to reach desired destinations.

## Notes
Excellent for thematic grounding—the bias becomes the board's natural law rather than arbitrary rules
Can create degenerate strategies where players only move with the bias, flattening decision space unless the win condition actively punishes this
