# Layering

## Definition
Layering is a mechanic where players manipulate overlapping transparent or semi-transparent pieces with different patterns and colors on each side, positioning and rotating them to match target configurations shown on cards or goals. The tension arises from spatial puzzle-solving—players must visualize how colors and shapes combine when layers overlap, then execute their solution faster than opponents or more accurately than others to score points.
