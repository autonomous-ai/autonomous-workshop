# Hidden Movement

## Definition
Hidden Movement hides one player's position while others attempt to deduce and track it through incomplete information and breadcrumbs. The hidden player records moves privately while seekers have perfect information only about themselves and clues left behind. Tension emerges from asymmetric knowledge: the hidden player experiences paranoia knowing they're being hunted (and watching seekers reason incorrectly), while seekers engage in cooperative deduction from scattered clues. Success depends on careful calibration—too many clues destroy mystery, too few cause frustration—and ensuring the hidden player retains meaningful agency and competitive tools beyond mere evasion.

## Notes
Requires retroactive path verification; complex recordkeeping directly causes credibility collapse.
Works best when hidden player has offensive or defensive capabilities; pure one-way pursuit becomes one-sided and disengaging for both sides.
