# Physical Removal

## Definition
Players remove pieces or elements from a physical structure during gameplay, with the core tension arising from the risk that removal could cause cascading failures, partial collapse, or complete structural failure. The mechanic provides immediate, visceral feedback—players can see and feel consequences as the structure destabilizes, creating psychological pressure around each removal decision.

## Notes
Physical removal derives tension from embodied risk rather than information asymmetry—the threat is visible and tangible.
Success often depends on dexterity and nerve as much as strategy, which can overshadow decision-making depth.
