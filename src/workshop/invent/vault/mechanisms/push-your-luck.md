# Push Your Luck

## Definition
Push Your Luck is a decision-making mechanism where players balance securing existing gains against the temptation to risk them for greater rewards through continued action (rolling dice, drawing cards, spinning, etc.). The core tension emerges from the fact that there is no safe optimal choice—players must consciously own their decision to either bank progress or gamble it away, creating emotional investment despite random outcomes.

## Notes
Paradoxically reduces analysis paralysis compared to complex games; simpler decision trees and transparent randomness make optimal play tractable.
Particularly effective in cooperative play where shared risk creates collective anguish and emotional engagement beyond what pure luck allows.
