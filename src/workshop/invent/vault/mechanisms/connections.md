# Connections

## Definition
Players build a continuous path or network of their pieces on a grid, typically aiming to connect two board edges or complete a closed loop. Tension arises from the dual imperative: advancing your own connection while strategically blocking opponent routes, creating a dynamic where defensive positioning becomes as critical as offensive building.

## Notes
Simple rules mask emergent tactical complexity; perfect-play solutions often exist for finite boards.
The offense-defense duality makes placement order and positioning critical—small mistakes in path geometry can cascade.
