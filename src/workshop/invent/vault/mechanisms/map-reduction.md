# Map Reduction

## Definition
Map Reduction removes playable board areas during the game, shrinking the space available for player actions and movement. This creates escalating tension through spatial constraint, forces direct player interaction as options collapse, and provides a visual timer that heightens urgency as players compete for control of the remaining zones.

## Notes
Visual representation of shrinkage (tile thickness in Survive, card turns in Forbidden Island) transforms arbitrary removal into thematic inevitability.
The sequence of which zones vanish and their rate matters more than the total area lost—early beaches vs. late mountains change entire strategy curves.
