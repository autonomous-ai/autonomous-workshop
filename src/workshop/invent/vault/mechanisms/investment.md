# Investment

## Definition
Players commit resources to acquire a stake (shares, ownership, or future revenue) in a game entity, whose value compounds or fluctuates based on subsequent game events. Tension arises from the timing dilemma—invest early for potential high returns but at uncertain cost, or wait for more information but risk missing opportunities—and from the cascading advantage problem where early investments seed runaway positions.

## Notes
Investment mechanisms naturally pull toward information-poor early decisions and information-rich late decisions; mitigation requires explicit catch-up structures or artificial payoff ceilings.
Works best with hidden or stochastic value revelation to prevent perfect-information optimization.
