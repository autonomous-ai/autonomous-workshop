# Real-Time

## Definition
Real-time eliminates structured turns and forces all players to act simultaneously under time pressure, typically using a timer or continuous clock. Players must make quick decisions and execute actions concurrently rather than waiting for sequential turns. The tension arises from the relentless passage of time, uncertainty about what opponents are doing, and the constant need to act before the clock runs out.

## Notes
Real-time mechanics can paradoxically reduce analysis paralysis by forcing faster decisions under time constraints.
Success depends on simple, quick-to-resolve actions; complex rules under pressure create confusion.
