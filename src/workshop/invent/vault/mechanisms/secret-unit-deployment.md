# Secret Unit Deployment

## Definition
Players make strategic decisions and deployments without knowing the exact positioning or composition of opponents' forces, which are kept hidden until revealed during play. Tension emerges from the uncertainty of not knowing what you face, requiring players to infer opponent intentions from behavior and past patterns while managing the fog of war.

## Notes
Commonly used in wargames to simulate fog of war and creates strong asymmetric information states.
Enables bluffing and deception strategies but requires player trust not to peek at hidden information.
