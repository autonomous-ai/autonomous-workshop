# Player Elimination

## Definition
A player is removed entirely from the game when they fail to meet victory conditions or are defeated by other players, forcing them to wait idle until the game concludes. The mechanism creates tension through the possibility of total exclusion from play, but generates frustration when eliminated players face extended downtime with no agency while others continue—particularly problematic in games with unpredictable lengths where an early mistake can mean sitting out for hours.

## Relations
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/idle-player]], [[anti-patterns/decided-early]], [[anti-patterns/spiral]], [[anti-patterns/luck-swing-endgame]]

## Notes
Viable only in quick games (≤20 min) where eliminated players' wait time stays minimal; longer games create taboo social friction.
Character elimination (respawning as a new player role) preserves participation while keeping elimination stakes—alternative to full player elimination.
