# Auction: Dexterity

## Definition
An auction where players compete to win items or actions through both bidding and physical dexterity performance. Tension arises from the dual commitment required: allocating resources through a bid while simultaneously executing a physical skill challenge (flicking, stacking, manipulating components) under time or competitive pressure. Success requires both economic judgment and manual coordination.

## Notes
Physical proximity and table positioning can create unintended player advantages unrelated to strategy or skill.
Works best when dexterity determines only who wins each auction, not total game outcome.
