# Pick-up and Deliver

## Definition
Players acquire goods or items from one location, transport them across the board, and deliver them to destination locations for rewards. Tension arises from route optimization—finding the most efficient path matters because arriving even one turn late can cost you the contract—and from competing with other players to reach pickup locations and deliveries first.

## Notes
Timing precision is the dominant strategic axis—one-turn delays can flip victory.
Works best with hidden or asymmetric delivery information to maintain tension.
