# Roles with Asymmetric Information

## Definition
Players are secretly assigned roles with different win conditions and receive unequal information about the game state. The mechanism creates tension by forcing players to deduce hidden roles through observation, discussion, and inference while managing the gap between private knowledge and public information. Success depends more on meta-game skills—reading behavior, bluffing, and probabilistic reasoning—than on hidden-perfect-information strategy.

## Notes
Communication becomes the primary tool despite information scarcity; players must establish trust patterns to coordinate.
Balance hinges on making role deduction difficult enough to sustain tension but not so opaque that players feel lost.
