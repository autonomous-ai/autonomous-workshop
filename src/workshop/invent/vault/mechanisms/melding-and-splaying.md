# Melding and Splaying

## Definition
Players form melds—groups of cards in specific relationships—and arrange them by splaying (overlapping) cards to strategically reveal or hide icons and abilities. A single physical arrangement creates different game states depending on which cards are visible, allowing previously-covered cards to become relevant again through splay actions that expose new icons and effects.

## Notes
The mechanism's strength depends on card text referencing splay directions and hidden icons—without meaningful payoff for revealing covered cards, splaying becomes ornamental rather than strategic.
Works best when splay cost or benefit is asymmetric: easy to hide cards, harder to reveal them (or vice versa).
