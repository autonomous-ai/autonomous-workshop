# Auction: English

## Definition
Players openly bid in ascending increments around a single item, each choosing to raise the current bid or pass (permanently exiting that auction). The last remaining bidder wins and pays their final bid amount. Tension arises from inferring competitors' true valuations from their bid escalation patterns, deciding when to push higher versus concede, and managing cash reserves since winning expensive auctions early can leave you depleted for later rounds.

## Notes
Simple rules but psychologically dense—reading opponent resolve and managing self-commitment is the real game.
Natural price-discovery mechanism; participants reveal information gradually, allowing mid-auction strategy pivots.
