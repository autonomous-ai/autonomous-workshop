# Finale Ending

## Definition
A game ends with a separate climactic phase—often a mini-game or timed challenge—that determines the final victor independently of the lead position or accumulated progress. The tension stems from unknowability: players cannot predict exactly when or how the game will conclude, creating potential for position swings and comebacks during the finale phase.

## Notes
Telegraphing the finale phase reduces its surprise value and allows players to game optimal strategies; ambiguity is the mechanism's primary tool.
Finale endings work best when the finale phase's win conditions are structurally different from main-game victory conditions, forcing players to shift tactics abruptly.
