# Legacy Game

## Definition
A campaign structure where each play session permanently alters the game's components, rules, and narrative state through irreversible changes—stickers applied, cards destroyed, sealed envelopes opened, rules rewritten. The mechanism generates tension by making player decisions consequential: mistakes cannot be undone, and the game your group plays becomes a unique physical artifact shaped by your collective choices across sessions.

## Notes
Pioneered by Rob Daviau with Pandemic Legacy; irreversibility is the core design lever for emotional weight.
Physical modifications (stickers, card destruction) anchor permanence more viscerally than rule-text changes alone.
