# Map Addition

## Definition
Players expand the playable area by progressively adding map tiles or revealing board sections as they explore. Tension arises from deciding which areas to discover first, balancing immediate benefits of known territory against the potential of unexplored regions, and managing how early exploration decisions constrain the final map layout.

- component:: [[components/interlocking-board-tile]]

## Notes
Placement decisions when adding tiles can cascade—poor early additions may trap players with awkward map geometry later.
Hidden vs. public tile drafting significantly affects strategic depth; drawing unknown tiles creates exploration feel but reduces player agency.
