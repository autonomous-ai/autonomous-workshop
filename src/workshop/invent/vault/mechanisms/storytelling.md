# Storytelling

## Definition
A mechanism where players collaboratively construct or discover a narrative arc through game choices, card prompts, or system-generated events. Tension arises from the core conflict between narrative progression (which must keep moving forward) and gameplay challenge (which resists advancement), forcing designers to either subordinate challenge to story flow or embed narrative deeply into mechanical systems.

## Notes
Success requires narrative to emerge from mechanical systems (e.g., Pandemic's epidemic cards creating story) rather than mechanical story bolted on top (theme without mechanical integration)
Replayability challenge: most narrative-first designs have predetermined plot trees, limiting meaningful variation across plays
