# Acting

## Definition
Acting is a mechanic where players use performance, mimicry, or verbal/physical roleplay to convey hidden information or deceive other players, who must interpret and respond to these performances. Tension comes from the uncertainty of whether a performance is truthful or deceptive, the social pressure of performing under scrutiny, and the challenge of reading others' authenticity.

## Notes
Highly skill-dependent on performer ability and reading ability—player experience disparity can dominate outcomes more than strategic decisions.
Works best with communication-limits rules to prevent excessive explanation; pure roleplay with no other verification creates ambiguity about whether deception succeeded.
