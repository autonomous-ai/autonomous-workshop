# Random Production

## Definition
Random production is a resource-generation system where players receive goods, materials, or currency determined by random events—dice rolls, card draws, or chance outcomes—rather than by their strategic choices or fixed rules. Tension emerges from the inability to guarantee what resources you'll have access to, forcing players to adapt their plans to potentially unfavorable random outcomes or to risk everything on volatile, unpredictable supply chains.

## Notes
Input randomness (random production options presented, player chooses) mitigates player frustration better than pure output randomness (roll dice, take whatever you get).
Pairing random production with information asymmetry or player agency (mitigation abilities) makes the mechanic more acceptable to experienced players.
