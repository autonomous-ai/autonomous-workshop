# Force Commitment

## Definition
Force Commitment is a mechanism where players irrevocably allocate resources, forces, or workers to different outcomes, categories, or battlefields before those outcomes resolve or are fully revealed. The tension derives from irreversibility—committed resources may be permanently lost or wasted regardless of result—forcing players to calculate risk against potential reward without complete information.

## Relations

- variant-of:: [[mechanisms/simultaneous-action-selection]]

## Notes
Often paired with area-majority or multi-front allocation systems (e.g., Dune's spice bidding + combat allocation).
The irreversibility is essential; without consequence for overcommitting or backing away, the mechanism loses its decision weight.
