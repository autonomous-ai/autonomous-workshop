# Highest-Lowest Scoring

## Definition
Players collect resources or claim areas across multiple categories, but their final score is determined by their lowest count rather than their highest. This inverts normal scoring incentives: instead of specializing in one domain, players must balance presence across all categories to avoid a single weak point bottlenecking their total. The tension emerges from managing opportunity costs—investing heavily in a strong category pulls resources from weaker areas, risking a devastating score if that weak area is overlooked.

## Notes
Forces players to value breadth over depth, creating a 'don't specialize' incentive that can feel artificial in some thematic contexts.
Knizia scoring (named after designer Reiner Knizia) is the canonical implementation; Tigris & Euphrates is the foundational example.
