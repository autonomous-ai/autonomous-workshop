# Income

## Definition
Income is a recurring, usually automatic grant of resources or currency each round based on what a player already owns or has built (buildings, population, production tiles, held bonds), rather than something earned via an action that turn. Because income scales with prior investment, spending early to grow your income base compounds over subsequent rounds, so the core tension is short-term spending power versus long-term compounding return, and when to convert accumulated income into victory points versus reinvesting it further.

## Notes
Income compounds like interest, so it is a classic positive-feedback engine unless the designer caps growth or tapers late-game returns (e.g. diminishing per-unit payouts).
Because early investment has more rounds to compound, income systems tend to reward decisions made in the opening turns disproportionately over later ones.
