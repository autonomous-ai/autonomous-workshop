# Singing

## Definition
Players recall and perform song lyrics in response to prompts or competing to be first to sing a song containing a specified word. Tension arises from the social performance aspect combined with the cognitive challenge of rapid song recall and knowledge commonality among players.

## Relations
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/analysis-paralysis]], [[anti-patterns/decided-early]], [[anti-patterns/idle-player]], [[anti-patterns/silent-calc]]

## Notes
Performance-based mechanics heavily dependent on player comfort with singing publicly; social friction can dominate game outcomes over mechanical skill.
Shared cultural knowledge of songs creates asymmetry—generational or genre preferences may make some players systematically unable to compete regardless of mechanic design.
