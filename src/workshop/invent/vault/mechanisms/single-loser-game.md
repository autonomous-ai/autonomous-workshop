# Single Loser Game

## Definition
A mechanism where the game concludes when exactly one player fails to meet a defined condition, making all other players simultaneous winners. Tension comes from players trying to avoid being the person who breaks the rules or makes the triggering mistake—each turn is a narrow escape.

## Relations
- conflicts-with:: [[mechanisms/cooperative-game]]
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/analysis-paralysis]], [[anti-patterns/decided-early]], [[anti-patterns/idle-player]], [[anti-patterns/kingmaking]]

## Notes
Engagement cliff: once a player appears certain to fail, others disengage from real strategy and coast to victory.
Works best with 3+ players where tension sustains through multiple rounds; two-player versions flatten the shared-victory advantage.
