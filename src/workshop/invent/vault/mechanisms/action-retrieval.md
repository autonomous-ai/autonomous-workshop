# Action Retrieval

## Definition
Action Retrieval allows players to reuse previously spent actions—typically cards, tokens, or board positions—by dedicating time or an action slot to retrieve them. The tension arises from the opportunity cost: spending your turn retrieving old options means forgoing new actions, forcing players to weigh whether replaying a key action is worth delaying other plans. This creates multi-turn strategic depth through combo potential and careful timing.

## Notes
Tracking retrieval history can cause mental load; works best when histories are short or visible.
Common in worker placement, deckbuilders, and euros; balance requires capping how much retrieval amplifies early-game gains.
