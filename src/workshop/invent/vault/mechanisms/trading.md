# Trading

## Definition
Trading is a player-to-player exchange mechanism where one player offers resources, cards, or game elements to another in negotiated deals. The tension arises from uncertainty about fair value, the fear that an opponent will benefit more from the exchange than intended, and the risk that refusing to trade leaves you at a disadvantage while accepting it may strengthen a rival.

## Notes
Forced trading (where players must participate) creates stronger game tension than optional trading, which can be entirely avoided.
Games without hidden information about resource scarcity tend to reduce trading to simple arithmetic rather than genuine negotiation.
