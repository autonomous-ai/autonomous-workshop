# Bingo

## Definition
Players mark randomly-called numbers on cards, racing to complete a predetermined pattern before others. Tension derives from the shared anticipation of each number draw and genuine uncertainty of whether the next call will help one's card, combined with equal odds for all players creating suspended disbelief about the outcome.

## Relations
- risks:: [[anti-patterns/luck-swing-endgame]], [[anti-patterns/idle-player]], [[anti-patterns/multiplayer-solitaire]], [[anti-patterns/missing-info]]

## Notes
Outcome is functionally decided at card distribution; play is mechanical marking with zero player agency or decision-making.
No meaningful interaction between players—each participant passively watches the caller and marks their card independently.
