# Interlocking Board Tile

## Definition
A square, hexagonal, or irregular terrain plate joins neighboring plates with loose dovetails, clips, or separate quarter-turn locks. It builds maps that can expand, shrink, deform, or change elevation during play. Molded relief and large icons encode terrain without artwork or labels.
