# Hidden-Choice Selector

## Definition
A covered dial or sliding shutter lets a player secretly select one of several engraved symbols, revealing it only when the housing is opened or rotated. It replaces written orders and text-heavy selection cards for simultaneous choices, bids, roles, or combat stances. Physical stops make every legal selection tactile and unambiguous.
