# Detented Slider Track

## Definition
A tab or collar slides along a numbered or icon-marked rail to record a changing value or position. Shallow bumps, scallops, or a flexible tongue provide detents so accidental table knocks do not alter the state. Tracks can connect end-to-end when a game needs a longer range.
