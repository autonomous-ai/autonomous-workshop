# Windowed Rotary Dial

## Definition
A captive wheel exposes one coarse numeral or icon through a housing window. It records resources, health, prices, or victory points without loose markers, while a friction rim and axle keep the chosen value from drifting. Raised or recessed symbols are sized for a 0.4 mm nozzle and can be color-separated by swapping filament between layers.
