# Tile-Dispensing Magazine

## Definition
A covered vertical or sloped magazine stores thick tiles or chits and exposes exactly one at its outlet. Drawing from the outlet randomizes concealed pieces while preventing players from seeing or handling the remaining supply. Interchangeable spacers adapt the magazine to several standard token sizes.
