# Flicking Puck and Gate

## Definition
Low-profile pucks travel across the table through printed gates, ramps, walls, or scoring sockets after being flicked by a finger. Replaceable rims and textured undersides let designers tune rebound and friction for different games. Mechanical scoring zones eliminate the need for a printed target sheet.

## Notes
- print: Print pucks flat with concentric top layers; chamfer the lower rim, keep gates heavy or broad-based, and test PLA versus PETG sliding behavior.
