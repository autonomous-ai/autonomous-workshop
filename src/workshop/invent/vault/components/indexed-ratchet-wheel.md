# Indexed Ratchet Wheel

## Definition
A toothed wheel advances in discrete steps under a flexible pawl and resists backward motion. It can enforce escalating costs, countdowns, round progression, or one-way production growth while making state changes audible and tactile. Coarse teeth and a replaceable PETG pawl keep the mechanism suitable for ordinary FDM tolerances.
