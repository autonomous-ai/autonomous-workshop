# Socketed Component Tray

## Definition
A shallow tray organizes tiles, tokens, units, or resource pieces in shaped wells and can become part of the play surface. Removable dividers and keyed docking edges support markets, drafting rows, supply limits, and rapid setup. A snap lid or mating second tray keeps the same printed pieces contained between sessions.

## Notes
- print: Print flat without supports; allow 0.4-0.7 mm around stored pieces, fillet well bottoms, and use 0.25-0.35 mm for docking joints.
