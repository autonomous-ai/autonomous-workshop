# Hinged Privacy Screen

## Definition
Interlocking printed panels stand around a player's private supply, planned orders, or hidden deployment area. Print-in-place or pin hinges let the screen fold into a compact stack and can include molded trays in its base. Its opacity supports secrecy without paper shields.

## Notes
- print: Print separate panels flat for best surfaces; use 0.35-0.5 mm hinge clearance and avoid supports with 45-degree knuckles or inserted filament pins.
